## Activities
- **File Patent Application**: The 'File Patent Application' activity represents the submission of a patent application to the relevant intellectual property office.
- **Conduct Patent Search**: The 'Conduct Patent Search' activity involves performing a search to identify prior art or existing patents related to a patent application.
- **Examine Patent Application**: The 'Examine Patent Application' activity represents the formal review and examination of a filed patent application by an examiner.
- **Grant Patent**: The 'Grant Patent' activity represents the official approval and issuance of a patent to the applicant.
- **Reject Patent Application**: The 'Reject Patent Application' activity represents the formal refusal of a patent application by the examining authority.
- **File Trademark Application**: The 'File Trademark Application' activity represents the submission of a trademark application to the relevant intellectual property office.
- **Conduct Trademark Search**: The 'Conduct Trademark Search' activity involves performing a search to identify existing trademarks that may conflict with a trademark application.
- **Examine Trademark Application**: The 'Examine Trademark Application' activity represents the formal review and examination of a filed trademark application by an examiner.
- **Grant Trademark**: The 'Grant Trademark' activity represents the official approval and registration of a trademark to the applicant.
- **Reject Trademark Application**: The 'Reject Trademark Application' activity represents the formal refusal of a trademark application by the examining authority.
- **File Copyright Registration**: The 'File Copyright Registration' activity represents the submission of a copyright registration application to the relevant intellectual property office.
- **Examine Copyright Application**: The 'Examine Copyright Application' activity represents the formal review and examination of a filed copyright registration application by an examiner.
- **Grant Copyright**: The 'Grant Copyright' activity represents the official approval and registration of a copyright to the applicant.
- **Reject Copyright Application**: The 'Reject Copyright Application' activity represents the formal refusal of a copyright registration application by the examining authority.
- **Appeal Decision**: The 'Appeal Decision' activity represents the process of filing an appeal against a rejection decision for a patent, trademark, or copyright application.

## Attributes
- **Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000000' represents the unique identification number assigned to an intellectual property application, ranging from 1 to 1,000,000.
- **Application Fee: float between 50.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Application Fee: float between 50.0 and 10000.0' represents the monetary fee required to file an intellectual property application, ranging from 50.0 to 10,000.0.
- **Application Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Application Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected' specifies the possible states of an intellectual property application during its lifecycle: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
- **Search Result: Clear, Conflict**: The attribute 'Search Result: Clear, Conflict' specifies the possible outcomes of a patent or trademark search: Clear, indicating no conflicting prior art or trademarks found, or Conflict, indicating that a conflicting existing record was identified.
- **Decision: Granted, Rejected**: The attribute 'Decision: Granted, Rejected' specifies the possible final decisions made on an intellectual property application: Granted, indicating approval, or Rejected, indicating refusal.
