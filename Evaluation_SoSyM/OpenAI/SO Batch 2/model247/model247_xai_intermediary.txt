## Activities
- **File Patent Application**: The 'File Patent Application' activity represents the initial step of submitting a patent application to the patent office.
- **Conduct Patent Search**: The 'Conduct Patent Search' activity involves performing a thorough search to identify any existing patents or prior art that may conflict with the patent application.
- **Examine Patent Application**: The 'Examine Patent Application' activity includes the detailed review and examination of the submitted patent application by the patent examiner.
- **Grant Patent**: The 'Grant Patent' activity signifies the official approval and granting of the patent rights to the applicant.
- **Reject Patent Application**: The 'Reject Patent Application' activity represents the decision to deny the patent application due to various reasons such as lack of novelty or non-compliance.
- **File Trademark Application**: The 'File Trademark Application' activity represents the initial step of submitting a trademark application to the trademark office.
- **Conduct Trademark Search**: The 'Conduct Trademark Search' activity involves performing a thorough search to identify any existing trademarks that may conflict with the trademark application.
- **Examine Trademark Application**: The 'Examine Trademark Application' activity includes the detailed review and examination of the submitted trademark application by the examiner.
- **Grant Trademark**: The 'Grant Trademark' activity signifies the official approval and granting of the trademark rights to the applicant.
- **Reject Trademark Application**: The 'Reject Trademark Application' activity represents the decision to deny the trademark application due to various reasons such as similarity to existing marks.
- **File Copyright Registration**: The 'File Copyright Registration' activity represents the initial step of submitting a copyright registration application.
- **Examine Copyright Application**: The 'Examine Copyright Application' activity includes the review and examination of the submitted copyright application.
- **Grant Copyright**: The 'Grant Copyright' activity signifies the official approval and granting of the copyright registration.
- **Reject Copyright Application**: The 'Reject Copyright Application' activity represents the decision to deny the copyright registration application.
- **Appeal Decision**: The 'Appeal Decision' activity involves filing an appeal against a rejection decision for patent, trademark, or copyright applications.

## Attributes
- **Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each intellectual property application.
- **Application Fee: float between 50.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Application Fee: float between 50.0 and 10000.0' represents the monetary fee paid for filing the intellectual property application.
- **Application Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Application Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected' specifies the current status of the intellectual property application during the examination process.
- **Search Result: Clear, Conflict**: The attribute 'Search Result: Clear, Conflict' indicates the outcome of the search conducted for prior art or conflicting marks in patent or trademark applications.
- **Decision: Granted, Rejected**: The attribute 'Decision: Granted, Rejected' represents the final decision made on the intellectual property application after examination.
