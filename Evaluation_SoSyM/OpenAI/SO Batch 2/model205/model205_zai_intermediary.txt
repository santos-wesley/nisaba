## Activities
- **Submit Benefit Request**: The 'Submit Benefit Request' activity represents the action of an employee submitting a request for a specific benefit.
- **Review Benefit Request**: The 'Review Benefit Request' activity involves examining and assessing a submitted benefit request to determine its validity and eligibility.
- **Approve Benefit Request**: The 'Approve Benefit Request' activity represents the action of formally authorizing a benefit request that has been reviewed and deemed eligible.
- **Reject Benefit Request**: The 'Reject Benefit Request' activity represents the action of formally declining a benefit request that has been reviewed and deemed ineligible.
- **Notify Employee**: The 'Notify Employee' activity involves informing the employee about the outcome of their benefit request, whether it has been approved or rejected.
- **Process Payment**: The 'Process Payment' activity represents the execution of a financial transaction to disburse the approved benefit amount to the employee.
- **Update Employee Record**: The 'Update Employee Record' activity involves modifying the employee's records to reflect the approved benefit and its associated details.
- **Audit Benefit Process**: The 'Audit Benefit Process' activity represents the systematic review and verification of the benefit processing workflow to ensure compliance and accuracy.
- **Submit Appeal**: The 'Submit Appeal' activity represents the action of an employee formally submitting an appeal against a rejected benefit request.
- **Review Appeal**: The 'Review Appeal' activity involves examining and reassessing a submitted appeal to determine whether the original decision should be upheld or overturned.
- **Approve Appeal**: The 'Approve Appeal' activity represents the action of formally accepting an appeal, thereby overturning the original rejection of the benefit request.
- **Reject Appeal**: The 'Reject Appeal' activity represents the action of formally declining an appeal, thereby upholding the original rejection of the benefit request.
- **Notify Appeal Decision**: The 'Notify Appeal Decision' activity involves informing the employee about the outcome of their submitted appeal, whether it has been approved or rejected.
- **Conduct Benefit Orientation**: The 'Conduct Benefit Orientation' activity represents the organization and delivery of an orientation session to educate employees about available benefits and their utilization.
- **Evaluate Benefit Utilization**: The 'Evaluate Benefit Utilization' activity involves assessing and analyzing the extent to which approved benefits are being used by employees.
