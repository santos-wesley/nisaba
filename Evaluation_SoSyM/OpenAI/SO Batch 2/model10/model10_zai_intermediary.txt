## Activities
- **Prepare Sample**: The 'Prepare Sample' activity represents the initial preparation of a material sample for testing and analysis.
- **Conduct X-ray Diffraction**: The 'Conduct X-ray Diffraction' activity involves performing X-ray diffraction analysis to determine the crystal structure of the material sample.
- **Measure Hardness**: The 'Measure Hardness' activity entails measuring the hardness value of the material sample.
- **Analyze Microstructure**: The 'Analyze Microstructure' activity involves examining and analyzing the microstructure type of the material sample.
- **Perform Thermal Analysis**: The 'Perform Thermal Analysis' activity consists of conducting thermal analysis to determine the thermal conductivity of the material sample.
- **Conduct Tensile Test**: The 'Conduct Tensile Test' activity involves performing a tensile strength test on the material sample.
- **Evaluate Corrosion Resistance**: The 'Evaluate Corrosion Resistance' activity entails assessing the corrosion resistance level of the material sample.
- **Perform Chemical Analysis**: The 'Perform Chemical Analysis' activity involves analyzing the chemical purity of the material sample.
- **Document Results**: The 'Document Results' activity includes recording and documenting the results of all tests and analyses performed on the material sample.
- **Review Findings**: The 'Review Findings' activity involves reviewing the documented findings and results of the material testing process.
- **Approve Material**: The 'Approve Material' activity represents the formal approval of the material based on the reviewed test results.
- **Reject Material**: The 'Reject Material' activity represents the formal rejection of the material based on the reviewed test results.
- **Store Sample**: The 'Store Sample' activity involves storing the material sample after testing and analysis are completed.
- **Calibrate Equipment**: The 'Calibrate Equipment' activity entails calibrating the testing equipment to ensure accurate and reliable measurements.
- **Prepare Report**: The 'Prepare Report' activity consists of preparing a comprehensive report summarizing all findings and results from the material testing process.

## Attributes
- **Sample ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Sample ID: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each material sample, ranging from 1 to 1000.
- **Hardness Value: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Hardness Value: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the measured hardness value of the material sample, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Tensile Strength: float between 0.0 and 2000.0**: The attribute 'Tensile Strength: float between 0.0 and 2000.0' represents the tensile strength measured during the tensile test, ranging from 0.0 to 2000.0.
- **Corrosion Resistance: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Corrosion Resistance: High, Medium, Low' specifies the level of corrosion resistance of the material sample, which can be High, Medium, or Low.
- **Approval Status: Approved, Rejected, Pending**: The attribute 'Approval Status: Approved, Rejected, Pending' indicates the approval status of the material, which can be Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
- **Thermal Conductivity: float between 0.0 and 500.0**: The attribute 'Thermal Conductivity: float between 0.0 and 500.0' represents the thermal conductivity value of the material sample, ranging from 0.0 to 500.0.
- **Microstructure Type: Grain, Fiber, Amorphous**: The attribute 'Microstructure Type: Grain, Fiber, Amorphous' specifies the type of microstructure observed in the material sample, which can be Grain, Fiber, or Amorphous.
- **Chemical Purity: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Chemical Purity: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the chemical purity percentage of the material sample, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Equipment Status: Calibrated, Not Calibrated**: The attribute 'Equipment Status: Calibrated, Not Calibrated' indicates the calibration status of the testing equipment, which can be Calibrated or Not Calibrated.
