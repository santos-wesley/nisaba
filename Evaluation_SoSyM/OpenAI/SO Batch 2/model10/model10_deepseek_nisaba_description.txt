#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Prepare Sample**: The 'Prepare Sample' activity represents the preparation of a material sample for subsequent testing and analysis.
- **Conduct X-ray Diffraction**: The 'Conduct X-ray Diffraction' activity involves performing X-ray diffraction analysis to determine the crystalline structure of the material.
- **Measure Hardness**: The 'Measure Hardness' activity involves measuring the hardness of the material sample using appropriate testing methods.
- **Analyze Microstructure**: The 'Analyze Microstructure' activity involves examining and characterizing the microstructure of the material sample.
- **Perform Thermal Analysis**: The 'Perform Thermal Analysis' activity involves conducting thermal analysis to evaluate the thermal properties of the material.
- **Conduct Tensile Test**: The 'Conduct Tensile Test' activity involves performing a tensile test to measure the material's tensile strength.
- **Evaluate Corrosion Resistance**: The 'Evaluate Corrosion Resistance' activity involves assessing the material's ability to resist corrosion under specified conditions.
- **Perform Chemical Analysis**: The 'Perform Chemical Analysis' activity involves conducting chemical analysis to determine the chemical composition and purity of the material.
- **Document Results**: The 'Document Results' activity involves recording and documenting all test results and findings from the material analysis.
- **Review Findings**: The 'Review Findings' activity involves reviewing the documented results and findings to make informed decisions about the material.
- **Approve Material**: The 'Approve Material' activity represents the formal approval of the material based on satisfactory test results and review.
- **Reject Material**: The 'Reject Material' activity represents the formal rejection of the material due to unsatisfactory test results or findings.
- **Store Sample**: The 'Store Sample' activity involves storing the material sample for future reference or further testing.
- **Calibrate Equipment**: The 'Calibrate Equipment' activity involves calibrating testing equipment to ensure accurate and reliable measurements.
- **Prepare Report**: The 'Prepare Report' activity involves compiling and preparing a comprehensive report summarizing all tests, results, and conclusions.

## Attributes
- **Sample ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Sample ID: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each material sample for tracking purposes.
- **Hardness Value: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Hardness Value: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the measured hardness value of the material sample.
- **Tensile Strength: float between 0.0 and 2000.0**: The attribute 'Tensile Strength: float between 0.0 and 2000.0' represents the measured tensile strength of the material sample.
- **Corrosion Resistance: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Corrosion Resistance: High, Medium, Low' specifies the corrosion resistance rating of the material sample.
- **Approval Status: Approved, Rejected, Pending**: The attribute 'Approval Status: Approved, Rejected, Pending' indicates the current approval status of the material sample.
- **Thermal Conductivity: float between 0.0 and 500.0**: The attribute 'Thermal Conductivity: float between 0.0 and 500.0' represents the measured thermal conductivity of the material sample.
- **Microstructure Type: Grain, Fiber, Amorphous**: The attribute 'Microstructure Type: Grain, Fiber, Amorphous' specifies the type of microstructure observed in the material sample.
- **Chemical Purity: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Chemical Purity: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the chemical purity percentage of the material sample.
- **Equipment Status: Calibrated, Not Calibrated**: The attribute 'Equipment Status: Calibrated, Not Calibrated' indicates whether the testing equipment has been calibrated.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the material analysis process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Prepare Sample,' which involves preparing a material sample for subsequent testing and analysis, assigning it a unique 'Sample ID' for tracking purposes.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with either the activity 'Approve Material' or 'Reject Material,' which formally approves or rejects the material based on satisfactory or unsatisfactory test results and review. These activities must be the last to occur in the process.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Prepare Sample' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that a material sample is prepared and assigned a 'Sample ID' before any testing begins, adding depth and reliability to the process.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
1. The activity 'Review Findings' is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Results' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Sample ID' of the 'Document Results' activity equals the 'Sample ID' of the 'Review Findings' activity. This ensures that findings are reviewed only after results are documented, maintaining a logical sequence.
2. The activity 'Review Findings' is permitted to occur only if an 'Approve Material' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Sample ID' of the 'Approve Material' activity equals the 'Sample ID' of the 'Review Findings' activity. This ensures that findings are reviewed only after material approval, guiding the review process.
3. The activity 'Review Findings' is permitted to occur only if a 'Reject Material' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Sample ID' of the 'Reject Material' activity equals the 'Sample ID' of the 'Review Findings' activity. This ensures that findings are reviewed only after material rejection, maintaining process integrity.
4. The activity 'Calibrate Equipment' is permitted to occur only if a 'Conduct X-ray Diffraction' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Equipment Status' of the 'Conduct X-ray Diffraction' activity is 'Calibrated'. This ensures that equipment calibration follows X-ray diffraction analysis when the equipment is already calibrated.
5. The activity 'Calibrate Equipment' is permitted to occur only if a 'Measure Hardness' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Equipment Status' of the 'Measure Hardness' activity is 'Calibrated'. This ensures that equipment calibration follows hardness measurement when the equipment is already calibrated.
6. The activity 'Calibrate Equipment' is permitted to occur only if a 'Conduct Tensile Test' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Equipment Status' of the 'Conduct Tensile Test' activity is 'Calibrated'. This ensures that equipment calibration follows tensile testing when the equipment is already calibrated.
7. The activity 'Calibrate Equipment' is permitted to occur only if a 'Perform Thermal Analysis' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Equipment Status' of the 'Perform Thermal Analysis' activity is 'Calibrated'. This ensures that equipment calibration follows thermal analysis when the equipment is already calibrated.
8. The activity 'Calibrate Equipment' is permitted to occur only if an 'Evaluate Corrosion Resistance' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Equipment Status' of the 'Evaluate Corrosion Resistance' activity is 'Calibrated'. This ensures that equipment calibration follows corrosion resistance evaluation when the equipment is already calibrated.
9. The activity 'Document Results' is permitted to occur only if a 'Prepare Report' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Sample ID' of the 'Prepare Report' activity equals the 'Sample ID' of the 'Document Results' activity, and this must happen within 0 to 1 day. This ensures that results are documented only after a report is prepared, maintaining a timely sequence.

#### **Response Constraints**

This ensures a timely analysis of the microstructure following X-ray diffraction, maintaining the process's momentum.
