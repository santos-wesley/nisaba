## Activities
- **Prepare Sample**: The 'Prepare Sample' activity represents the preparation of a material sample for subsequent testing and analysis.
- **Conduct X-ray Diffraction**: The 'Conduct X-ray Diffraction' activity involves performing X-ray diffraction analysis on a sample to determine its structural properties.
- **Measure Hardness**: The 'Measure Hardness' activity includes measuring the hardness of a material sample.
- **Analyze Microstructure**: The 'Analyze Microstructure' activity involves examining the microstructure of a sample to identify its structural characteristics.
- **Perform Thermal Analysis**: The 'Perform Thermal Analysis' activity involves analyzing the thermal properties of a material sample.
- **Conduct Tensile Test**: The 'Conduct Tensile Test' activity involves testing a sample to determine its tensile strength.
- **Evaluate Corrosion Resistance**: The 'Evaluate Corrosion Resistance' activity involves assessing a sample's resistance to corrosion.
- **Perform Chemical Analysis**: The 'Perform Chemical Analysis' activity involves analyzing the chemical composition of a sample.
- **Document Results**: The 'Document Results' activity involves recording and documenting the results obtained from various tests and analyses.
- **Review Findings**: The 'Review Findings' activity involves reviewing the documented results and findings from the testing process.
- **Approve Material**: The 'Approve Material' activity represents the approval of a material based on the reviewed findings.
- **Reject Material**: The 'Reject Material' activity represents the rejection of a material based on the reviewed findings.
- **Store Sample**: The 'Store Sample' activity involves storing the material sample after testing and analysis.
- **Calibrate Equipment**: The 'Calibrate Equipment' activity involves calibrating the equipment used for testing and analysis to ensure accuracy.
- **Prepare Report**: The 'Prepare Report' activity involves compiling a report summarizing the results and findings of the testing process.

## Attributes
- **Sample ID: Integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Sample ID: Integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to a material sample.
- **Hardness Value: Float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Hardness Value: Float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the measured hardness value of a material sample.
- **Tensile Strength: Float between 0.0 and 2000.0**: The attribute 'Tensile Strength: Float between 0.0 and 2000.0' represents the measured tensile strength of a material sample.
- **Corrosion Resistance: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Corrosion Resistance: High, Medium, Low' specifies the level of corrosion resistance evaluated for a material sample.
- **Approval Status: Approved, Rejected, Pending**: The attribute 'Approval Status: Approved, Rejected, Pending' specifies the current approval state of a material sample.
- **Thermal Conductivity: Float between 0.0 and 500.0**: The attribute 'Thermal Conductivity: Float between 0.0 and 500.0' represents the measured thermal conductivity of a material sample.
- **Microstructure Type: Grain, Fiber, Amorphous**: The attribute 'Microstructure Type: Grain, Fiber, Amorphous' specifies the type of microstructure identified in a material sample.
- **Chemical Purity: Float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Chemical Purity: Float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the measured chemical purity level of a material sample.
- **Equipment Status: Calibrated, Not Calibrated**: The attribute 'Equipment Status: Calibrated, Not Calibrated' specifies whether the equipment used for testing and analysis is calibrated.
