**Natural Language Description:**

This Multi-Perspective Declare model describes a **material testing and certification workflow** for laboratory samples (e.g., metals, alloys, or composites). The process is centered around a **Sample ID** that links all related activities.

### Process Flow

The process **must begin** with **Prepare Sample**. After performing various material characterization tests, the process **ends** in one of two final states: either **Approve Material** or **Reject Material**.

### Core Testing Sequence

The laboratory must perform several analytical activities, with the following mandatory ordering and dependencies:

- **Conduct X-ray Diffraction** must be performed **before** **Analyze Microstructure** (with the same Sample ID). Up to 2 occurrences of each activity are allowed.
- **Measure Hardness** must be directly followed (chained) by **Conduct Tensile Test** on the same sample, with no other activities in between.
- **Perform Thermal Analysis** must be executed **before** **Evaluate Corrosion Resistance** on the same sample.
- **Document Results** must occur **before** **Review Findings**.
- **Review Findings** must occur **before** both **Approve Material** and **Reject Material**.

### Equipment and Quality Constraints

All testing equipment must be properly calibrated before use. Specifically:
- **Calibrate Equipment** (which sets the *Equipment Status* to "Calibrated") must occur **before** any of the following activities: Conduct X-ray Diffraction, Measure Hardness, Conduct Tensile Test, Perform Thermal Analysis, or Evaluate Corrosion Resistance.

### Data Perspectives

Each activity is associated with specific data attributes:

- **Prepare Sample** → captures *Sample ID* (integer 1–1000)
- **Measure Hardness** → records *Hardness Value* (float 0.0–100.0)
- **Conduct Tensile Test** → records *Tensile Strength* (float 0.0–2000.0)
- **Evaluate Corrosion Resistance** → assigns *Corrosion Resistance* level (High/Medium/Low)
- **Perform Thermal Analysis** → measures *Thermal Conductivity* (float 0.0–500.0)
- **Analyze Microstructure** → determines *Microstructure Type* (Grain/Fiber/Amorphous)
- **Conduct X-ray Diffraction** → measures *X-ray Intensity* (integer 0–10000)
- **Perform Chemical Analysis** → determines *Chemical Purity* (float 0.0–100.0)
- **Calibrate Equipment** → sets *Equipment Status* (Calibrated/Not Calibrated)
- **Approve Material** → sets *Approval Status* (Approved/Rejected/Pending)

### Additional Requirements

- **Prepare Report** must be completed **before** **Document Results**.
- **Perform Chemical Analysis** and **Store Sample** are available activities but are not strictly constrained by the declared rules.
- **Review Findings** serves as a quality gate before final approval or rejection.

The model enforces a structured, quality-controlled laboratory workflow where proper equipment calibration, logical test ordering, and complete documentation are mandatory before a material can be either approved or rejected.
