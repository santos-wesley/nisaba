**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Currency Exchange Process**

This model describes a **currency exchange office** workflow with strong control-flow, data, and temporal constraints.

### Process Overview

The process begins when a customer brings in foreign currency. After authenticity verification and rate calculation, the exchange is either approved or rejected. If approved, the exchange is processed, a receipt is issued, the customer is notified, and the transaction is recorded. The system also maintains up-to-date exchange rates.

### Activities
- **Receive Currency**
- **Verify Currency Authenticity**
- **Calculate Exchange Rate**
- **Approve Exchange**
- **Reject Exchange**
- **Process Exchange**
- **Issue Receipt**
- **Notify Customer**
- **Record Transaction**
- **Update Exchange Rates**

### Data Perspectives

**Data Objects & Domains:**
- **Amount**: integer between 1 and 10000
- **Currency Type**: {USD, EUR, GBP, JPY}
- **Exchange Rate**: float between 0.5 and 1.5
- **Exchange Status**: {Pending, Approved, Rejected}
- **Transaction ID**: integer between 1000 and 9999

**Data Bindings:**
- `Receive Currency` captures **Amount** and **Currency Type**
- `Verify Currency Authenticity` uses **Currency Type**
- `Calculate Exchange Rate` and `Update Exchange Rates` produce **Exchange Rate**
- `Approve Exchange` and `Reject Exchange` set **Exchange Status**
- `Process Exchange`, `Issue Receipt`, `Record Transaction` work with **Transaction ID**
- `Notify Customer` uses **Exchange Status**

### Control-Flow Constraints

**Mandatory Flow:**
- The process **must start** with `Receive Currency`
- The process **must end** with `Issue Receipt`
- `Calculate Exchange Rate` must occur **at least once**
- `Approve Exchange` can occur **at most once** (Exactly[1])

**Sequential Dependencies:**
- `Receive Currency` must precede `Verify Currency Authenticity`
- `Verify Currency Authenticity` must precede `Approve Exchange`
- `Calculate Exchange Rate` must precede `Process Exchange`
- `Approve Exchange` must be followed (eventually) by `Process Exchange` **if** the Exchange Status is "Approved"
- `Reject Exchange` must be followed by `Notify Customer` **if** the Exchange Status is "Rejected"
- `Process Exchange` must be followed by `Record Transaction` (with matching Transaction ID)

**Mutual Exclusions & Negative Constraints:**
- `Verify Currency Authenticity` and `Approve Exchange` **cannot both occur** in the same trace (Not Co-Existence)
- `Receive Currency` cannot be directly followed by `Reject Exchange` (Not Succession)

**Existence & Correlation:**
- If `Process Exchange` occurs, `Update Exchange Rates` must have occurred at some point (Responded Existence)
- `Process Exchange` and `Record Transaction` are tightly linked via matching **Transaction ID**

### Temporal & Metric Constraints

- **Chain Succession** between `Calculate Exchange Rate` and `Update Exchange Rates` with a **maximum delay of 30 minutes** (medium confidence)
- **Chain Succession** between `Receive Currency` and `Record Transaction` with a **maximum delay of 1 day**, requiring matching Transaction ID (high confidence)

### Summary

This is a **data-aware, temporally constrained** currency exchange process that enforces:
1. Strong sequencing (receive → verify → approve/reject → process)
2. Data-driven branching via `Exchange Status`
3. Tight correlation between process steps using `Transaction ID`
4. Real-time rate management (rates must be updated shortly after calculation)
5. Clear separation between verification and approval (they cannot both happen in one case)
6. Mandatory recording and receipt issuance for completed exchanges

The model ensures both regulatory compliance and operational efficiency in a currency exchange business.
