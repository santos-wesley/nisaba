#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Assess Initial Condition**: The 'Assess Initial Condition' activity represents the evaluation of a client's initial condition before treatment begins.
- **Develop Treatment Plan**: The 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity involves creating a personalized treatment plan for the client.
- **Schedule First Session**: The 'Schedule First Session' activity includes arranging the client's first therapy session.
- **Conduct Therapy Session**: The 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity represents carrying out a therapy session with the client.
- **Evaluate Progress**: The 'Evaluate Progress' activity involves assessing the client's progress during treatment.
- **Adjust Treatment Plan**: The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity involves modifying the treatment plan based on the client's progress.
- **Provide Feedback**: The 'Provide Feedback' activity includes giving feedback to the client regarding their treatment.
- **Schedule Follow-Up**: The 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity involves arranging a follow-up session for the client.
- **Complete Treatment**: The 'Complete Treatment' activity represents the conclusion of the client's treatment process.
- **Conduct Final Assessment**: The 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity involves performing a final evaluation of the client's condition after treatment.

## Attributes
- **Client ID: Integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Client ID: Integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number of the client.
- **Session Type: Initial, Follow-Up, Crisis**: The attribute 'Session Type: Initial, Follow-Up, Crisis' specifies the type of therapy session being conducted.
- **Progress Score: Float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Progress Score: Float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the numeric score indicating the client's progress in treatment.
- **Therapist ID: Integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Therapist ID: Integer between 1 and 9999' represents the unique identification number of the therapist.
- **Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative' specifies the nature of the feedback provided to the client.
- **Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed**: The attribute 'Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed' specifies the current status of the client's treatment plan.

## Binds
- **Assess Initial Condition: Client ID**: The bind 'Assess Initial Condition: Client ID' links the 'Assess Initial Condition' activity with the attribute 'Client ID'.
- **Develop Treatment Plan: Client ID, Therapist ID**: The bind 'Develop Treatment Plan: Client ID, Therapist ID' links the 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Therapist ID'.
- **Schedule First Session: Client ID**: The bind 'Schedule First Session: Client ID' links the 'Schedule First Session' activity with the attribute 'Client ID'.
- **Conduct Therapy Session: Client ID, Session Type**: The bind 'Conduct Therapy Session: Client ID, Session Type' links the 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Session Type'.
- **Evaluate Progress: Client ID, Progress Score**: The bind 'Evaluate Progress: Client ID, Progress Score' links the 'Evaluate Progress' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Progress Score'.
- **Adjust Treatment Plan: Client ID, Progress Score, Treatment Status**: The bind 'Adjust Treatment Plan: Client ID, Progress Score, Treatment Status' links the 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity with the attributes 'Client ID', 'Progress Score', and 'Treatment Status'.
- **Provide Feedback: Client ID, Feedback Type**: The bind 'Provide Feedback: Client ID, Feedback Type' links the 'Provide Feedback' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Feedback Type'.
- **Complete Treatment: Client ID**: The bind 'Complete Treatment: Client ID' links the 'Complete Treatment' activity with the attribute 'Client ID'.
- **Conduct Final Assessment: Client ID, Therapist ID, Progress Score**: The bind 'Conduct Final Assessment: Client ID, Therapist ID, Progress Score' links the 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity with the attributes 'Client ID', 'Therapist ID', and 'Progress Score'.

## Constraints


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the therapy treatment process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Assess Initial Condition,' which involves evaluating the client's initial condition, identified by 'Client ID,' before any treatment begins. This establishes the foundational context from which all subsequent therapeutic actions proceed.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Complete Treatment,' which represents the conclusion of the client's treatment process, identified by 'Client ID.' This activity must be the last to occur, coherently closing the therapeutic journey once all necessary steps have been fulfilled.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraints**
The 'Evaluate Progress' activity must occur at least once during the process, assessing the client's progress through a recorded 'Progress Score.' This adds depth to the treatment process, ensuring that the client's development is continuously monitored rather than assumed.

Similarly, the 'Provide Feedback' activity must occur at least once, delivering feedback categorized by 'Feedback Type' as Positive, Neutral, or Negative. This reinforces the importance of communication between therapist and client throughout the treatment journey.

#### **Absence Constraints**
In contrast, the 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity must not occur more than once within the process. This exclusion preserves the integrity of the evaluation phase, ensuring that the final assessment remains a singular, conclusive event rather than a repeated occurrence.

#### **Exactly Constraints**
In summary, the 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity must occur exactly once in the process, binding 'Client ID' and 'Therapist ID.' This fixed boundary ensures that each client receives precisely one personalized treatment plan, avoiding redundancy or conflicting plans.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraints**
If the 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity occurs, modifying the plan based on 'Progress Score' and 'Treatment Status,' then the 'Evaluate Progress' activity must also occur somewhere in the process. This cause-effect relationship ensures that any adjustment to the treatment plan is necessarily grounded in a prior or subsequent evaluation of the client's progress, reinforcing coherence between assessment and adaptation.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
1. The activity 'Develop Treatment Plan' is permitted to occur only if an 'Assess Initial Condition' activity has occurred before it. This sets up the initial context in which the client's condition must be evaluated before any treatment strategy can be formulated.
2. The activity 'Schedule First Session' is permitted to occur only if a 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity has occurred before it, ensuring that the personalized plan is established prior to arranging the client's first therapy session.
3. The activity 'Complete Treatment' is permitted to occur only if a 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity, binding 'Therapist ID' and 'Progress Score,' has occurred before it. This guarantees that treatment cannot be concluded without a final evaluation confirming the client's status.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If the 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity occurs with the condition 'Session Type is Initial,' then the 'Evaluate Progress' activity must eventually occur afterward. This ensures a cohesive temporal structure in which initial sessions are followed by an assessment of the client's progress.
2. If the 'Evaluate Progress' activity occurs with a 'Progress Score' below a satisfactory threshold, then the 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity must occur afterward, with 'Treatment Status' set to Adjusted. This ensures the treatment plan is responsively modified in accordance with the client's demonstrated progress.

#### **Succession Constraints**
Whenever an 'Evaluate Progress' activity occurs, it must be causally linked to an eventual 'Provide Feedback' activity, ensuring that feedback consistently follows progress evaluation. This establishes a logical, ordered progression connecting assessment to communication throughout the therapeutic process.

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Alternate Response Constraints**
Each occurrence of the 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity must be followed by a unique 'Provide Feedback' activity before another 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity can take place. This promotes balance, ensuring that each therapy session is individually acknowledged with corresponding feedback, without overlapping or omitted responses.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
Whenever a 'Schedule First Session' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity with 'Session Type is Initial,' with no intervening activities. This captures an immediate cause-and-effect relationship, ensuring that scheduling directly and promptly leads to the first therapeutic engagement.

#### **Chain Succession Constraints**
Whenever an 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity occurs with 'Treatment Status is Adjusted,' it must be immediately succeeded by a 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity, with no intervening events. This tightly coupled sequence ensures that any adjustment to the treatment plan is instantly followed by the scheduling of a follow-up session, preserving continuity of care.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity and the 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity are mutually dependent, ensuring that whenever one occurs, the other occurs as well within the same process instance. This conjunction reflects the necessary pairing of plan modification with continued client engagement.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Complete Treatment' activity and the 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity cannot both occur once 'Treatment Status' has been marked as Completed. This exclusion ensures that a completed treatment cannot be subsequently modified, thereby preserving the integrity of the process by preventing conflicting statuses.

### **Negative Dependency Constraints**

#### **Not Responded Existence Constraints**
The occurrence of 'Complete Treatment' does not necessitate nor imply a subsequent 'Assess Initial Condition' activity. This contrast reinforces that treatment conclusion for one client does not trigger the initiation of a new assessment cycle, disallowing unintended contingent occurrences.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraints**
The 'Complete Treatment' activity must never be immediately followed or immediately preceded by a 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity. This contrast prevents an abrupt and structurally inconsistent transition between the conclusion of treatment and the resumption of therapeutic sessions, preserving the model's defined and orderly flow.
