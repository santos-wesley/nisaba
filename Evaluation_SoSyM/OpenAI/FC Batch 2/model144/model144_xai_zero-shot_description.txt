**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Therapy Process**

This model describes a structured **psychotherapy treatment pathway** that manages the lifecycle of a client’s therapy from intake to completion. It enforces a logical, partially flexible sequence of clinical and administrative activities while incorporating data constraints and cardinality rules.

### Process Overview

The therapy process begins with an **initial assessment** and follows a core sequence of treatment planning, therapy delivery, progress monitoring, and eventual closure. The model allows for iterative treatment adjustments while maintaining strict clinical safeguards.

### Core Activity Flow

1. **Assess Initial Condition** must be the very first activity performed for any new client.
2. **Develop Treatment Plan** can only occur after the initial assessment has been completed.
3. Once the treatment plan is developed, the system **must** schedule the first session (**Chain Response**).
4. Therapy sessions can only be conducted after a session has been scheduled.
5. After conducting therapy sessions, the therapist **must** evaluate progress.
6. Based on the progress evaluation, the treatment plan may be adjusted. When an adjustment occurs, the model enforces that **feedback must be provided**, which in turn **must** lead to scheduling a follow-up session (strong chaining).
7. The process supports multiple therapy sessions and adjustment cycles.
8. **Complete Treatment** can only occur after a final assessment has been conducted.
9. **Conduct Final Assessment** is the designated end activity of the process.

### Key Constraints

**Ordering & Control Flow:**
- Strict precedence: Initial assessment → Treatment plan → First session.
- Therapy sessions cannot be scheduled before the initial session has been scheduled (**Not Co-Existence** between `Schedule First Session` and `Schedule Follow-Up`).
- Final assessment cannot occur before treatment is marked as complete, and it must be the last activity.

**Cardinality & Occurrence:**
- At least **3 therapy sessions** must be conducted (`Existence3[Conduct Therapy Session]`).
- **Provide Feedback** can occur at most 4 times (`Absence5`).

**Data Perspective (Bindings):**
- Every activity is linked to a **Client ID** (1–999999).
- Treatment planning and final assessment require a **Therapist ID** (1–9999).
- Therapy sessions must specify a **Session Type** (Initial, Follow-Up, or Crisis).
- Progress evaluations and final assessments record a **Progress Score** (0.0–100.0).
- Feedback activities must declare a **Feedback Type** (Positive, Neutral, Negative).
- Treatment adjustments track a **Treatment Status** (Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed).

**Negative Constraints:**
- Once treatment is completed, no further treatment plan adjustments are allowed (`Not Succession`).
- The first session and follow-up sessions are mutually exclusive in terms of scheduling logic.

### Summary

The model represents a **clinically governed, iterative therapy workflow** that ensures:
- Proper intake and planning before treatment begins,
- Sufficient therapeutic contact (minimum 3 sessions),
- Mandatory progress evaluation and feedback loops,
- Clear separation between initial and follow-up scheduling,
- A formal, documented closure involving a final assessment by a therapist.

The combination of Declare templates with data bindings creates a rich, enforceable model suitable for a clinical management system that balances structure with the natural variability of psychological treatment.
