## Activities
- **Assess Initial Condition**: The 'Assess Initial Condition' activity involves evaluating the client's initial state and needs at the beginning of the therapeutic process.
- **Develop Treatment Plan**: The 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity involves creating a customized therapeutic plan based on the client's initial assessment.
- **Schedule First Session**: The 'Schedule First Session' activity involves arranging the initial therapy session with the client.
- **Conduct Therapy Session**: The 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity involves delivering therapeutic interventions to the client during a session.
- **Evaluate Progress**: The 'Evaluate Progress' activity involves assessing the client's improvement and development throughout the treatment.
- **Adjust Treatment Plan**: The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity involves modifying the therapeutic approach based on the client's progress evaluation.
- **Provide Feedback**: The 'Provide Feedback' activity involves sharing observations and recommendations with the client regarding their treatment.
- **Schedule Follow-Up**: The 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity involves arranging subsequent therapy sessions to continue the treatment.
- **Complete Treatment**: The 'Complete Treatment' activity involves finalizing the therapeutic process with the client.
- **Conduct Final Assessment**: The 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity involves performing a comprehensive evaluation of the client's progress at the end of treatment.

- **Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative' specifies the nature of the feedback provided to the client.
- **Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed**: The attribute 'Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed' indicates the current state of the client's treatment plan.
