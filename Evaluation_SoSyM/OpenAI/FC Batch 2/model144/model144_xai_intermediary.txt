## Activities
- **Assess Initial Condition**: The 'Assess Initial Condition' activity represents the initial evaluation phase where the client's current state is thoroughly assessed to establish a baseline for treatment.
- **Develop Treatment Plan**: The 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity involves creating a customized treatment strategy based on the initial assessment to address the client's specific needs.
- **Schedule First Session**: The 'Schedule First Session' activity includes arranging the initial therapy appointment for the client following the development of the treatment plan.
- **Conduct Therapy Session**: The 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity entails carrying out the actual therapy sessions with the client according to the scheduled plan and session type.
- **Evaluate Progress**: The 'Evaluate Progress' activity focuses on assessing the client's advancement in treatment using a progress score to determine effectiveness.
- **Adjust Treatment Plan**: The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity involves modifying the existing treatment strategy based on evaluated progress and current treatment status.
- **Provide Feedback**: The 'Provide Feedback' activity consists of giving constructive input to the client regarding their progress, categorized by feedback type.
- **Schedule Follow-Up**: The 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity includes setting up subsequent appointments after providing feedback to continue the treatment process.
- **Complete Treatment**: The 'Complete Treatment' activity marks the conclusion of the primary treatment phase for the client.
- **Conduct Final Assessment**: The 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity involves performing an end-of-treatment evaluation to measure overall progress and outcomes.

## Attributes
- **Client ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Client ID: integer between 1 and 999999' uniquely identifies each client participating in the therapy process for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
- **Session Type: Initial, Follow-Up, Crisis**: The attribute 'Session Type: Initial, Follow-Up, Crisis' specifies the category of the therapy session being conducted, indicating whether it is the first meeting, a subsequent follow-up, or an emergency crisis intervention.
- **Progress Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Progress Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0' quantifies the client's improvement level on a scale from 0.0 to 100.0, providing a measurable indicator of treatment effectiveness.
- **Therapist ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Therapist ID: integer between 1 and 9999' uniquely identifies the therapist responsible for the client's treatment, ensuring proper assignment and accountability.
- **Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative' categorizes the nature of the feedback provided to the client, reflecting an overall positive, neutral, or negative assessment of their progress.
- **Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed**: The attribute 'Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed' indicates the current state of the treatment plan, whether it is in progress, has been modified, or has reached completion.
