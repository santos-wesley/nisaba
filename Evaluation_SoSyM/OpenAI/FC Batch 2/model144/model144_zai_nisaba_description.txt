#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Assess Initial Condition**: The 'Assess Initial Condition' activity represents the initial evaluation of a client's condition at the start of the therapy process.
- **Develop Treatment Plan**: The 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity involves creating a tailored treatment plan for the client based on the initial assessment.
- **Schedule First Session**: The 'Schedule First Session' activity entails arranging the first therapy session for the client.
- **Conduct Therapy Session**: The 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity involves carrying out a therapy session with the client.
- **Evaluate Progress**: The 'Evaluate Progress' activity includes assessing the client's progress during the course of treatment.
- **Adjust Treatment Plan**: The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity involves modifying the existing treatment plan based on the client's progress evaluation.
- **Provide Feedback**: The 'Provide Feedback' activity entails delivering feedback to the client regarding their therapy progress.
- **Schedule Follow-Up**: The 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity involves arranging a follow-up appointment for the client after feedback has been provided.
- **Complete Treatment**: The 'Complete Treatment' activity represents the formal completion of the client's treatment process.
- **Conduct Final Assessment**: The 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity involves performing a final evaluation of the client's overall progress and treatment outcomes.

## Attributes
- **Client ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Client ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each client in the therapy process.
- **Session Type: Initial, Follow-Up, Crisis**: The attribute 'Session Type: Initial, Follow-Up, Crisis' specifies the category of a therapy session, indicating whether it is an initial session, a follow-up session, or a crisis session.
- **Progress Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Progress Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents a numerical measure of the client's progress in therapy, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Therapist ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Therapist ID: integer between 1 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each therapist involved in the treatment process.
- **Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative' specifies the nature of the feedback provided to the client, categorizing it as positive, neutral, or negative.
- **Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed**: The attribute 'Treatment Status: Ongoing, Adjusted, Completed' indicates the current state of the treatment plan, categorizing it as ongoing, adjusted, or completed.

- **Develop Treatment Plan: Client ID, Therapist ID**: The bind 'Develop Treatment Plan: Client ID, Therapist ID' links the 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Therapist ID'.
- **Schedule First Session: Client ID**: The bind 'Schedule First Session: Client ID' links the 'Schedule First Session' activity with the attribute 'Client ID'.

- **Evaluate Progress: Client ID, Progress Score**: The bind 'Evaluate Progress: Client ID, Progress Score' links the 'Evaluate Progress' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Progress Score'.
- **Adjust Treatment Plan: Client ID, Progress Score, Treatment Status**: The bind 'Adjust Treatment Plan: Client ID, Progress Score, Treatment Status' links the 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity with the attributes 'Client ID', 'Progress Score', and 'Treatment Status'.
- **Provide Feedback: Client ID, Feedback Type**: The bind 'Provide Feedback: Client ID, Feedback Type' links the 'Provide Feedback' activity with the attributes 'Client ID' and 'Feedback Type'.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the therapy treatment process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Assess Initial Condition,' which involves the initial evaluation of a client's condition at the start of the therapy process. This activity is linked to the 'Client ID' attribute, establishing the foundational context for the entire treatment journey.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Conduct Final Assessment,' which involves performing a final evaluation of the client's overall progress and treatment outcomes. This activity is linked to the 'Client ID,' 'Therapist ID,' and 'Progress Score' attributes, ensuring a comprehensive closure to the therapy process. This activity must be the last to occur in the process.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraints**
The 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity must occur at least 3 times in the process. This ensures that the client receives adequate therapeutic intervention throughout the treatment process, adding depth and substance to the therapy journey. Each session is linked to the 'Client ID' and 'Session Type' attributes, which may be categorized as Initial, Follow-Up, or Crisis sessions.

#### **Absence Constraints**
The 'Provide Feedback' activity must not occur 5 times in the process. This restriction highlights the exclusions within the process and reinforces process integrity by specifying that excessive feedback instances are not permissible. The feedback activity is linked to the 'Client ID' and 'Feedback Type' attributes, where the feedback type may be Positive, Neutral, or Negative.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
1. The 'Assess Initial Condition' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This sets up an initial context where the treatment plan development must logically precede the initial condition assessment, guiding the sequence within the model. The 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity is linked to the 'Client ID' and 'Therapist ID' attributes.
2. The 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Evaluate Progress' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This ensures that therapy sessions are conducted based on prior progress evaluations, maintaining a logical and informed sequence. The 'Evaluate Progress' activity is linked to the 'Client ID' and 'Progress Score' attributes.
3. The 'Complete Treatment' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Conduct Final Assessment' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This ensures that the formal completion of treatment is based on a thorough final assessment, preserving the intended sequence within the model.
4. The 'Schedule First Session' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance, and the time between the 'Conduct Therapy Session' event and the 'Schedule First Session' event must be between 0 and 30 minutes. This temporal constraint ensures that the scheduling of the first session is closely tied to a preceding therapy session, maintaining a timely and coherent process flow.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Schedule First Session' activity occurs, then a 'Conduct Therapy Session' activity must eventually occur after the 'Schedule First Session' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Schedule First Session' events can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Conduct Therapy Session' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Schedule First Session' events. This defines an ordered flow where the therapy session follows the scheduling activity, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure. The 'Schedule First Session' activity is linked to the 'Client ID' attribute.
2. If an 'Evaluate Progress' activity occurs, then an 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity must eventually occur after the 'Evaluate Progress' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Evaluate Progress' events can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Adjust Treatment Plan' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Evaluate Progress' events. This ensures that progress evaluations lead to treatment plan adjustments, maintaining a responsive and adaptive process flow. The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity is linked to the 'Client ID,' 'Progress Score,' and 'Treatment Status' attributes, where the treatment status may be Ongoing, Adjusted, or Completed.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
1. Whenever a 'Develop Treatment Plan' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Schedule First Session' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The 'Schedule First Session' activity is allowed to happen in isolation. This captures an immediate cause-and-effect relationship where the development of a treatment plan directly triggers the scheduling of the first session, ensuring a seamless transition from planning to execution.
2. Whenever an 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Provide Feedback' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The 'Provide Feedback' activity is allowed to happen in isolation. This ensures that any adjustment to the treatment plan is directly communicated to the client through feedback, maintaining transparency and continuity in the therapeutic process.
3. Whenever a 'Provide Feedback' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between, and this must occur within at most 48 hours. The 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity is allowed to happen in isolation. This tightly coupled sequence ensures that feedback is promptly followed by the scheduling of a follow-up appointment, maintaining the momentum of the therapeutic process and ensuring timely continuity of care.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Schedule First Session' activity and the 'Schedule Follow-Up' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This means that if 'Schedule First Session' occurs one or more times, 'Schedule Follow-Up' must not occur at all in that process instance, and vice versa. It is acceptable for neither event to occur. This exclusion enforces a mutual exclusion between the two scheduling activities, allowing for different process variants based on which event, if any, occurs. This maintains process integrity by preventing conflicting scheduling events within the same client's treatment journey.

### **Negative Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Succession Constraints**
The 'Adjust Treatment Plan' activity and 'Complete Treatment' activity must not occur such that the 'Adjust Treatment Plan' event is followed by the 'Complete Treatment' event at any point later in the same process instance. Similarly, a 'Complete Treatment' event must not be preceded by an 'Adjust Treatment Plan' event. This means that once an 'Adjust Treatment Plan' occurs, 'Complete Treatment' cannot occur afterward, and if 'Complete Treatment' occurs, it cannot have been preceded by an 'Adjust Treatment Plan' event. Both events can occur in the same process instance, but not in the prohibited sequence. This restriction preserves the model's defined flow by ensuring that treatment adjustments do not lead directly to treatment completion, maintaining a clear distinction between ongoing therapeutic adjustments and the final closure of treatment.
