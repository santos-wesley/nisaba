## Activities
- **Identify Donor**: The 'Identify Donor' activity represents the identification phase for finding potential donors.
- **Contact Donor**: The 'Contact Donor' activity involves reaching out to potential donors to build relationships.
- **Request Donation**: The 'Request Donation' activity includes asking identified donors for contributions.
- **Receive Donation**: The 'Receive Donation' activity represents the receipt of actual donations from donors.
- **Send Thank You Note**: The 'Send Thank You Note' activity involves expressing gratitude to donors after receiving their donation.
- **Plan Fundraiser**: The 'Plan Fundraiser' activity covers the planning phase for organizing fundraising events.
- **Promote Event**: The 'Promote Event' activity includes marketing and promoting the fundraiser to attract attendees and donors.
- **Host Event**: The 'Host Event' activity represents the execution of the fundraising event itself.
- **Collect Feedback**: The 'Collect Feedback' activity involves gathering input from participants and donors about the event.
- **Analyze Feedback**: The 'Analyze Feedback' activity includes reviewing and interpreting the collected feedback.
- **Identify Improvement Areas**: The 'Identify Improvement Areas' activity represents pinpointing specific areas for enhancement based on feedback.
- **Implement Improvements**: The 'Implement Improvements' activity involves applying changes and improvements identified from the feedback.
- **Report Results**: The 'Report Results' activity covers documenting and reporting the outcomes of the fundraiser.
- **Archive Records**: The 'Archive Records' activity involves storing all records and documentation for future reference.
- **Evaluate Campaign**: The 'Evaluate Campaign' activity represents the final assessment of the entire fundraising campaign.

## Attributes
- **Donor ID: integer between 1 and 10000**: The attribute 'Donor ID: integer between 1 and 10000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each donor.
- **Donation Type: Cash, Check, Online Transfer**: The attribute 'Donation Type: Cash, Check, Online Transfer' specifies the possible methods through which donations can be made.
- **Donation Amount: float between 10 and 100000**: The attribute 'Donation Amount: float between 10 and 100000' indicates the monetary value of the donation received.
- **Event Type: Gala, Auction, Online Campaign**: The attribute 'Event Type: Gala, Auction, Online Campaign' defines the different formats of fundraising events that can be promoted.
- **Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative' categorizes the nature of the feedback collected from participants.
