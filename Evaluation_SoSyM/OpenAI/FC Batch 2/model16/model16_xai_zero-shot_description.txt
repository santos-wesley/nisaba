**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Charity Fundraising Campaign**

This model describes the end-to-end process of a nonprofit organization’s fundraising activities, combining donor management, event organization, and continuous improvement.

### Process Overview

The process always begins with **Identify Donor** and must eventually reach **Evaluate Campaign**, which marks the official end of the campaign cycle.

### Main Process Flow

1. **Donor Management Stream**
   - After identifying a donor, the organization must **Contact Donor**.
   - Once contact is made, a **Request Donation** must follow.
   - If a donation is received, the organization is obligated to **Send Thank You Note** (within 48 hours).
   - Donations over $100 have a particularly strict requirement to send the thank-you note.

2. **Event Organization Stream**
   - The organization **Promotes** exactly one fundraising **Event** (Gala, Auction, or Online Campaign).
   - After promotion, the **Host Event** must occur.
   - Following the event, the organization must **Report Results**.

3. **Feedback & Improvement Cycle**
   - The process requires collecting feedback **at least 3 times** during the campaign.
   - Every collection of feedback must eventually lead to its **Analysis**.
   - Analysis must lead to the identification of **Improvement Areas**.
   - Once improvement areas are identified, they must be implemented within **7 days**.

4. **Closure**
   - After hosting the event, results are reported.
   - Records are then **Archived**.
   - Finally, the entire campaign is **Evaluated**.

### Data Perspective

The model tracks the following data elements:
- **Donor ID**: Integer (1–10,000)
- **Donation Type**: Cash, Check, or Online Transfer
- **Donation Amount**: Floating point value between $10 and $100,000
- **Event Type**: Gala, Auction, or Online Campaign
- **Feedback Type**: Positive, Neutral, or Negative

### Key Constraints

- **Exactly one** event promotion is allowed per campaign.
- There is a maximum **24-day** window between requesting and receiving a donation.
- A thank-you note must be sent no later than **48 hours** after receiving a donation (especially critical for donations > $100).
- Improvement actions must be implemented quickly — within **7 days** of identification.
- Feedback collection is mandatory and must occur at least three times.

The model enforces a well-structured, accountable fundraising lifecycle that combines donor stewardship, event execution, systematic feedback collection, and continuous organizational improvement.
