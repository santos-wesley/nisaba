## Activities
- **Identify Donor**: The 'Identify Donor' activity represents the process of identifying potential donors for a fundraising campaign.
- **Contact Donor**: The 'Contact Donor' activity involves reaching out to identified donors to establish communication.
- **Request Donation**: The 'Request Donation' activity entails formally requesting a donation from a contacted donor.
- **Receive Donation**: The 'Receive Donation' activity represents the act of receiving a donation from a donor.
- **Send Thank You Note**: The 'Send Thank You Note' activity involves sending a note of gratitude to donors who have made a contribution.
- **Plan Fundraiser**: The 'Plan Fundraiser' activity encompasses the planning phase for organizing a fundraising event.
- **Promote Event**: The 'Promote Event' activity involves promoting a planned fundraising event to attract attendees and donors.
- **Host Event**: The 'Host Event' activity represents the execution and hosting of the fundraising event.
- **Collect Feedback**: The 'Collect Feedback' activity includes gathering feedback from participants and stakeholders about the fundraising event.
- **Analyze Feedback**: The 'Analyze Feedback' activity involves analyzing the collected feedback to assess the event's effectiveness.
- **Identify Improvement Areas**: The 'Identify Improvement Areas' activity entails identifying areas for improvement based on the feedback analysis.
- **Implement Improvements**: The 'Implement Improvements' activity represents the implementation of identified improvements for future campaigns.
- **Report Results**: The 'Report Results' activity involves compiling and reporting the results of the fundraising campaign.
- **Archive Records**: The 'Archive Records' activity encompasses archiving all records related to the fundraising campaign for future reference.
- **Evaluate Campaign**: The 'Evaluate Campaign' activity represents the final evaluation of the overall fundraising campaign's success and impact.

## Attributes
- **Donor ID: integer between 1 and 10000**: The attribute 'Donor ID: integer between 1 and 10000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each donor within the system.
- **Donation Type: Cash, Check, Online Transfer**: The attribute 'Donation Type: Cash, Check, Online Transfer' specifies the possible methods through which a donation can be made, including cash, check, or online transfer.
- **Donation Amount: float between 10 and 100000**: The attribute 'Donation Amount: float between 10 and 100000' represents the monetary value of a donation, ranging from 10 to 100000.
- **Event Type: Gala, Auction, Online Campaign**: The attribute 'Event Type: Gala, Auction, Online Campaign' specifies the types of fundraising events that can be organized, including a gala, an auction, or an online campaign.
- **Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Feedback Type: Positive, Neutral, Negative' specifies the possible categories of feedback received from participants, ranging from positive to neutral to negative.
