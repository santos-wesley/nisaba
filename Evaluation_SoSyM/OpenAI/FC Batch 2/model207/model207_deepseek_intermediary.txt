## Activities
- **activity Plan Event**: The 'Plan Event' activity represents the planning phase for organizing an event.
- **activity Book Venue**: The 'Book Venue' activity involves reserving a location for the event.
- **activity Send Invitations**: The 'Send Invitations' activity includes distributing invitations to guests for the event.
- **activity Arrange Catering**: The 'Arrange Catering' activity involves organizing food and beverage services for the event.
- **activity Hire Staff**: The 'Hire Staff' activity includes recruiting personnel to work during the event.
- **activity Set Up Venue**: The 'Set Up Venue' activity involves preparing and arranging the event location.
- **activity Conduct Rehearsal**: The 'Conduct Rehearsal' activity includes running a practice session before the event.
- **activity Welcome Guests**: The 'Welcome Guests' activity involves greeting attendees as they arrive at the event.
- **activity Facilitate Activities**: The 'Facilitate Activities' activity includes managing and coordinating event activities.
- **activity Deliver Speech**: The 'Deliver Speech' activity involves presenting a speech during the event.
- **activity Serve Meals**: The 'Serve Meals' activity includes providing food service to guests during the event.
- **activity Coordinate Photography**: The 'Coordinate Photography' activity involves managing photography services to capture moments during the event.
- **activity Distribute Gifts**: The 'Distribute Gifts' activity includes handing out gifts to attendees at the event.
- **activity Tear Down Venue**: The 'Tear Down Venue' activity involves dismantling and cleaning up the event location after the event.
- **activity Collect Feedback**: The 'Collect Feedback' activity includes gathering feedback from guests and staff about the event.

## Attributes
- **Event ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Event ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each event.
- **Venue: Hall A, Hall B, Hall C**: The attribute 'Venue: Hall A, Hall B, Hall C' specifies the available locations where the event can be held.
- **Catering Service: Buffet, Sit-down Dinner, Cocktail**: The attribute 'Catering Service: Buffet, Sit-down Dinner, Cocktail' specifies the types of catering options available for the event.
- **Number of Guests: integer between 10 and 500**: The attribute 'Number of Guests: integer between 10 and 500' represents the count of guests invited to the event.
- **Invitation Status: Sent, Not Sent**: The attribute 'Invitation Status: Sent, Not Sent' indicates whether invitations have been sent to guests.
- **Staff Role: Waiter, Coordinator, Cleaner**: The attribute 'Staff Role: Waiter, Coordinator, Cleaner' specifies the roles assigned to hired staff members.
- **Rehearsal ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Rehearsal ID: integer between 1 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each rehearsal session.
- **Budget: float between 1000 and 50000**: The attribute 'Budget: float between 1000 and 50000' represents the allocated budget for planning and executing the event.
