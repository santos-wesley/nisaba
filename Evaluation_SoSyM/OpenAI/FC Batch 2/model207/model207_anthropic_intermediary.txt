## Activities
- **Plan Event**: The 'Plan Event' activity represents the planning phase for organizing an event.
- **Book Venue**: The 'Book Venue' activity involves reserving a location for the event.
- **Send Invitations**: The 'Send Invitations' activity includes distributing invitations to guests for the event.
- **Arrange Catering**: The 'Arrange Catering' activity involves organizing food and beverage services for the event.
- **Hire Staff**: The 'Hire Staff' activity involves recruiting personnel to support the event.
- **Set Up Venue**: The 'Set Up Venue' activity involves preparing the venue's physical layout and equipment for the event.
- **Conduct Rehearsal**: The 'Conduct Rehearsal' activity involves practicing the event proceedings before the actual occasion.
- **Welcome Guests**: The 'Welcome Guests' activity involves greeting and receiving attendees as they arrive at the event.
- **Facilitate Activities**: The 'Facilitate Activities' activity involves managing and coordinating the planned activities during the event.
- **Deliver Speech**: The 'Deliver Speech' activity involves presenting a speech to the attendees during the event.
- **Serve Meals**: The 'Serve Meals' activity involves providing food to the guests during the event.
- **Coordinate Photography**: The 'Coordinate Photography' activity involves managing photography services to capture moments during the event.
- **Distribute Gifts**: The 'Distribute Gifts' activity involves handing out gifts to guests or participants at the event.
- **Tear Down Venue**: The 'Tear Down Venue' activity involves dismantling and cleaning up the venue after the event concludes.
- **Collect Feedback**: The 'Collect Feedback' activity includes gathering feedback from guests and staff about the event.

## Attributes
- **Event ID: Integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Event ID: Integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number of the event.
- **Venue: Hall A, Hall B, Hall C**: The attribute 'Venue: Hall A, Hall B, Hall C' specifies the possible locations where the event can be held.
- **Catering Service: Buffet, Sit-down Dinner, Cocktail**: The attribute 'Catering Service: Buffet, Sit-down Dinner, Cocktail' specifies the types of catering services available for the event.
- **Number of Guests: Integer between 10 and 500**: The attribute 'Number of Guests: Integer between 10 and 500' represents the total number of attendees expected at the event.
- **Invitation Status: Sent, Not Sent**: The attribute 'Invitation Status: Sent, Not Sent' specifies whether the invitations for the event have been sent.
- **Staff Role: Waiter, Coordinator, Cleaner**: The attribute 'Staff Role: Waiter, Coordinator, Cleaner' specifies the possible roles assigned to staff members hired for the event.
- **Rehearsal ID: Integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Rehearsal ID: Integer between 1 and 9999' represents the unique identification number of a rehearsal session.
- **Budget: Float between 1000 and 50000**: The attribute 'Budget: Float between 1000 and 50000' represents the amount of money allocated for the event.
