#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Analyze International Market**: The 'Analyze International Market' activity represents the process of evaluating and analyzing a foreign market to understand its potential for business expansion.
- **Identify Business Opportunities**: The 'Identify Business Opportunities' activity involves discovering and cataloging potential business prospects in the target international market.
- **Conduct Feasibility Study**: The 'Conduct Feasibility Study' activity entails performing a detailed assessment to determine the viability and practicality of entering the international market.
- **Develop Business Plan**: The 'Develop Business Plan' activity involves creating a comprehensive plan outlining the strategy, objectives, and operational details for international expansion.
- **Acquire Necessary Permits**: The 'Acquire Necessary Permits' activity represents the process of obtaining all required legal and regulatory approvals to operate in the target international market.
- **Set Up International Office**: The 'Set Up International Office' activity involves establishing a physical or operational presence in the target foreign market.
- **Hire Local Staff**: The 'Hire Local Staff' activity entails recruiting and onboarding local employees to support operations in the international market.
- **Initiate Marketing Campaign**: The 'Initiate Marketing Campaign' activity involves launching promotional and marketing efforts to create awareness and demand in the new international market.
- **Establish Supply Chain**: The 'Establish Supply Chain' activity represents the process of setting up the logistics and distribution networks required to support international operations.
- **Negotiate with Local Vendors**: The 'Negotiate with Local Vendors' activity involves engaging with local suppliers and partners to secure favorable terms for goods and services.
- **Monitor Market Response**: The 'Monitor Market Response' activity entails tracking and evaluating the market's reaction to the business's entry and marketing efforts.
- **Adjust Strategy**: The 'Adjust Strategy' activity involves modifying and refining the business approach based on observed market feedback and performance data.
- **Manage International Logistics**: The 'Manage International Logistics' activity represents the ongoing coordination and oversight of cross-border supply chain and distribution operations.
- **Assess Performance**: The 'Assess Performance' activity involves evaluating the overall outcomes and effectiveness of the international business operations.
- **Report to Headquarters**: The 'Report to Headquarters' activity entails compiling and communicating performance results and strategic updates to the central corporate office.

## Attributes
- **Market Size: integer between 1000 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Market Size: integer between 1000 and 1000000' represents the estimated size of the target international market in terms of potential customers or market volume.
- **Investment Amount: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Investment Amount: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0' represents the financial investment required or allocated for the international market entry.
- **Market Attractiveness: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Market Attractiveness: High, Medium, Low' specifies the assessed attractiveness level of the target international market.
- **Feasibility Status: Positive, Negative**: The attribute 'Feasibility Status: Positive, Negative' indicates the outcome of the feasibility study, determining whether the international expansion is viable.
- **Permit Status: Approved, Pending, Rejected**: The attribute 'Permit Status: Approved, Pending, Rejected' specifies the current status of the necessary permits required for operating in the international market.
- **Staffing Level: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Staffing Level: High, Medium, Low' indicates the level of local staffing required or achieved for the international office.
- **Marketing Budget: float between 5000.0 and 500000.0**: The attribute 'Marketing Budget: float between 5000.0 and 500000.0' represents the financial budget allocated for marketing campaigns in the international market.
- **Market Response: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Market Response: Positive, Neutral, Negative' specifies the observed response from the market regarding the business's entry and marketing efforts.
- **Strategy Adjustment: Yes, No**: The attribute 'Strategy Adjustment: Yes, No' indicates whether a strategy adjustment has been made in response to market feedback.
- **Performance Evaluation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor**: The attribute 'Performance Evaluation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor' represents the assessed performance level of the international business operations.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the international market expansion process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Analyze International Market,' which involves evaluating and analyzing a foreign market to understand its potential for business expansion. This activity requires the attributes 'Market Size' and 'Market Attractiveness,' establishing the initial context for the entire process.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Report to Headquarters,' which entails compiling and communicating performance results and strategic updates to the central corporate office. This activity must be the last to occur in the process, providing a coherent closure to the international expansion effort.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraints**
1. The 'Initiate Marketing Campaign' activity must occur at least once in the process. This involves launching promotional and marketing efforts to create awareness and demand in the new international market, adding depth and visibility to the business's entry.
2. The 'Manage International Logistics' activity must occur at least once in the process. This represents the ongoing coordination and oversight of cross-border supply chain and distribution operations, ensuring that the logistical backbone of the international operations is established and maintained.

#### **Exactly Constraints**
The 'Report to Headquarters' activity must occur exactly once in the process. This sets a fixed boundary on the activity's occurrence, ensuring precision and a singular, definitive communication of performance results and strategic updates to the central corporate office.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If an 'Analyze International Market' activity occurs, then an 'Identify Business Opportunities' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that the evaluation of the foreign market is followed by the discovery and cataloging of potential business prospects, maintaining a cohesive temporal structure.
2. If a 'Conduct Feasibility Study' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'Feasibility Status' is 'Positive,' then a 'Develop Business Plan' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that a comprehensive plan outlining the strategy, objectives, and operational details for international expansion is developed only when the feasibility study yields a positive outcome.
3. If a 'Develop Business Plan' activity occurs, then an 'Acquire Necessary Permits' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that the process of obtaining all required legal and regulatory approvals to operate in the target international market follows the development of the business plan.
4. If a 'Set Up International Office' activity occurs, then a 'Hire Local Staff' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that the establishment of a physical or operational presence in the target foreign market is followed by the recruitment and onboarding of local employees to support operations.
5. If a 'Hire Local Staff' activity occurs, then a 'Negotiate with Local Vendors' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that engaging with local suppliers and partners to secure favorable terms for goods and services follows the hiring of local staff.
6. If an 'Initiate Marketing Campaign' activity occurs, then a 'Monitor Market Response' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that tracking and evaluating the market's reaction to the business's entry and marketing efforts follows the launch of promotional activities.
7. If an 'Adjust Strategy' activity occurs, then an 'Assess Performance' activity must eventually occur after it. This ensures that the evaluation of the overall outcomes and effectiveness of the international business operations follows any modifications and refinements to the business approach based on observed market feedback.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
1. Whenever an 'Acquire Necessary Permits' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Set Up International Office' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. Additionally, the 'Set Up International Office' must occur within 0 to 5 days after the 'Acquire Necessary Permits' event. This direct cause-and-effect relationship ensures an uninterrupted transition from obtaining legal approvals to establishing the operational presence in the international market.
2. Whenever a 'Monitor Market Response' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'Market Response' is 'Negative,' it must be immediately followed by an 'Adjust Strategy' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. Additionally, the 'Adjust Strategy' must occur within 0 to 7 days after the 'Monitor Market Response' event. This direct cause-and-effect relationship ensures that a negative market reaction promptly triggers a strategic adjustment without delay.
3. Whenever an 'Assess Performance' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Report to Headquarters' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. Additionally, the 'Report to Headquarters' must occur within 0 to 48 hours after the 'Assess Performance' event. This direct cause-and-effect relationship ensures that performance evaluation results are promptly communicated to the central corporate office, maintaining a tightly coupled flow of information.
