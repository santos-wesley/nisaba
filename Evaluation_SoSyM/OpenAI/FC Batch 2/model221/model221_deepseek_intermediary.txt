## Activities
- **Analyze International Market**: The 'Analyze International Market' activity involves examining global market trends, competitors, and potential opportunities for international expansion.
- **Identify Business Opportunities**: The 'Identify Business Opportunities' activity focuses on recognizing potential business prospects in the international market.
- **Conduct Feasibility Study**: The 'Conduct Feasibility Study' activity evaluates the viability and potential success of entering a specific international market.
- **Develop Business Plan**: The 'Develop Business Plan' activity involves creating a comprehensive plan outlining the strategy for international business operations.
- **Acquire Necessary Permits**: The 'Acquire Necessary Permits' activity involves obtaining all required legal permissions and licenses for international operations.
- **Set Up International Office**: The 'Set Up International Office' activity involves establishing a physical or virtual office presence in the target international market.
- **Hire Local Staff**: The 'Hire Local Staff' activity involves recruiting and employing personnel from the local market.
- **Initiate Marketing Campaign**: The 'Initiate Marketing Campaign' activity involves launching promotional activities to introduce products or services in the international market.
- **Establish Supply Chain**: The 'Establish Supply Chain' activity involves setting up logistics and supply networks for international operations.
- **Negotiate with Local Vendors**: The 'Negotiate with Local Vendors' activity involves discussing terms and agreements with local suppliers and partners.
- **Monitor Market Response**: The 'Monitor Market Response' activity involves tracking and analyzing customer and market reactions to business activities.
- **Adjust Strategy**: The 'Adjust Strategy' activity involves modifying the business approach based on market feedback and performance data.
- **Manage International Logistics**: The 'Manage International Logistics' activity involves overseeing the transportation, warehousing, and distribution of goods across international borders.
- **Assess Performance**: The 'Assess Performance' activity involves evaluating the effectiveness and outcomes of international business operations.
- **Report to Headquarters**: The 'Report to Headquarters' activity involves communicating results, insights, and updates to the central corporate office.

## Attributes
- **Market Size: integer between 1000 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Market Size: integer between 1000 and 1000000' represents the total addressable market size in terms of potential customers or units.
- **Investment Amount: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Investment Amount: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0' represents the financial investment required for the feasibility study or business initiative.
- **Market Attractiveness: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Market Attractiveness: High, Medium, Low' specifies the level of appeal and potential of the target international market.
- **Feasibility Status: Positive, Negative**: The attribute 'Feasibility Status: Positive, Negative' indicates whether the feasibility study resulted in a favorable or unfavorable outcome.
- **Permit Status: Approved, Pending, Rejected**: The attribute 'Permit Status: Approved, Pending, Rejected' indicates the current status of the permit application process.
- **Staffing Level: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Staffing Level: High, Medium, Low' specifies the level of staffing required or achieved for local operations.
- **Marketing Budget: float between 5000.0 and 500000.0**: The attribute 'Marketing Budget: float between 5000.0 and 500000.0' represents the allocated budget for marketing campaigns in the international market.
- **Market Response: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Market Response: Positive, Neutral, Negative' indicates the type of feedback received from the target market.
- **Strategy Adjustment: Yes, No**: The attribute 'Strategy Adjustment: Yes, No' indicates whether a strategy adjustment was made based on market response.
- **Performance Evaluation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor**: The attribute 'Performance Evaluation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor' specifies the rating of the overall performance assessment.
