## Activities
- **Analyze International Market**: The 'Analyze International Market' activity represents the initial analysis of the international market to identify potential for business expansion.
- **Identify Business Opportunities**: The 'Identify Business Opportunities' activity involves pinpointing specific business opportunities within the analyzed international market.
- **Conduct Feasibility Study**: The 'Conduct Feasibility Study' activity includes evaluating the practicality and potential success of entering the international market.
- **Develop Business Plan**: The 'Develop Business Plan' activity entails creating a detailed plan outlining the strategy for international business operations.
- **Acquire Necessary Permits**: The 'Acquire Necessary Permits' activity focuses on obtaining all required legal permits for operating in the international market.
- **Set Up International Office**: The 'Set Up International Office' activity involves establishing a physical or operational office in the target international location.
- **Hire Local Staff**: The 'Hire Local Staff' activity includes recruiting and employing local personnel to support international operations.
- **Initiate Marketing Campaign**: The 'Initiate Marketing Campaign' activity represents launching promotional efforts to introduce the business in the international market.
- **Establish Supply Chain**: The 'Establish Supply Chain' activity involves setting up the logistics and supply networks necessary for international business.
- **Negotiate with Local Vendors**: The 'Negotiate with Local Vendors' activity includes discussing and finalizing agreements with local suppliers and partners.
- **Monitor Market Response**: The 'Monitor Market Response' activity entails tracking and analyzing the market's reaction to the business activities.
- **Adjust Strategy**: The 'Adjust Strategy' activity involves modifying the business approach based on market feedback and performance.
- **Manage International Logistics**: The 'Manage International Logistics' activity focuses on overseeing the transportation and distribution of goods across borders.
- **Assess Performance**: The 'Assess Performance' activity includes evaluating the overall success and effectiveness of the international operations.
- **Report to Headquarters**: The 'Report to Headquarters' activity represents compiling and sending performance reports back to the main company headquarters.

## Attributes
- **Market Size: integer between 1000 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Market Size: integer between 1000 and 1000000' represents the estimated number of potential customers or market volume in the target international market.
- **Investment Amount: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Investment Amount: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0' indicates the financial resources required or allocated for the international venture.
- **Market Attractiveness: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Market Attractiveness: High, Medium, Low' specifies the level of appeal of the international market based on various economic and competitive factors.
- **Feasibility Status: Positive, Negative**: The attribute 'Feasibility Status: Positive, Negative' denotes whether the feasibility study concludes in favor of or against proceeding with the international expansion.
- **Permit Status: Approved, Pending, Rejected**: The attribute 'Permit Status: Approved, Pending, Rejected' reflects the current state of the legal permits needed for international operations.
- **Staffing Level: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Staffing Level: High, Medium, Low' describes the adequacy of the local staff hired in terms of numbers and expertise.
- **Marketing Budget: float between 5000.0 and 500000.0**: The attribute 'Marketing Budget: float between 5000.0 and 500000.0' represents the allocated funds for marketing activities in the international market.
- **Market Response: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Market Response: Positive, Neutral, Negative' captures the overall reaction of the market to the launched business and marketing efforts.
- **Strategy Adjustment: Yes, No**: The attribute 'Strategy Adjustment: Yes, No' indicates whether changes to the business strategy are required based on performance monitoring.
- **Performance Evaluation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor**: The attribute 'Performance Evaluation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor' rates the success of the international operations across key performance indicators.
