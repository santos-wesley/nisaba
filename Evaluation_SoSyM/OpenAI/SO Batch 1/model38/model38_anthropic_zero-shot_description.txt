# Description of the Multi-Perspective Declare Model

## Overview
This process model describes a report generation workflow that begins with data collection and progresses through analysis, drafting, review, approval, and publication, with optional feedback and revision cycles.

## Activities and Data Attributes
The process involves eight activities, several of which carry associated data:

- **Collect Data** records the number of **Data Points** collected, an integer ranging from 100 to 10,000.
- **Analyze Data** records the **Report Accuracy** achieved, a float value between 0.0 and 100.0.
- **Approve Report** records the **Approval Status**, which can be *Pending*, *Approved*, or *Rejected*.
- **Gather Feedback** records the **Feedback Type** received, categorized as *Positive*, *Negative*, or *Neutral*.
- **Revise Report** records the **Revision Count**, an integer between 0 and 10.

## Control-Flow and Data Constraints

1. **Process Initiation**: The process must always start with **Collect Data**.

2. **Mandatory Publication**: **Publish Report** must occur at least once during the process.

3. **Ordering of Approval and Review**: **Review Report** must occur before **Approve Report** can take place (every approval must be preceded by a review).

4. **Approval Triggers Publication**: Whenever **Approve Report** occurs with an Approval Status of *Approved*, then **Publish Report** must eventually follow, within 2 days.

5. **Negative Feedback Triggers Revision**: Whenever **Gather Feedback** occurs with a Feedback Type of *Negative*, then **Revise Report** must eventually follow, within 5 days.

6. **Revision Limit Constraint**: If a **Revise Report** occurs with a Revision Count greater than 3, no subsequent **Revise Report** can follow it later in the process (no repeated excessive revisions in succession).

7. **High Accuracy Requirement**: Whenever **Approve Report** occurs and the associated Report Accuracy is 90 or above, this must have been preceded by an **Analyze Data** activity (ensuring sufficient analysis quality before approval).

8. **Alternating Analysis and Drafting**: **Analyze Data** and **Draft Report** must alternate strictly—each occurrence of one must be followed by the other before it can repeat—and this alternation must happen within a 48-hour window between occurrences.

9. **Immediate Chain from Approval to Publication**: Immediately after **Approve Report** occurs with an Approval Status of *Approved*, **Publish Report** must be the very next activity, occurring within 3 days.

10. **Immediate Chain from Draft to Review**: **Draft Report** must be immediately followed by **Review Report** as the next activity in the process, within 1 hour.

## Summary
This model enforces a structured, quality-controlled reporting workflow: data must be collected first, reports must be reviewed before approval, approved reports must be promptly published, negative feedback mandates revisions, and revision counts are capped to avoid excessive rework, all while maintaining tight temporal and sequential dependencies between key steps like drafting, reviewing, approving, and publishing.
