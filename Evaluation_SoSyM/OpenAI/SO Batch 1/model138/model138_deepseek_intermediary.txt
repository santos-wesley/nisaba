## Activities
- **Identify Risks**: The 'Identify Risks' activity represents the process of identifying potential risks that could affect business continuity.
- **Develop Continuity Plan**: The 'Develop Continuity Plan' activity involves creating a comprehensive plan to ensure business operations can continue during disruptions.
- **Test Continuity Plan**: The 'Test Continuity Plan' activity involves executing tests to validate the effectiveness of the continuity plan.
- **Review Test Results**: The 'Review Test Results' activity includes analyzing the outcomes of continuity plan tests to assess performance.
- **Implement Improvements**: The 'Implement Improvements' activity involves making necessary changes and enhancements based on test results.
- **Communicate Plan**: The 'Communicate Plan' activity involves disseminating the continuity plan to relevant stakeholders.
- **Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity involves continuously tracking identified risks to detect changes or new threats.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity involves providing instruction and education to staff on continuity procedures.
- **Activate Continuity Plan**: The 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity involves putting the continuity plan into action during an incident.
- **Evaluate Incident Response**: The 'Evaluate Incident Response' activity involves assessing the effectiveness of the response to an incident.

## Attributes
- **Risk Level: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Risk Level: integer between 1 and 10' represents the severity level assigned to an identified risk, ranging from low (1) to high (10).
- **Plan Effectiveness: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Plan Effectiveness: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the measured effectiveness of the continuity plan as a percentage score.
- **Test Outcome: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Test Outcome: Pass, Fail' specifies whether the continuity plan test resulted in a pass or fail.
- **Improvement Status: Completed, Pending**: The attribute 'Improvement Status: Completed, Pending' indicates whether an improvement action has been completed or is still pending.
- **Communication Method: Email, Meeting, Newsletter**: The attribute 'Communication Method: Email, Meeting, Newsletter' specifies the method used to communicate the continuity plan to stakeholders.
- **Training Hours: integer between 1 and 40**: The attribute 'Training Hours: integer between 1 and 40' represents the number of hours allocated for training sessions on continuity procedures.
- **Incident Severity: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Incident Severity: Low, Medium, High' specifies the severity level of an incident that triggers the activation of the continuity plan.
- **Response Time: float between 0.0 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Response Time: float between 0.0 and 24.0' represents the time taken to respond to an incident, measured in hours.
- **Stakeholder Engagement: High, Moderate, Low**: The attribute 'Stakeholder Engagement: High, Moderate, Low' indicates the level of stakeholder involvement in developing the continuity plan.
- **Monitoring Frequency: integer between 1 and 30**: The attribute 'Monitoring Frequency: integer between 1 and 30' represents the frequency of risk monitoring activities, measured in days.
