#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Identify Risks**: The 'Identify Risks' activity represents the initial phase of pinpointing potential threats and vulnerabilities in the business continuity management process.
- **Develop Continuity Plan**: The 'Develop Continuity Plan' activity involves creating a comprehensive strategy to ensure business operations can continue during disruptions.
- **Test Continuity Plan**: The 'Test Continuity Plan' activity includes simulating scenarios to evaluate the effectiveness of the continuity plan.
- **Review Test Results**: The 'Review Test Results' activity focuses on analyzing outcomes from continuity plan tests to identify strengths and weaknesses.
- **Implement Improvements**: The 'Implement Improvements' activity entails applying changes and enhancements based on test reviews to strengthen the continuity plan.
- **Communicate Plan**: The 'Communicate Plan' activity is responsible for disseminating the continuity plan details to relevant stakeholders using appropriate methods.
- **Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity involves ongoing surveillance of identified risks to detect any changes or emerging threats.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity includes educating staff on the continuity plan and their roles during an incident.
- **Activate Continuity Plan**: The 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity triggers the execution of the continuity plan in response to an actual incident.
- **Evaluate Incident Response**: The 'Evaluate Incident Response' activity assesses the performance and outcomes of the activated continuity plan after an incident.

## Attributes
- **Risk Level: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Risk Level: integer between 1 and 10' quantifies the severity or priority of identified risks on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Plan Effectiveness: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Plan Effectiveness: float between 0.0 and 100.0' measures the performance of the continuity plan as a percentage value ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Test Outcome: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Test Outcome: Pass, Fail' indicates whether the continuity plan test was successful (Pass) or unsuccessful (Fail).
- **Improvement Status: Completed, Pending**: The attribute 'Improvement Status: Completed, Pending' tracks the progress of implemented improvements as either Completed or Pending.
- **Communication Method: Email, Meeting, Newsletter**: The attribute 'Communication Method: Email, Meeting, Newsletter' specifies the channel used to communicate the continuity plan, which can be Email, Meeting, or Newsletter.
- **Training Hours: integer between 1 and 40**: The attribute 'Training Hours: integer between 1 and 40' represents the duration of training sessions in hours, ranging from 1 to 40.
- **Incident Severity: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Incident Severity: Low, Medium, High' categorizes the seriousness of an incident as Low, Medium, or High.
- **Response Time: float between 0.0 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Response Time: float between 0.0 and 24.0' measures the time taken to respond to an incident in hours, with values from 0.0 to 24.0.
- **Stakeholder Engagement: High, Moderate, Low**: The attribute 'Stakeholder Engagement: High, Moderate, Low' assesses the level of involvement from stakeholders as High, Moderate, or Low.
- **Monitoring Frequency: integer between 1 and 30**: The attribute 'Monitoring Frequency: integer between 1 and 30' indicates how often risks are monitored, in days or units, ranging from 1 to 30.

## Constraints
- **Init[Identify Risks] | |**: The 'Identify Risks' must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **End[Evaluate Incident Response] | |**: The 'Evaluate Incident Response' must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Alternate Response[Test Continuity Plan, Review Test Results] |A.Test Outcome is Pass | |0,2,d**: If a 'Test Continuity Plan' activity occurs with the condition that A.Test Outcome is Pass, then a 'Review Test Results' activity must occur after that 'Test Continuity Plan' event and before any subsequent 'Test Continuity Plan' occurs; no other occurrence of 'Test Continuity Plan' is allowed between a 'Test Continuity Plan' and its corresponding 'Review Test Results'. This must happen within a time frame of 0 to 2 days.
- **Chain Response[Review Test Results, Implement Improvements] |A.Plan Effectiveness < 80.0 |A.Improvement Status is Pending |0,48,h**: Whenever a 'Review Test Results' activity occurs with the condition that A.Plan Effectiveness < 80.0, it must be immediately followed by an 'Implement Improvements' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between, where the correlation condition is A.Improvement Status is Pending. This must occur within a time frame of 0 to 48 hours. The 'Implement Improvements' activity is allowed to happen isolated.
- **Response[Develop Continuity Plan, Communicate Plan] |A.Stakeholder Engagement is High |A.Communication Method in (Email, Meeting) |5,13,m**: If a 'Develop Continuity Plan' activity occurs with the condition that A.Stakeholder Engagement is High, then a 'Communicate Plan' activity must eventually occur after the 'Develop Continuity Plan' event at some point in the same process instance, where the correlation condition is A.Communication Method in (Email, Meeting). There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between, but it must happen within a time frame of 5 to 13 minutes. Multiple 'Develop Continuity Plan' can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Communicate Plan' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Develop Continuity Plan'. If 'Develop Continuity Plan' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Communicate Plan'.
- **Responded Existence[Communicate Plan, Conduct Training] |A.Communication Method is Meeting | |**: If 'Communicate Plan' activity occurs with the condition that A.Communication Method is Meeting in a process instance, then 'Conduct Training' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Communicate Plan' event. If 'Communicate Plan' does not occur, there is no requirement for 'Conduct Training' to occur. Multiple occurrences of 'Communicate Plan' are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Conduct Training', and multiple 'Conduct Training' instances are allowed.
- **Precedence[Activate Continuity Plan, Identify Risks] |A.Incident Severity in (Medium, High) | |**: The 'Identify Risks' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance, where the activation condition is A.Incident Severity in (Medium, High). Activations that occur without any prior 'Activate Continuity Plan' are violations of the constraint. Multiple 'Activate Continuity Plan' can occur, and any number of events (including other 'Identify Risks') can happen between an 'Activate Continuity Plan' and a subsequent 'Identify Risks'. This ensures that certain actions cannot proceed without the necessary prerequisites being met first.
- **Co-Existence[Monitor Risks, Identify Risks] |A.Monitoring Frequency <= 7 | |**: If 'Monitor Risks' activity occurs with the condition that A.Monitoring Frequency <= 7 in a process instance, then 'Identify Risks' must also occur in the same process instance, and vice versa.
- **Not Chain Succession[Activate Continuity Plan, Evaluate Incident Response] |A.Incident Severity is High | |10,15,s**: An 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity with the condition that A.Incident Severity is High cannot be immediately followed by an 'Evaluate Incident Response' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, an 'Evaluate Incident Response' event cannot be immediately preceded by an 'Activate Continuity Plan' event. This constraint allows multiple occurrences of 'Activate Continuity Plan' and 'Evaluate Incident Response' in any order, provided they are not adjacent in the event sequence. This must be considered within a time frame of 10 to 15 seconds.
- **Existence[Conduct Training] |A.Training Hours >= 8 |**: The 'Conduct Training' activity must occur at least once in the process where the activation condition is A.Training Hours >= 8.


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for business continuity management is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Identify Risks,' which represents the initial phase of pinpointing potential threats and vulnerabilities in the business continuity management process and requires the 'Risk Level' attribute.
#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Evaluate Incident Response,' which assesses the performance and outcomes of the activated continuity plan after an incident and uses the 'Response Time' attribute. This activity must be the last to occur in the process.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Conduct Training' activity must occur at least once in the process where the activation condition is 'Training Hours >= 8'. This ensures that staff education on the continuity plan and their roles during an incident takes place with sufficient duration, adding depth and reliability to the overall preparedness of the business continuity management process.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The activity 'Identify Risks' is permitted to occur only if an 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance, where the activation condition is 'Incident Severity in (Medium, High)'. This sets up the initial context that certain risk identification actions cannot proceed without the necessary prerequisites of an incident activation being met first.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Develop Continuity Plan' activity occurs with the condition that 'Stakeholder Engagement is High,' then a 'Communicate Plan' activity must eventually occur after the 'Develop Continuity Plan' event at some point in the same process instance, where the correlation condition is 'Communication Method in (Email, Meeting)'. This must happen within a time frame of 5 to 13 minutes. This defines an ordered flow where comprehensive strategy creation is followed by dissemination to relevant stakeholders, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure.
2. If a 'Test Continuity Plan' activity occurs with the condition that 'Test Outcome is Pass,' then a 'Review Test Results' activity must occur after that 'Test Continuity Plan' event and before any subsequent 'Test Continuity Plan' occurs; no other occurrence of 'Test Continuity Plan' is allowed between a 'Test Continuity Plan' and its corresponding 'Review Test Results'. This must happen within a time frame of 0 to 2 days. This defines an ordered flow where simulation of scenarios is followed by analysis of outcomes to identify strengths and weaknesses.

#### **Succession Constraints**
Whenever a 'Review Test Results' activity occurs with the condition that 'Plan Effectiveness < 80.0,' it must be immediately followed by an 'Implement Improvements' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between, where the correlation condition is 'Improvement Status is Pending'. This must occur within a time frame of 0 to 48 hours. This establishes a causal link between analyzing test outcomes and applying changes to strengthen the continuity plan, ensuring a cohesive progression within the model. The 'Implement Improvements' activity is allowed to happen isolated.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraints**
If a 'Monitor Risks' activity occurs with the condition that 'Monitoring Frequency <= 7' in a process instance, then an 'Identify Risks' activity must also occur in the same process instance, and vice versa. This establishes mutual dependency through conjunction, ensuring that ongoing surveillance of identified risks and initial risk pinpointing occur together within the business continuity management process flow.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraints**
If a 'Communicate Plan' activity occurs with the condition that 'Communication Method is Meeting' in a process instance, then a 'Conduct Training' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Communicate Plan' event. This establishes dependency where disseminating the continuity plan via meeting necessitates educating staff on the plan and their roles, enhancing coherence in the process. Multiple occurrences of 'Communicate Plan' are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Conduct Training'.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraints**
An 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity with the condition that 'Incident Severity is High' cannot be immediately followed by an 'Evaluate Incident Response' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, an 'Evaluate Incident Response' event cannot be immediately preceded by an 'Activate Continuity Plan' event with that condition. This contrast prevents direct succession within a time frame of 10 to 15 seconds, thereby refining the process model by excluding specific immediate sequences between triggering the continuity plan and assessing its performance after an incident.
