#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Identify Risks**: The 'Identify Risks' activity represents the process of identifying potential risks that could impact business continuity.
- **Develop Continuity Plan**: The 'Develop Continuity Plan' activity involves creating a comprehensive continuity plan to address identified risks and ensure operational resilience.
- **Test Continuity Plan**: The 'Test Continuity Plan' activity entails executing tests on the developed continuity plan to evaluate its effectiveness and readiness.
- **Review Test Results**: The 'Review Test Results' activity involves analyzing the outcomes of continuity plan tests to assess performance and identify areas for improvement.
- **Implement Improvements**: The 'Implement Improvements' activity consists of applying corrective actions and enhancements to the continuity plan based on test result reviews.
- **Communicate Plan**: The 'Communicate Plan' activity involves disseminating the continuity plan to relevant stakeholders through appropriate communication channels.
- **Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity entails continuously tracking and observing identified risks to detect changes in their severity or likelihood.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity involves providing training sessions to personnel on the continuity plan procedures and their roles during an incident.
- **Activate Continuity Plan**: The 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity represents the execution of the continuity plan in response to an actual incident or disruption.
- **Evaluate Incident Response**: The 'Evaluate Incident Response' activity involves assessing the effectiveness of the activated continuity plan and the overall response to the incident.

## Attributes
- **Risk Level: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Risk Level: integer between 1 and 10' represents the severity level assigned to an identified risk on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Plan Effectiveness: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Plan Effectiveness: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the measured effectiveness score of the continuity plan expressed as a percentage.
- **Test Outcome: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Test Outcome: Pass, Fail' specifies the possible results of a continuity plan test, indicating whether the test was successful or unsuccessful.
- **Improvement Status: Completed, Pending**: The attribute 'Improvement Status: Completed, Pending' indicates whether an improvement action has been completed or is still pending.
- **Communication Method: Email, Meeting, Newsletter**: The attribute 'Communication Method: Email, Meeting, Newsletter' specifies the available channels through which the continuity plan can be communicated to stakeholders.
- **Training Hours: integer between 1 and 40**: The attribute 'Training Hours: integer between 1 and 40' represents the number of hours dedicated to conducting training sessions on the continuity plan.
- **Incident Severity: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Incident Severity: Low, Medium, High' classifies the severity level of an incident triggering the activation of the continuity plan.
- **Response Time: float between 0.0 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Response Time: float between 0.0 and 24.0' represents the time taken to respond to an incident, measured in hours up to 24.
- **Stakeholder Engagement: High, Moderate, Low**: The attribute 'Stakeholder Engagement: High, Moderate, Low' indicates the level of engagement of stakeholders during the development of the continuity plan.
- **Monitoring Frequency: integer between 1 and 30**: The attribute 'Monitoring Frequency: integer between 1 and 30' represents how often risks are monitored, expressed as the number of times per period within a range of 1 to 30.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the business continuity management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Identify Risks,' which involves identifying potential risks that could impact business continuity. This activity must be the first to occur in the process and is associated with the 'Risk Level' attribute, specifying that a severity level must be assigned to each identified risk on a scale from 1 to 10.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Evaluate Incident Response,' which involves assessing the effectiveness of the activated continuity plan and the overall response to the incident. This activity must be the last to occur in the process and is associated with the 'Response Time' attribute, specifying that the response time to the incident must be assessed and recorded, measured in hours up to 24.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Conduct Training' activity must occur at least once in the process, with the activation condition that the training hours are greater than or equal to 8. This ensures that personnel receive adequate training on the continuity plan procedures and their roles during an incident, adding depth and reliability to the process by guaranteeing sufficient training duration.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraint**
The 'Identify Risks' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance, with the activation condition that the incident severity is in the set (Medium, High). This sets up an initial context where the identification of risks is guided by the prior activation of the continuity plan in response to a medium or high severity incident, ensuring that risk identification is contextualized within an active incident scenario. Multiple 'Activate Continuity Plan' events can occur, and any number of events can happen between an 'Activate Continuity Plan' and a subsequent 'Identify Risks.'

#### **Response Constraint**
If a 'Develop Continuity Plan' activity occurs with the activation condition that the stakeholder engagement is 'High,' then a 'Communicate Plan' activity must eventually occur after the 'Develop Continuity Plan' event at some point in the same process instance. The correlation condition requires that the communication method of the 'Communicate Plan' event is in the set (Email, Meeting). Additionally, the time condition requires that the 'Communicate Plan' activity must be triggered between 5 and 13 minutes after the 'Develop Continuity Plan' event. This defines an ordered flow where the communication of the plan follows its development, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure in the model. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Develop Continuity Plan' events can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Communicate Plan' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Develop Continuity Plan' events. If 'Develop Continuity Plan' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Communicate Plan.'

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Alternate Response Constraint**
If a 'Test Continuity Plan' activity occurs with the activation condition that the test outcome is 'Pass,' then a 'Review Test Results' activity must occur after that 'Test Continuity Plan' event and before any subsequent 'Test Continuity Plan' occurs, with no other occurrence of 'Test Continuity Plan' allowed between a 'Test Continuity Plan' and its corresponding 'Review Test Results.' Additionally, the time condition requires that the 'Review Test Results' activity must be triggered between 0 and 2 days after the 'Test Continuity Plan' event. This promotes balance by ensuring each occurrence of a successful test is followed by a unique review of test results, with no intervening test executions, supporting clear one-to-one activity pairing between testing and reviewing.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraint**
Whenever a 'Review Test Results' activity occurs with the activation condition that the plan effectiveness is less than 80.0, it must be immediately followed by an 'Implement Improvements' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The correlation condition requires that the improvement status of the 'Implement Improvements' event is 'Pending.' Additionally, the time condition requires that the 'Implement Improvements' activity must be triggered within at most 48 hours after the 'Review Test Results' event. This captures an immediate cause-and-effect relationship where each review of test results revealing insufficient plan effectiveness is directly followed by the implementation of improvements, ensuring that corrective actions are promptly initiated.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraint**
If a 'Monitor Risks' activity occurs with the activation condition that the monitoring frequency is less than or equal to 7, then an 'Identify Risks' activity must also occur in the same process instance, and vice versa. This mutual obligation ensures that neither event can occur without the other, establishing a mutual dependency between continuous risk monitoring and risk identification. The events can occur multiple times and in any order relative to each other. If neither event occurs, the constraint is still considered satisfied.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraint**
If a 'Communicate Plan' activity occurs in a process instance with the activation condition that the communication method is 'Meeting,' then a 'Conduct Training' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Communicate Plan' event. This establishes a dependency where the communication of the plan through a meeting necessitates the presence of training, enhancing coherence in the process by ensuring that stakeholder communication through meetings is complemented by proper training. If 'Communicate Plan' does not occur, there is no requirement for 'Conduct Training' to occur. Multiple occurrences of 'Communicate Plan' are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Conduct Training,' and multiple 'Conduct Training' instances are allowed.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraint**
An 'Activate Continuity Plan' activity with the activation condition that the incident severity is 'High' cannot be immediately followed by an 'Evaluate Incident Response' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, an 'Evaluate Incident Response' event cannot be immediately preceded by an 'Activate Continuity Plan' event. This constraint allows multiple occurrences of 'Activate Continuity Plan' and 'Evaluate Incident Response' in any order, provided they are not adjacent in the event sequence. Additionally, the time condition requires that the constraint is evaluated within a window of 10 to 15 seconds. This prevents direct succession between the activation of the continuity plan for high-severity incidents and the evaluation of the incident response, thereby refining the process model by excluding specific immediate sequences and ensuring that sufficient time and intervening activities separate these critical events.
