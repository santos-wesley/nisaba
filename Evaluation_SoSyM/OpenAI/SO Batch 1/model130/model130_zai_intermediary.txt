## Activities
- **Draft Contract**: The 'Draft Contract' activity represents the initial phase of creating a contract document.
- **Review Contract**: The 'Review Contract' activity involves examining a drafted contract to assess its content and compliance.
- **Negotiate Terms**: The 'Negotiate Terms' activity entails discussing and adjusting the conditions of the contract between involved parties.
- **Approve Contract**: The 'Approve Contract' activity represents the formal authorization of a contract after review and negotiation.
- **Sign Contract**: The 'Sign Contract' activity involves the formal signing of the contract by the relevant parties.
- **Archive Contract**: The 'Archive Contract' activity represents the final step of storing the completed contract for record-keeping.

## Attributes
- **Contract ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Contract ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each contract.
- **Contract Value: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Contract Value: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0' represents the monetary value of the contract.
- **Approval Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Approval Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected' specifies the possible approval states of the contract.
- **Signature Status: Unsigned, Partially Signed, Fully Signed**: The attribute 'Signature Status: Unsigned, Partially Signed, Fully Signed' specifies the signing progress of the contract.
- **Review Outcome: Accepted, Requires Changes, Rejected**: The attribute 'Review Outcome: Accepted, Requires Changes, Rejected' specifies the possible outcomes of the contract review process.

## Binds
- **Draft Contract: Contract ID, Contract Value**: The bind 'Draft Contract: Contract ID, Contract Value' specifies that the activity 'Draft Contract' requires the attributes 'Contract ID' and 'Contract Value'.
- **Review Contract: Review Outcome**: The bind 'Review Contract: Review Outcome' links the 'Review Contract' activity with the attribute 'Review Outcome'.
