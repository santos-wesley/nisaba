#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Draft Contract**: The 'Draft Contract' activity represents the initial phase of creating a contract document.
- **Review Contract**: The 'Review Contract' activity involves examining a drafted contract to assess its content and compliance.
- **Negotiate Terms**: The 'Negotiate Terms' activity entails discussing and adjusting the conditions of the contract between involved parties.
- **Approve Contract**: The 'Approve Contract' activity represents the formal authorization of a contract after review and negotiation.
- **Sign Contract**: The 'Sign Contract' activity involves the formal signing of the contract by the relevant parties.
- **Archive Contract**: The 'Archive Contract' activity represents the final step of storing the completed contract for record-keeping.

## Attributes
- **Contract ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Contract ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each contract.
- **Contract Value: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Contract Value: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0' represents the monetary value of the contract.
- **Approval Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Approval Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected' specifies the possible approval states of the contract.
- **Signature Status: Unsigned, Partially Signed, Fully Signed**: The attribute 'Signature Status: Unsigned, Partially Signed, Fully Signed' specifies the signing progress of the contract.
- **Review Outcome: Accepted, Requires Changes, Rejected**: The attribute 'Review Outcome: Accepted, Requires Changes, Rejected' specifies the possible outcomes of the contract review process.

## Binds
- **Draft Contract: Contract ID, Contract Value**: The bind 'Draft Contract: Contract ID, Contract Value' specifies that the activity 'Draft Contract' requires the attributes 'Contract ID' and 'Contract Value'.
- **Review Contract: Review Outcome**: The bind 'Review Contract: Review Outcome' links the 'Review Contract' activity with the attribute 'Review Outcome'.
- **Approve Contract: Approval Status**: The bind 'Approve Contract: Approval Status' links the 'Approve Contract' activity with the attribute 'Approval Status'.
- **Sign Contract: Signature Status**: The bind 'Sign Contract: Signature Status' links the 'Sign Contract' activity with the attribute 'Signature Status'.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the contract management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Draft Contract,' which represents the initial phase of creating a contract document. This activity requires the assignment of a 'Contract ID' (an integer between 1000 and 9999) and a 'Contract Value' (a float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0), establishing the initial context and foundational data for the entire contract lifecycle.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Archive Contract,' which represents the final step of storing the completed contract for record-keeping. This activity must be the last to occur in the process, ensuring that all necessary steps have been completed and the contract is properly preserved for future reference.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Review Contract' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that the drafted contract is examined to assess its content and compliance, adding depth and reliability to the process by guaranteeing that every contract undergoes a formal review.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The 'Approve Contract' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Sign Contract' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This constraint applies when the activation condition 'Signature Status is Unsigned' is met, and the correlation condition 'Approval Status is Approved' is satisfied. Additionally, the 'Approve Contract' activity must occur within 0 to 1 day after the 'Sign Contract' event. This sets up an initial context where the signing of the contract logically guides the sequence toward formal authorization, ensuring that approval follows a prior signing event under the specified conditions.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Draft Contract' activity occurs, then a 'Review Contract' activity must eventually occur after the 'Draft Contract' event at some point in the same process instance. The 'Review Contract' activity must occur within 0 to 2 days after the 'Draft Contract' event. This defines an ordered flow where the initial drafting of the contract is followed by a timely review, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure that maintains the process's momentum.

2. If a 'Review Contract' activity occurs with the activation condition 'Review Outcome is Requires Changes,' then a 'Negotiate Terms' activity must eventually occur after the 'Review Contract' event. The 'Negotiate Terms' activity must occur within 0 to 3 days after the 'Review Contract' event. This ensures that when a review identifies the need for changes, the involved parties promptly engage in discussing and adjusting the conditions of the contract, maintaining a coherent progression through the negotiation phase.

3. If a 'Negotiate Terms' activity occurs, then an 'Approve Contract' activity must eventually occur after the 'Negotiate Terms' event. The 'Approve Contract' activity must occur within 0 to 2 days after the 'Negotiate Terms' event. This establishes a sequential flow from negotiation to formal authorization, ensuring that once terms have been discussed and adjusted, the contract proceeds to approval in a timely manner.

4. If an 'Approve Contract' activity occurs with the activation condition 'Approval Status is Approved' and the correlation condition 'Signature Status is Unsigned,' then a 'Sign Contract' activity must eventually occur after the 'Approve Contract' event. The 'Sign Contract' activity must occur within 0 to 1 day after the 'Approve Contract' event. This ensures that once a contract has been formally approved, it is promptly signed by the relevant parties, maintaining the procedural flow from authorization to execution.

5. If a 'Sign Contract' activity occurs with the activation condition 'Signature Status is Fully Signed,' then an 'Archive Contract' activity must eventually occur after the 'Sign Contract' event. This ensures that once the contract has been fully signed by all relevant parties, it is subsequently archived for record-keeping, completing the lifecycle of the contract in a structured and coherent manner.

### **Negative Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Precedence Constraints**
A 'Review Contract' activity cannot be preceded by a 'Sign Contract' activity within the same process instance. This means that if any 'Sign Contract' event has occurred at any point before a 'Review Contract' event, then 'Review Contract' is disallowed. This constraint applies when the activation condition 'Signature Status is Unsigned' is met and the correlation condition 'Review Outcome is Rejected' is satisfied. While multiple instances of 'Review Contract' and 'Sign Contract' can occur, all instances of 'Review Contract' must occur before any instance of 'Sign Contract.' This restriction preserves the intended sequence within the model by ensuring that the review process is not revisited after the contract has already moved to the signing phase, thereby maintaining the integrity of the process's defined flow.
