## Activities
- **Draft Contract**: The 'Draft Contract' activity represents the initial creation and outlining of the contract terms and conditions.
- **Review Contract**: The 'Review Contract' activity involves examining the drafted contract for accuracy, completeness, and compliance.
- **Negotiate Terms**: The 'Negotiate Terms' activity includes discussions and adjustments to the contract terms between involved parties.
- **Approve Contract**: The 'Approve Contract' activity signifies the formal endorsement of the contract by authorized personnel.
- **Sign Contract**: The 'Sign Contract' activity entails the execution of the contract through signatures from relevant parties.
- **Archive Contract**: The 'Archive Contract' activity involves storing the finalized and signed contract for record-keeping and future reference.

## Attributes
- **Contract ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Contract ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' represents a unique numerical identifier assigned to each contract within the specified range for tracking and reference purposes.
- **Contract Value: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Contract Value: float between 10000.0 and 1000000.0' indicates the monetary value associated with the contract, ranging from 10000.0 to 1000000.0, reflecting the financial scope of the agreement.
- **Approval Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Approval Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected' specifies the current state of the contract's approval process, which can be Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
- **Signature Status: Unsigned, Partially Signed, Fully Signed**: The attribute 'Signature Status: Unsigned, Partially Signed, Fully Signed' denotes the signing progress of the contract, possible values being Unsigned, Partially Signed, or Fully Signed.
- **Review Outcome: Accepted, Requires Changes, Rejected**: The attribute 'Review Outcome: Accepted, Requires Changes, Rejected' captures the result of the contract review, with possible outcomes of Accepted, Requires Changes, or Rejected.

## Binds
- **bind Draft Contract: Contract ID, Contract Value**: The bind 'Draft Contract: Contract ID, Contract Value' specifies that the activity 'Draft Contract' is associated with the attributes 'Contract ID' and 'Contract Value'.
