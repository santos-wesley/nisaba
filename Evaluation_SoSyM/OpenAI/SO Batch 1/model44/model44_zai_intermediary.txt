## Activities
- **Report Incident**: The 'Report Incident' activity represents the initial step of logging a new incident into the system.
- **Assess Impact**: The 'Assess Impact' activity involves evaluating the severity and consequences of the reported incident.
- **Prioritize Incident**: The 'Prioritize Incident' activity assigns a priority level to the incident based on its assessed impact.
- **Assign Incident**: The 'Assign Incident' activity designates a responsible team to handle the incident.
- **Investigate Incident**: The 'Investigate Incident' activity entails examining the incident to identify its root cause and potential solutions.
- **Resolve Incident**: The 'Resolve Incident' activity involves implementing a fix or solution to address the incident.
- **Verify Resolution**: The 'Verify Resolution' activity confirms whether the applied resolution has successfully resolved the incident.
- **Close Incident**: The 'Close Incident' activity formally marks the incident as closed after verification is complete.
- **Escalate Incident**: The 'Escalate Incident' activity raises the incident to a higher level of support or management when resolution efforts are insufficient.
- **Document Incident**: The 'Document Incident' activity records all relevant details and actions taken regarding the incident for future reference.

## Attributes
- **Impact Level: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Impact Level: integer between 1 and 5' represents the severity level of the incident's impact, ranging from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
- **Priority: Low, Medium, High, Critical**: The attribute 'Priority: Low, Medium, High, Critical' specifies the possible priority levels assigned to an incident.
- **Resolution Time: float between 0.1 and 72.0**: The attribute 'Resolution Time: float between 0.1 and 72.0' represents the time taken to resolve the incident, measured in hours.
- **Status: New, In Progress, Resolved, Closed**: The attribute 'Status: New, In Progress, Resolved, Closed' specifies the possible states of an incident throughout its lifecycle.
- **Assigned Team ID: integer between 1 and 100**: The attribute 'Assigned Team ID: integer between 1 and 100' represents the unique identifier of the team assigned to handle the incident.
- **Verification Result: Successful, Failed**: The attribute 'Verification Result: Successful, Failed' specifies the outcome of verifying whether the incident resolution was successful.
- **Investigation Time: float between 0.5 and 48.0**: The attribute 'Investigation Time: float between 0.5 and 48.0' represents the time spent investigating the incident, measured in hours.
- **Escalation Level: None, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3**: The attribute 'Escalation Level: None, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3' specifies the escalation tier assigned to the incident when it is escalated.
- **Document ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Document ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identifier assigned to the documentation record of the incident.
- **Assessment Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Assessment Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0' represents the time taken to assess the impact of the incident, measured in hours.
