**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Incident Management Process**

This model describes a structured **incident management lifecycle** with both control-flow constraints and data-aware (multi-perspective) conditions.

### Process Overview

The process begins when an **incident is reported** and must eventually reach a **closed** state. It follows a typical IT incident handling flow with optional escalation and mandatory documentation at the end.

### Activities and Data Attributes

- **Report Incident** → captures initial `Status`
- **Assess Impact** → records `Impact Level` (1–5) and `Assessment Time`
- **Prioritize Incident** → assigns `Priority` (Low, Medium, High, Critical)
- **Assign Incident** → assigns an `Assigned Team ID` (1–100)
- **Investigate Incident** → records `Investigation Time`
- **Resolve Incident** → records `Resolution Time`
- **Verify Resolution** → records `Verification Result` (Successful/Failed)
- **Close Incident** → updates final `Status`
- **Escalate Incident** → records `Escalation Level` (None, Level 1–3)
- **Document Incident** → generates a `Document ID` (1000–9999)

### Declarative Constraints

#### **Control-Flow Constraints**

- The process **must start** with `Report Incident`.
- The process **must end** with `Close Incident`.
- `Report Incident` must be **followed eventually** by `Assess Impact` (within 24 hours).
- `Assess Impact` must be **directly followed** by `Prioritize Incident`.
- `Resolve Incident` must be **directly preceded** by `Investigate Incident`.
- `Resolve Incident` must be **followed** by `Verify Resolution` (within 48 hours).
- `Close Incident` must be **directly followed** by `Document Incident`.
- `Escalate Incident` can occur **at most once** (Absence2).

#### **Data-Aware & Conditional Constraints**

- **Precedence[Assign Incident, Prioritize Incident]** with condition:  
  → `Assign Incident` can only occur **before** `Prioritize Incident` **if** the priority is **High or Critical**. This implies that high/critical incidents may require early assignment.

- **Response[Verify Resolution, Escalate Incident]** with condition:  
  → If `Verification Result` is **Failed**, then `Escalate Incident` must eventually occur.

### Summary

This is a **data-aware declarative incident management model** that:
- Enforces a core sequence (Report → Assess → Prioritize → Investigate → Resolve → Verify → Close → Document)
- Allows flexibility in assignment and escalation
- Uses data conditions (especially Priority and Verification Result) to dynamically influence the required path
- Imposes strict time windows on initial assessment and post-resolution verification
- Ensures every incident is properly documented at the end and that escalation is not overused

The model balances structured compliance with the flexibility needed in real-world incident handling.
