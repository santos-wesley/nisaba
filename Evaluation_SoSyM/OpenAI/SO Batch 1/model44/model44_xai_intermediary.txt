## Activities
- **Report Incident**: The 'Report Incident' activity represents the initial reporting of an incident in the process.
- **Assess Impact**: The 'Assess Impact' activity involves evaluating the impact level and assessment time of the incident.
- **Prioritize Incident**: The 'Prioritize Incident' activity includes assigning a priority to the incident based on its assessment.
- **Assign Incident**: The 'Assign Incident' activity entails assigning the incident to a specific team using the assigned team ID.
- **Investigate Incident**: The 'Investigate Incident' activity covers the investigation phase, recording the investigation time.
- **Resolve Incident**: The 'Resolve Incident' activity focuses on resolving the incident and noting the resolution time.
- **Verify Resolution**: The 'Verify Resolution' activity involves verifying if the resolution was successful or failed.
- **Close Incident**: The 'Close Incident' activity represents the final closure of the incident, updating its status.
- **Escalate Incident**: The 'Escalate Incident' activity is for escalating the incident to a higher level when necessary.
- **Document Incident**: The 'Document Incident' activity includes documenting the incident with a document ID.

## Attributes
- **Impact Level: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Impact Level: integer between 1 and 5' represents the severity level of the incident's impact, ranging from 1 to 5.
- **Priority: Low, Medium, High, Critical**: The attribute 'Priority: Low, Medium, High, Critical' specifies the priority levels for handling the incident.
- **Resolution Time: float between 0.1 and 72.0**: The attribute 'Resolution Time: float between 0.1 and 72.0' indicates the time taken to resolve the incident, in hours, between 0.1 and 72.0.
- **Status: New, In Progress, Resolved, Closed**: The attribute 'Status: New, In Progress, Resolved, Closed' tracks the current status of the incident throughout the process.
- **Assigned Team ID: integer between 1 and 100**: The attribute 'Assigned Team ID: integer between 1 and 100' identifies the team assigned to handle the incident, with IDs ranging from 1 to 100.
- **Verification Result: Successful, Failed**: The attribute 'Verification Result: Successful, Failed' denotes whether the resolution verification was successful or failed.
- **Investigation Time: float between 0.5 and 48.0**: The attribute 'Investigation Time: float between 0.5 and 48.0' measures the time spent investigating the incident, in hours, between 0.5 and 48.0.
- **Escalation Level: None, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3**: The attribute 'Escalation Level: None, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3' indicates the level to which the incident has been escalated.
- **Document ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Document ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' provides a unique identifier for the documentation of the incident, ranging from 1000 to 9999.
- **Assessment Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Assessment Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0' records the time taken to assess the impact of the incident, in hours, between 0.1 and 24.0.
