## Activities
- **Plan Eco-Friendly Trip**: The 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' activity represents the initial planning phase for organizing an environmentally conscious journey.
- **Book Sustainable Accommodation**: The 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' activity involves reserving lodging options that prioritize environmental sustainability.
- **Choose Green Transportation**: The 'Choose Green Transportation' activity includes selecting eco-friendly modes of travel to minimize carbon footprint.
- **Participate in Local Conservation Efforts**: The 'Participate in Local Conservation Efforts' activity entails engaging in activities that support and preserve the local environment.
- **Purchase Local Products**: The 'Purchase Local Products' activity focuses on buying goods produced locally to support sustainable economies.
- **Attend Cultural Workshops**: The 'Attend Cultural Workshops' activity involves participating in educational sessions about local culture and traditions.
- **Evaluate Environmental Impact**: The 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' activity consists of assessing the overall ecological effects of the trip.
- **Provide Feedback to Hosts**: The 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' activity includes giving evaluations and comments to the accommodation providers.
- **Share Sustainable Practices**: The 'Share Sustainable Practices' activity represents disseminating learned eco-friendly methods with others.

## Attributes
- **Accommodation Type: Eco-Lodge, Green Hotel, Hostel**: The attribute 'Accommodation Type: Eco-Lodge, Green Hotel, Hostel' specifies the different sustainable lodging options available for booking.
- **Transportation Mode: Bicycle, Electric Car, Train**: The attribute 'Transportation Mode: Bicycle, Electric Car, Train' indicates the various green travel options for transportation.
- **Feedback Rating: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor**: The attribute 'Feedback Rating: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor' represents the possible ratings given in feedback to hosts.

## Binds
- **bind Book Sustainable Accommodation: Accommodation Type**: The bind 'Book Sustainable Accommodation: Accommodation Type' specifies that the activity 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' requires the attribute 'Accommodation Type'.
- **bind Choose Green Transportation: Transportation Mode**: The bind 'Choose Green Transportation: Transportation Mode' links the 'Choose Green Transportation' activity with the attribute 'Transportation Mode'.
- **bind Provide Feedback to Hosts: Feedback Rating**: The bind 'Provide Feedback to Hosts: Feedback Rating' links the 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' activity with the attribute 'Feedback Rating'.

## Constraints
- **Init[Plan Eco-Friendly Trip] | |**: The 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **End[Share Sustainable Practices] | |**: The 'Share Sustainable Practices' must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Chain Response[Book Sustainable Accommodation, Provide Feedback to Hosts] | | |0,2,d**: Whenever a 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession ensures a direct and uninterrupted transition from 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' to 'Provide Feedback to Hosts'. Multiple 'Book Sustainable Accommodation'-'Provide Feedback to Hosts' pairs can exist in a process instance, each adhering to this immediate succession rule. Events of 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' can occur elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' and its corresponding 'Provide Feedback to Hosts'. The time condition 0,2,d must be satisfied.
- **Response[Choose Green Transportation, Evaluate Environmental Impact] | | |**: If a 'Choose Green Transportation' activity occurs, then a 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' activity must eventually occur after the 'Choose Green Transportation' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Choose Green Transportation' can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Choose Green Transportation'. If 'Choose Green Transportation' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Evaluate Environmental Impact'.
- **Co-Existence[Participate in Local Conservation Efforts, Attend Cultural Workshops] | | |**: If 'Participate in Local Conservation Efforts' activity occurs, then 'Attend Cultural Workshops' must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa.
- **Not Chain Response[Plan Eco-Friendly Trip, Purchase Local Products] | | |**: The 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' activity cannot be directly followed by a 'Purchase Local Products' activity, meaning that a 'Purchase Local Products' event cannot occur immediately after a 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' without any intervening events.
- **Exclusive Choice[Book Sustainable Accommodation, Choose Green Transportation] | | |**: Exactly one of the activities 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' or 'Choose Green Transportation' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if only 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' event occurs without any occurrence of 'Choose Green Transportation' event, or only 'Choose Green Transportation' occurs without any occurrence of 'Book Sustainable Accommodation'. The process is non-compliant if both 'Book Sustainable Accommodation' and 'Choose Green Transportation' occur, or if neither occurs. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusivity and a mandatory occurrence of one of the specified activities.
- **Responded Existence[Plan Eco-Friendly Trip, Participate in Local Conservation Efforts] |A.Trip Duration > 7 | |**: If 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' activity occurs in a process instance, then 'Participate in Local Conservation Efforts' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' event. If 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' does not occur, there is no requirement for 'Participate in Local Conservation Efforts' to occur. Multiple occurrences of 'Plan Eco-Friendly Trip' are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Participate in Local Conservation Efforts', and multiple 'Participate in Local Conservation Efforts' instances are allowed. The activation condition A.Trip Duration > 7 must be satisfied.
- **Not Responded Existence[Provide Feedback to Hosts, Evaluate Environmental Impact] |A.Feedback Rating is Poor | |**: If a 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' activity occurs in a process instance, then a 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' activity must not occur anywhere in the same process instance. This means that the occurrence of 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' event prohibits any occurrence of 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' event. However, if 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' does not occur, 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' may occur freely. Multiple occurrences of 'Provide Feedback to Hosts' are allowed, provided 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' does not occur at all in the process instance. The activation condition A.Feedback Rating is Poor must be satisfied.
- **Existence[Evaluate Environmental Impact] | |**: The 'Evaluate Environmental Impact' activity must occur at least once in the process.
