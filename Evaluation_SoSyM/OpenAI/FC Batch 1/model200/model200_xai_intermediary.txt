## Activities
- **Identify Threat**: The 'Identify Threat' activity represents the initial detection and recognition of potential security threats in the system.
- **Collect Data**: The 'Collect Data' activity involves gathering relevant information and logs associated with the identified threat from various sources.
- **Analyze Data**: The 'Analyze Data' activity includes examining the collected data to determine the nature and severity of the threat.
- **Report Threat**: The 'Report Threat' activity entails documenting and communicating the analyzed threat details to relevant stakeholders.
- **Mitigate Threat**: The 'Mitigate Threat' activity focuses on implementing measures to reduce or eliminate the impact of the threat.
- **Monitor Systems**: The 'Monitor Systems' activity involves ongoing surveillance of the systems to ensure the threat has been effectively addressed.
- **Update Threat Intelligence**: The 'Update Threat Intelligence' activity consists of incorporating lessons learned from the threat into the organization's threat intelligence database.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity includes educating staff on the latest threat awareness and response procedures.
- **Perform Incident Response**: The 'Perform Incident Response' activity encompasses the coordinated actions taken to handle and recover from a security incident.

## Attributes
- **Threat ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Threat ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents a unique identifier assigned to each detected threat for tracking purposes.
- **Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware**: The attribute 'Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware' specifies the category of the security threat being addressed.
- **Data Source ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Data Source ID: integer between 1 and 9999' identifies the origin or source from which threat-related data is collected.
- **Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive**: The attribute 'Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive' indicates the outcome of the data analysis regarding the validity of the threat.
- **Report ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Report ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents a unique identifier for the threat report generated.
- **Mitigation Success Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Success Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0' quantifies the effectiveness of the mitigation efforts as a percentage.
- **Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed' reflects the current state of system monitoring post-mitigation.
- **Update ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Update ID: integer between 1 and 999999' serves as a unique identifier for updates made to the threat intelligence.
