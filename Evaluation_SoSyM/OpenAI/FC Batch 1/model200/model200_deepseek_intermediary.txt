## Activities
- **Identify Threat**: The 'Identify Threat' activity represents the initial detection and identification of a potential security threat.
- **Collect Data**: The 'Collect Data' activity involves gathering relevant data and information related to the identified threat.
- **Analyze Data**: The 'Analyze Data' activity includes examining and evaluating the collected data to determine the nature of the threat.
- **Report Threat**: The 'Report Threat' activity involves documenting and communicating the findings about the identified threat.
- **Mitigate Threat**: The 'Mitigate Threat' activity represents the actions taken to neutralize or reduce the impact of the threat.
- **Monitor Systems**: The 'Monitor Systems' activity involves overseeing system operations to ensure the threat has been properly handled.
- **Update Threat Intelligence**: The 'Update Threat Intelligence' activity includes refreshing threat databases and knowledge bases with new threat information.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity involves educating personnel on threat awareness and response procedures.
- **Perform Incident Response**: The 'Perform Incident Response' activity represents executing the formal incident response plan to address a security event.

## Attributes
- **Threat ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Threat ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identifier assigned to each identified threat for tracking purposes.
- **Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware**: The attribute 'Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware' specifies the category of the security threat being handled.
- **Data Source ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Data Source ID: integer between 1 and 9999' represents the identifier of the source from which threat data is collected.
- **Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive**: The attribute 'Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive' indicates the outcome of the data analysis process for the threat.
- **Report ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Report ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identifier assigned to the threat report.
- **Mitigation Success Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Success Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the percentage indicating how successful the threat mitigation efforts were.
- **Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed' indicates whether the system monitoring for the threat is still in progress or has been finished.
- **Update ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Update ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identifier assigned to each threat intelligence update.
