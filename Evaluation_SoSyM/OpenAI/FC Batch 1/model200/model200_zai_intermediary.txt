## Activities
- **Identify Threat**: The 'Identify Threat' activity represents the process of detecting and recognizing potential security threats within the system.
- **Collect Data**: The 'Collect Data' activity involves gathering relevant data associated with identified threats from various sources.
- **Analyze Data**: The 'Analyze Data' activity entails examining and evaluating the collected data to determine the nature and validity of the threat.
- **Report Threat**: The 'Report Threat' activity involves formally documenting and communicating the details of a confirmed threat to relevant stakeholders.
- **Mitigate Threat**: The 'Mitigate Threat' activity represents the actions taken to reduce, contain, or eliminate the impact of an identified threat.
- **Monitor Systems**: The 'Monitor Systems' activity involves continuously observing system activity to detect any further signs of threat or suspicious behavior.
- **Update Threat Intelligence**: The 'Update Threat Intelligence' activity involves refreshing and enhancing the threat intelligence database with new information gathered from recent incidents.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity represents the organization and delivery of training sessions to personnel on threat awareness and response procedures.
- **Perform Incident Response**: The 'Perform Incident Response' activity involves executing the coordinated response plan to address and resolve a security incident.

## Attributes
- **Threat ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Threat ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each detected threat.
- **Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware**: The attribute 'Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware' specifies the possible categories of security threats that can be identified within the system.
- **Data Source ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Data Source ID: integer between 1 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each data source used during threat data collection.
- **Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive**: The attribute 'Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive' specifies the possible outcomes of the data analysis process, indicating whether a threat is confirmed, a false alarm, or undetermined.
- **Report ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Report ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each generated threat report.
- **Mitigation Success Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Success Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the effectiveness percentage of the mitigation actions taken against a threat.
- **Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed' specifies the current state of the system monitoring activity, indicating whether it is still in progress or has been completed.
- **Update ID: integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Update ID: integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number assigned to each threat intelligence update.

## Constraints
- **Init[Identify Threat] | |**: The 'Identify Threat' activity must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **Chain Response[Identify Threat, Collect Data] | | |0,10,m**: Whenever an 'Identify Threat' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Collect Data' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession must occur within 0 to 10 minutes. The 'Collect Data' activity is allowed to happen elsewhere in the process but not between an 'Identify Threat' and its corresponding 'Collect Data'.
- **Chain Response[Collect Data, Analyze Data] | | |0,30,m**: Whenever a 'Collect Data' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by an 'Analyze Data' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession must occur within 0 to 30 minutes. The 'Analyze Data' activity is allowed to happen elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Collect Data' and its corresponding 'Analyze Data'.
- **Chain Response[Analyze Data, Report Threat] |A.Analysis Result is Confirmed | |0,1,h**: Whenever an 'Analyze Data' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'Analysis Result' is 'Confirmed', it must be immediately followed by a 'Report Threat' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession must occur within 0 to 1 hour. The 'Report Threat' activity is allowed to happen elsewhere in the process but not between an 'Analyze Data' and its corresponding 'Report Threat'.
- **Chain Response[Report Threat, Mitigate Threat] | | |0,2,h**: Whenever a 'Report Threat' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Mitigate Threat' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession must occur within 0 to 2 hours. The 'Mitigate Threat' activity is allowed to happen elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Report Threat' and its corresponding 'Mitigate Threat'.
- **Chain Response[Mitigate Threat, Monitor Systems] | | |**: Whenever a 'Mitigate Threat' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Monitor Systems' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The 'Monitor Systems' activity is allowed to happen elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Mitigate Threat' and its corresponding 'Monitor Systems'.
- **End[Monitor Systems] | |**: The 'Monitor Systems' activity must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Chain Response[Mitigate Threat, Update Threat Intelligence] |A.Mitigation Success Rate > 90.0 | |**: Whenever a 'Mitigate Threat' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'Mitigation Success Rate' is greater than 90.0, it must be immediately followed by an 'Update Threat Intelligence' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The 'Update Threat Intelligence' activity is allowed to happen elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Mitigate Threat' and its corresponding 'Update Threat Intelligence'.
