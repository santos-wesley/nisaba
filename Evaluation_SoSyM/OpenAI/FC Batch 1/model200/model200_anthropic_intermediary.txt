## Activities
- **Identify Threat**: The 'Identify Threat' activity represents the identification of a potential security threat.
- **Collect Data**: The 'Collect Data' activity involves gathering relevant data related to a identified threat.
- **Analyze Data**: The 'Analyze Data' activity includes analyzing the collected data to determine the nature and validity of the threat.
- **Report Threat**: The 'Report Threat' activity represents the reporting of a confirmed threat to relevant stakeholders.
- **Mitigate Threat**: The 'Mitigate Threat' activity involves taking actions to reduce or eliminate the impact of the identified threat.
- **Monitor Systems**: The 'Monitor Systems' activity includes continuously observing systems to detect ongoing or new threats.
- **Update Threat Intelligence**: The 'Update Threat Intelligence' activity represents updating the threat intelligence database with new information about the threat.
- **Conduct Training**: The 'Conduct Training' activity involves providing training sessions to staff regarding threat awareness and response.
- **Perform Incident Response**: The 'Perform Incident Response' activity represents executing the necessary steps to respond to a security incident.

## Attributes
- **Threat ID: Integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Threat ID: Integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number for a threat.
- **Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware**: The attribute 'Threat Type: Phishing, Malware, DDoS, Ransomware' specifies the category of the identified threat.
- **Data Source ID: Integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Data Source ID: Integer between 1 and 9999' represents the unique identification number of the data source used for data collection.
- **Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive**: The attribute 'Analysis Result: Confirmed, False Positive, Inconclusive' specifies the outcome of the data analysis for a threat.
- **Report ID: Integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Report ID: Integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number assigned to a threat report.
- **Mitigation Success Rate: Float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Success Rate: Float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the percentage of success achieved in mitigating a threat.
- **Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Monitoring Status: Ongoing, Completed' specifies the current state of the system monitoring process.
- **Update ID: Integer between 1 and 999999**: The attribute 'Update ID: Integer between 1 and 999999' represents the unique identification number for a threat intelligence update.

The 'Collect Data' activity must occur between 0 and 10 minutes after the 'Identify Threat' activity. The 'Collect Data' activity is allowed to happen isolated.
