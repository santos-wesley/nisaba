## Activities
- **Identify Needs**: The 'Identify Needs' activity represents the initial phase where the organization determines its requirements for goods or services.
- **Select Vendor**: The 'Select Vendor' activity involves choosing an appropriate vendor based on the identified needs.
- **Negotiate Contract**: The 'Negotiate Contract' activity includes discussing and agreeing on the terms of the contract with the selected vendor.
- **Sign Contract**: The 'Sign Contract' activity formalizes the agreement by signing the contract with the vendor.
- **Monitor Performance**: The 'Monitor Performance' activity entails ongoing assessment of the vendor's performance against agreed standards.
- **Review Compliance**: The 'Review Compliance' activity involves evaluating whether the vendor adheres to compliance requirements.
- **Issue Payment**: The 'Issue Payment' activity is the process of releasing payment to the vendor upon satisfactory performance.
- **Conduct Review**: The 'Conduct Review' activity includes performing a final review of the vendor's overall performance and outcomes.

## Attributes
- **Vendor ID: integer between 1 and 9999**: The attribute 'Vendor ID: integer between 1 and 9999' represents a unique numerical identifier assigned to each vendor, ranging from 1 to 9999.
- **Vendor Name: Vendor A, Vendor B, Vendor C**: The attribute 'Vendor Name: Vendor A, Vendor B, Vendor C' specifies the possible names of the vendors that can be selected, limited to Vendor A, Vendor B, or Vendor C.
- **Contract Amount: integer between 1000 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Contract Amount: integer between 1000 and 1000000' indicates the monetary value of the contract, which must be an integer value between 1000 and 1000000.
- **KPI Score: float between 0.0 and 10.0**: The attribute 'KPI Score: float between 0.0 and 10.0' measures the vendor's performance using a floating-point score ranging from 0.0 to 10.0.
- **Compliance Status: Compliant, Non-Compliant**: The attribute 'Compliance Status: Compliant, Non-Compliant' denotes whether the vendor meets compliance standards, with possible values of Compliant or Non-Compliant.
- **Review Outcome: Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory**: The attribute 'Review Outcome: Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory' represents the result of the review, which can be either Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory.
- **Payment Status: Pending, Completed**: The attribute 'Payment Status: Pending, Completed' indicates the current state of the payment, which can be Pending or Completed.
