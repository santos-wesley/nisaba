## Activities
- **Submit Request**: The 'Submit Request' activity represents the action of an employee submitting a request, such as for remote work or flexible hours.
- **Review Request**: The 'Review Request' activity involves a manager reviewing a submitted request to determine its approval or rejection.
- **Approve Request**: The 'Approve Request' activity represents the action of a manager formally approving a submitted request.
- **Reject Request**: The 'Reject Request' activity represents the action of a manager formally rejecting a submitted request.
- **Setup Remote Access**: The 'Setup Remote Access' activity involves configuring remote access for an employee whose request has been approved.
- **Update Payroll**: The 'Update Payroll' activity represents the action of updating the payroll system to reflect changes related to an approved request.
- **Notify Employee**: The 'Notify Employee' activity involves sending a notification to the employee regarding the status or outcome of their request.
- **Monitor Performance**: The 'Monitor Performance' activity represents the action of a manager monitoring and evaluating an employee's performance.
- **Revoke Access**: The 'Revoke Access' activity involves revoking previously granted remote access from an employee.
- **Complete Survey**: The 'Complete Survey' activity represents the action of completing a survey, potentially related to employee satisfaction or process feedback.
