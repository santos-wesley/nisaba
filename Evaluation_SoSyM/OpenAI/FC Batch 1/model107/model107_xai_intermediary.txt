## Activities
- **Define Requirements**: The 'Define Requirements' activity represents the initial phase where the project requirements are clearly outlined and documented.
- **Develop Initial Prototype**: The 'Develop Initial Prototype' activity involves creating the first version of the prototype based on the defined requirements.
- **Test Prototype**: The 'Test Prototype' activity includes conducting tests on the prototype to evaluate its functionality and performance.
- **Gather Feedback**: The 'Gather Feedback' activity focuses on collecting input and opinions from stakeholders regarding the prototype.
- **Analyze Feedback**: The 'Analyze Feedback' activity entails reviewing and interpreting the gathered feedback to identify areas for improvement.
- **Revise Prototype**: The 'Revise Prototype' activity involves making changes and updates to the prototype based on the analyzed feedback.
- **Final Testing**: The 'Final Testing' activity consists of performing comprehensive tests on the revised prototype to ensure it meets all requirements.
- **Document Findings**: The 'Document Findings' activity includes recording all observations, results, and insights from the development and testing phases.
- **Present Prototype**: The 'Present Prototype' activity involves showcasing the final prototype to stakeholders or decision-makers.
- **Approve Prototype**: The 'Approve Prototype' activity represents the formal approval of the prototype, signifying the completion of the development process.

## Attributes
- **Stakeholder Name: John Doe, Jane Smith, Alice Johnson**: The attribute 'Stakeholder Name: John Doe, Jane Smith, Alice Johnson' specifies the possible names of stakeholders providing feedback on the prototype.
- **Feedback Rating: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Feedback Rating: integer between 1 and 10' represents the rating given by stakeholders on the prototype, ranging from 1 to 10.
- **Improvement Cost: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Improvement Cost: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' indicates the estimated cost of revisions or improvements to the prototype, ranging from 100.0 to 10000.0.
- **Test Result: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Test Result: Pass, Fail' specifies the outcome of the prototype testing, which can be either Pass or Fail.
- **Revision Version: integer between 1 and 20**: The attribute 'Revision Version: integer between 1 and 20' represents the version number of the prototype after revisions, ranging from 1 to 20.
