## Activities
- **Define Requirements**: The 'Define Requirements' activity represents the initial phase of identifying and documenting the project requirements.
- **Develop Initial Prototype**: The 'Develop Initial Prototype' activity involves creating the first version of the prototype based on the defined requirements.
- **Test Prototype**: The 'Test Prototype' activity includes conducting tests on the developed prototype to evaluate its functionality and performance.
- **Gather Feedback**: The 'Gather Feedback' activity involves collecting feedback from stakeholders regarding the tested prototype.
- **Analyze Feedback**: The 'Analyze Feedback' activity includes reviewing and interpreting the feedback gathered from stakeholders to identify areas for improvement.
- **Revise Prototype**: The 'Revise Prototype' activity involves modifying and improving the prototype based on the analyzed feedback.
- **Final Testing**: The 'Final Testing' activity includes conducting comprehensive tests on the revised prototype to ensure it meets all requirements.
- **Document Findings**: The 'Document Findings' activity involves recording the results and observations from the final testing phase.
- **Present Prototype**: The 'Present Prototype' activity includes showcasing the finalized prototype to stakeholders for review and approval.
- **Approve Prototype**: The 'Approve Prototype' activity represents the formal acceptance and approval of the prototype by the relevant stakeholders.

## Attributes
- **Stakeholder Name: John Doe, Jane Smith, Alice Johnson**: The attribute 'Stakeholder Name: John Doe, Jane Smith, Alice Johnson' specifies the possible names of stakeholders involved in providing feedback, limited to the values John Doe, Jane Smith, or Alice Johnson.
- **Feedback Rating: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Feedback Rating: integer between 1 and 10' represents the numerical rating assigned by stakeholders to evaluate the prototype, ranging from 1 to 10.
- **Improvement Cost: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Improvement Cost: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the estimated cost associated with implementing improvements to the prototype, ranging from 100.0 to 10000.0.
- **Test Result: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Test Result: Pass, Fail' specifies the possible outcomes of a prototype test, limited to the values Pass or Fail.
- **Revision Version: integer between 1 and 20**: The attribute 'Revision Version: integer between 1 and 20' represents the version number assigned to each revision of the prototype, ranging from 1 to 20.
