## Activities
- **Define Requirements**: The 'Define Requirements' activity represents the process of specifying and documenting the requirements needed for the prototype.
- **Develop Initial Prototype**: The 'Develop Initial Prototype' activity involves creating the first version of the prototype based on the defined requirements.
- **Test Prototype**: The 'Test Prototype' activity includes conducting tests on the prototype to evaluate its functionality and performance.
- **Gather Feedback**: The 'Gather Feedback' activity involves collecting feedback from stakeholders regarding the prototype.
- **Analyze Feedback**: The 'Analyze Feedback' activity includes reviewing and interpreting the feedback collected from stakeholders.
- **Revise Prototype**: The 'Revise Prototype' activity represents making improvements or changes to the prototype based on the analyzed feedback.
- **Final Testing**: The 'Final Testing' activity involves conducting the final round of tests on the revised prototype.
- **Document Findings**: The 'Document Findings' activity includes recording the results and insights obtained throughout the prototype development process.
- **Present Prototype**: The 'Present Prototype' activity involves showcasing the prototype to relevant parties for review.
- **Approve Prototype**: The 'Approve Prototype' activity represents the formal approval of the prototype after review.

## Attributes
- **Stakeholder Name: John Doe, Jane Smith, Alice Johnson**: The attribute 'Stakeholder Name: John Doe, Jane Smith, Alice Johnson' specifies the possible stakeholders providing feedback on the prototype.
- **Feedback Rating: Integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Feedback Rating: Integer between 1 and 10' represents the numeric rating given by a stakeholder as feedback on the prototype.
- **Improvement Cost: Float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Improvement Cost: Float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the monetary cost associated with revising and improving the prototype.
- **Test Result: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Test Result: Pass, Fail' specifies the outcome of a test conducted on the prototype.
- **Revision Version: Integer between 1 and 20**: The attribute 'Revision Version: Integer between 1 and 20' represents the version number assigned to a particular revision of the prototype.
