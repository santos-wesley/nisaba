#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Collect Digital Evidence**: The 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity represents the gathering of digital evidence during an investigation.
- **Preserve Digital Evidence**: The 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity involves safeguarding digital evidence to maintain its original state.
- **Analyze Digital Evidence**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity includes examining digital evidence to extract relevant information.
- **Document Chain Custody**: The 'Document Chain Custody' activity involves recording the chronological chain of custody of the evidence.
- **Secure Evidence Storage**: The 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity represents storing digital evidence in a secure manner.
- **Review Evidence Report**: The 'Review Evidence Report' activity includes examining the report generated from the evidence analysis.
- **Authorize Evidence Release**: The 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity involves granting official permission to release the evidence.
- **Dispose Digital Evidence**: The 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity represents the disposal of digital evidence following proper procedures.
- **Validate Evidence Integrity**: The 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity involves verifying that the evidence has not been tampered with or altered.
- **Generate Evidence Report**: The 'Generate Evidence Report' activity includes creating a formal report documenting the evidence findings.
- **Classify Digital Evidence**: The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity involves categorizing digital evidence based on its type or nature.
- **Index Digital Evidence**: The 'Index Digital Evidence' activity represents organizing digital evidence for easy retrieval and reference.
- **Audit Evidence Access**: The 'Audit Evidence Access' activity involves reviewing and tracking who accessed the evidence and when.
- **Report Security Breach**: The 'Report Security Breach' activity includes notifying relevant parties of any security breach related to the evidence.
- **Update Evidence Metadata**: The 'Update Evidence Metadata' activity involves modifying descriptive information associated with the evidence.

## Attributes
- **Evidence Type: Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File**: The attribute 'Evidence Type: Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File' specifies the different categories of digital evidence that can be collected.
- **Evidence Size: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Evidence Size: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the size of the digital evidence.
- **Custody Status: Collected, Preserved, Analyzed, Released, Disposed**: The attribute 'Custody Status: Collected, Preserved, Analyzed, Released, Disposed' specifies the current stage of the evidence within the chain of custody.
- **Integrity Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Integrity Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents a numeric measure of the evidence's integrity.
- **Security Level: Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified**: The attribute 'Security Level: Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified' specifies the classification level assigned to the evidence.
- **Access Count: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Access Count: integer between 0 and 100' represents the number of times the evidence has been accessed.
- **Legal Hold: Yes, No**: The attribute 'Legal Hold: Yes, No' specifies whether the evidence is subject to a legal hold.
- **Analysis Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Analysis Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0' represents the duration of time spent analyzing the evidence.
- **Retention Period: integer between 30 and 3650**: The attribute 'Retention Period: integer between 30 and 3650' specifies the number of days the evidence must be retained.
- **Disposal Method: Shredding, Wiping, Deletion**: The attribute 'Disposal Method: Shredding, Wiping, Deletion' specifies the technique used to dispose of the digital evidence.

## Binds
- **bind Collect Digital Evidence: Evidence Type, Evidence Size**: The bind 'Collect Digital Evidence: Evidence Type, Evidence Size' specifies that the activity 'Collect Digital Evidence' requires the attributes 'Evidence Type' and 'Evidence Size'.
- **bind Preserve Digital Evidence: Custody Status, Integrity Score**: The bind 'Preserve Digital Evidence: Custody Status, Integrity Score' links the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Custody Status' and 'Integrity Score'.
- **bind Analyze Digital Evidence: Analysis Time, Security Level**: The bind 'Analyze Digital Evidence: Analysis Time, Security Level' links the 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Analysis Time' and 'Security Level'.
- **bind Dispose Digital Evidence: Disposal Method, Retention Period**: The bind 'Dispose Digital Evidence: Disposal Method, Retention Period' links the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Disposal Method' and 'Retention Period'.
- **bind Audit Evidence Access: Access Count, Legal Hold**: The bind 'Audit Evidence Access: Access Count, Legal Hold' links the 'Audit Evidence Access' activity with the attributes 'Access Count' and 'Legal Hold'.

## Constraints
- **Init[Collect Digital Evidence]**: The 'Collect Digital Evidence' must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **End[Dispose Digital Evidence]**: The 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Succession[Collect Digital Evidence, Preserve Digital Evidence]**: A 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must be followed by a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity at some point later in the same process instance. Conversely, a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event can occur only if a 'Collect Digital Evidence' event has occurred before it within the same process instance. This bidirectional and ordered dependency ensures that 'Collect Digital Evidence' impose obligations for future 'Preserve Digital Evidence', and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' cannot occur without prior 'Collect Digital Evidence'. Multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' are allowed, with each 'Collect Digital Evidence' eventually leading to a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' and each 'Preserve Digital Evidence' being justified by a preceding 'Collect Digital Evidence'.
- **Response[Preserve Digital Evidence, Analyze Digital Evidence]**: If a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must eventually occur after the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Preserve Digital Evidence' can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Analyze Digital Evidence' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Preserve Digital Evidence'. If 'Preserve Digital Evidence' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Analyze Digital Evidence'.
- **Precedence[Document Chain Custody, Analyze Digital Evidence]**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Chain Custody' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance.
- **Responded Existence[Audit Evidence Access, Collect Digital Evidence] |A.Legal Hold is Yes**: If an 'Audit Evidence Access' activity occurs in a process instance and the activation condition 'A.Legal Hold is Yes' holds, then a 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Audit Evidence Access' event. If 'Audit Evidence Access' does not occur, or occurs without satisfying the condition, there is no requirement for 'Collect Digital Evidence' to occur. Multiple occurrences of 'Audit Evidence Access' satisfying the condition are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Collect Digital Evidence', and multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' instances are allowed.
- **Chain Response[Analyze Digital Evidence, Generate Evidence Report]**: Whenever an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Generate Evidence Report' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The 'Generate Evidence Report' activity is allowed to happen isolated.
- **Co-Existence[Secure Evidence Storage, Validate Evidence Integrity]**: If 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity occurs, then 'Validate Evidence Integrity' must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa.
- **Not Chain Succession[Report Security Breach, Dispose Digital Evidence]**: A 'Report Security Breach' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event cannot be immediately preceded by a 'Report Security Breach' event. This constraint allows multiple occurrences of 'Report Security Breach' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence' in any order, provided they are not adjacent in the event sequence.
- **Not Co-Existence[Classify Digital Evidence, Dispose Digital Evidence]**: The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity and the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This means that if 'Classify Digital Evidence' event occurs one or more times, 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must not occur at all in that process instance, and vice-versa. It is acceptable for neither event to occur. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusion between 'Classify Digital Evidence' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence', allowing for different process variants based on which event (if any) occurs.
- **Choice[Index Digital Evidence, Update Evidence Metadata]**: At least one of the activities 'Index Digital Evidence' or 'Update Evidence Metadata' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if 'Index Digital Evidence' event occurs, 'Update Evidence Metadata' event occurs, or both occur. There is no restriction on the order or the number of times they occur. However, if neither 'Index Digital Evidence' nor 'Update Evidence Metadata' occurs, the constraint is violated.
- **Exclusive Choice[Authorize Evidence Release, Dispose Digital Evidence]**: Exactly one of the activities 'Authorize Evidence Release' or 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if only 'Authorize Evidence Release' event occurs without any occurrence of 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event, or only 'Dispose Digital Evidence' occurs without any occurrence of 'Authorize Evidence Release'. The process is non-compliant if both 'Authorize Evidence Release' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence' occur, or if neither occurs. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusivity and a mandatory occurrence of one of the specified activities.
- **Existence[Analyze Digital Evidence]**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must occur at least once in the process.
- **Alternate Response[Review Evidence Report, Authorize Evidence Release]**: If a 'Review Evidence Report' activity occurs, then an 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity must occur after that 'Review Evidence Report' event and before any subsequent 'Review Evidence Report' occurs; no other occurrence of 'Review Evidence Report' is allowed between a 'Review Evidence Report' and its corresponding 'Authorize Evidence Release'.
- **Chain Response[Preserve Digital Evidence, Validate Evidence Integrity] |1,2,h**: Whenever a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between, and this succession must occur within a time frame of between 1 and 2 hours. The 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity is allowed to happen isolated.


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the digital forensic evidence management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
Against the backdrop of a forensic investigation, the process begins with 'Collect Digital Evidence,' which involves gathering digital evidence characterized by its 'Evidence Type' and 'Evidence Size.' This establishes the foundational context from which every subsequent activity unfolds.

#### **End Constraints**
Bringing the process to its logical close, the model concludes with 'Dispose Digital Evidence,' which represents the disposal of digital evidence following proper procedures, governed by 'Disposal Method' and 'Retention Period.' This activity must be the final event, providing coherent closure to the entire evidence lifecycle.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
Adding depth to the evidentiary workflow, the 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that, regardless of how the process unfolds, the examination of evidence to extract relevant information is never omitted, reinforcing the reliability of the overall investigation.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraint**
As a consequence of accountability requirements, if an 'Audit Evidence Access' activity occurs under the condition that 'Legal Hold is Yes,' then a 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must also occur somewhere within the same process instance, whether before or after. This dependency ensures that any audited access tied to a legal hold is always anchored to an actual collection of evidence, reinforcing accountability throughout the chain of custody.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraint**
Setting up the necessary groundwork, the 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Chain Custody' activity has occurred beforehand. This establishes that the chain of custody must be documented first, guiding the logical sequence of evidentiary handling before any analysis can take place.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. Following a cohesive temporal flow, if a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must eventually occur afterward, with no restriction on timing or intervening events. This ensures that preserved evidence is always eventually subjected to analysis, even if multiple preservation events accumulate before a single analysis resolves them all.
2. In a similarly ordered fashion, whenever an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Generate Evidence Report' activity in the very next position, with the report generation also permitted to occur independently. This tight temporal coupling ensures that analysis results are captured without delay.

#### **Succession Constraint**
Establishing a causal backbone for the entire process, a 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must always eventually be followed by a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity, and conversely, no 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event may occur without a prior 'Collect Digital Evidence' event. This bidirectional causal link ensures that collection and preservation remain logically and inseparably tied together, with multiple instances of each permitted as long as the causal chain holds.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraint**
Reinforcing interdependence between safeguarding and verification, if 'Secure Evidence Storage' occurs, then 'Validate Evidence Integrity' must also occur within the same process instance, and vice versa. This mutual linkage ensures that whenever evidence is placed into secure storage, its integrity is always correspondingly verified, and neither activity is left to occur in isolation.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Choice Constraint**
Offering flexibility within the organizational phase, at least one of 'Index Digital Evidence' or 'Update Evidence Metadata' must occur in the process instance. Either activity—or both—may satisfy this requirement, allowing alternative paths for organizing and describing the evidence without imposing a strict order or fixed frequency.

#### **Exclusive Choice Constraint**
In contrast, presenting a strictly mutually exclusive pathway, exactly one of 'Authorize Evidence Release' or 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must occur in the process instance. The process is only compliant if release is authorized without disposal, or disposal occurs without release authorization—never both, and never neither—ensuring a clean bifurcation between releasing evidence and disposing of it.

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraint**
Setting a firm boundary between classification and disposal, the 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity and the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. If evidence is classified, it must not be disposed of within that instance, and vice versa, thereby preserving process integrity by preventing these two activities from coexisting.

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Alternate Response Constraint**
Promoting a balanced, one-to-one pairing between review and authorization, if a 'Review Evidence Report' activity occurs, then an 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity must occur afterward and before any subsequent 'Review Evidence Report' takes place. This ensures that each review is distinctly and individually followed by its own authorization, with no overlapping or repeated reviews interrupting the pairing.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraint**
Capturing an immediate cause-and-effect relationship bound by strict timing, whenever a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity in the very next position, and this succession must take place within a window of one to two hours. The 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity may also occur independently, but whenever preservation happens, verification of integrity must follow directly and promptly.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraint**
Preventing any troubling immediate adjacency, a 'Report Security Breach' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity, nor can 'Dispose Digital Evidence' be immediately preceded by 'Report Security Breach.' At least one intervening event must separate these two activities whenever both occur, thereby excluding this specific immediate sequence and preserving the structural integrity of the evidence disposal process.
