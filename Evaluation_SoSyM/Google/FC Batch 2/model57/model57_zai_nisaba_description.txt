#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Collect Digital Evidence**: The 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity represents the process of gathering digital evidence from various sources for investigative purposes.
- **Preserve Digital Evidence**: The 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity involves safeguarding collected digital evidence to maintain its integrity and prevent alteration.
- **Analyze Digital Evidence**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity entails examining and interpreting digital evidence to extract relevant information for an investigation.
- **Document Chain Custody**: The 'Document Chain Custody' activity involves recording the chain of custody to ensure the traceability and accountability of digital evidence handling.
- **Secure Evidence Storage**: The 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity ensures that digital evidence is stored in a secure environment to prevent unauthorized access or tampering.
- **Review Evidence Report**: The 'Review Evidence Report' activity consists of examining the generated evidence report for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with legal standards.
- **Authorize Evidence Release**: The 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity involves granting official permission to release digital evidence to authorized parties.
- **Dispose Digital Evidence**: The 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity represents the secure and permanent disposal of digital evidence once it is no longer needed.
- **Validate Evidence Integrity**: The 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity involves verifying that digital evidence has not been altered or corrupted since its collection.
- **Generate Evidence Report**: The 'Generate Evidence Report' activity entails creating a comprehensive report summarizing the findings from the analysis of digital evidence.
- **Classify Digital Evidence**: The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity involves categorizing digital evidence based on its type, sensitivity, and relevance to the investigation.
- **Index Digital Evidence**: The 'Index Digital Evidence' activity consists of organizing and cataloging digital evidence to facilitate efficient retrieval and reference.
- **Audit Evidence Access**: The 'Audit Evidence Access' activity involves reviewing and recording all access events to digital evidence to ensure accountability and detect unauthorized access.
- **Report Security Breach**: The 'Report Security Breach' activity entails documenting and notifying relevant parties about any security breaches that may compromise digital evidence.
- **Update Evidence Metadata**: The 'Update Evidence Metadata' activity involves modifying or refreshing the metadata associated with digital evidence to reflect current status and attributes.

## Attributes
- **Evidence Type: Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File**: The attribute 'Evidence Type: Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File' specifies the possible types of digital evidence that can be collected and processed.
- **Evidence Size: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Evidence Size: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the size of the digital evidence, measured in a unit ranging from 1 to 1000.
- **Custody Status: Collected, Preserved, Analyzed, Released, Disposed**: The attribute 'Custody Status: Collected, Preserved, Analyzed, Released, Disposed' indicates the current status of the digital evidence within the chain of custody lifecycle.
- **Integrity Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Integrity Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents a numerical measure of the integrity of the digital evidence, ranging from 0.0 (no integrity) to 1.0 (full integrity).
- **Security Level: Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified**: The attribute 'Security Level: Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified' specifies the security classification assigned to the digital evidence during analysis.
- **Access Count: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Access Count: integer between 0 and 100' represents the number of times the digital evidence has been accessed, ranging from 0 to 100.
- **Legal Hold: Yes, No**: The attribute 'Legal Hold: Yes, No' indicates whether the digital evidence is under a legal hold, preventing its disposal or alteration.
- **Analysis Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Analysis Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0' represents the time spent analyzing the digital evidence, measured in hours ranging from 0.1 to 24.0.
- **Retention Period: integer between 30 and 3650**: The attribute 'Retention Period: integer between 30 and 3650' specifies the duration for which the digital evidence must be retained, ranging from 30 to 3650 days.
- **Disposal Method: Shredding, Wiping, Deletion**: The attribute 'Disposal Method: Shredding, Wiping, Deletion' specifies the method used to securely dispose of digital evidence.

## Binds
- **Collect Digital Evidence: Evidence Type, Evidence Size**: The bind 'Collect Digital Evidence: Evidence Type, Evidence Size' links the 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Evidence Type' and 'Evidence Size'.
- **Preserve Digital Evidence: Custody Status, Integrity Score**: The bind 'Preserve Digital Evidence: Custody Status, Integrity Score' links the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Custody Status' and 'Integrity Score'.
- **Analyze Digital Evidence: Analysis Time, Security Level**: The bind 'Analyze Digital Evidence: Analysis Time, Security Level' links the 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Analysis Time' and 'Security Level'.
- **Dispose Digital Evidence: Disposal Method, Retention Period**: The bind 'Dispose Digital Evidence: Disposal Method, Retention Period' links the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Disposal Method' and 'Retention Period'.
- **Audit Evidence Access: Access Count, Legal Hold**: The bind 'Audit Evidence Access: Access Count, Legal Hold' links the 'Audit Evidence Access' activity with the attributes 'Access Count' and 'Legal Hold'.

## Constraints
- **Init[Collect Digital Evidence] | |**: The 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **End[Dispose Digital Evidence] | |**: The 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Succession[Collect Digital Evidence, Preserve Digital Evidence] | | |**: A 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must be followed by a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity at some point later in the same process instance. Conversely, a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event can occur only if a 'Collect Digital Evidence' event has occurred before it within the same process instance. This bidirectional and ordered dependency ensures that 'Collect Digital Evidence' imposes obligations for future 'Preserve Digital Evidence', and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' cannot occur without prior 'Collect Digital Evidence'. Multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' are allowed, with each 'Collect Digital Evidence' eventually leading to a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' and each 'Preserve Digital Evidence' being justified by a preceding 'Collect Digital Evidence'.
- **Response[Preserve Digital Evidence, Analyze Digital Evidence] | | |**: If a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must eventually occur after the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Preserve Digital Evidence' can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Analyze Digital Evidence' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Preserve Digital Evidence'. If 'Preserve Digital Evidence' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Analyze Digital Evidence'.
- **Precedence[Document Chain Custody, Analyze Digital Evidence] | | |**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Chain Custody' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. 'Analyze Digital Evidence' events that occur without any prior 'Document Chain Custody' are violations of the constraint. Multiple 'Document Chain Custody' can occur, and any number of events (including other 'Analyze Digital Evidence') can happen between a 'Document Chain Custody' and a subsequent 'Analyze Digital Evidence'.
- **Responded Existence[Audit Evidence Access, Collect Digital Evidence] |A.Legal Hold is Yes | |**: If 'Audit Evidence Access' activity occurs in a process instance, then 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Audit Evidence Access' event, provided that the activation condition 'A.Legal Hold is Yes' is satisfied (i.e., the 'Legal Hold' attribute of the 'Audit Evidence Access' activity is 'Yes'). If 'Audit Evidence Access' does not occur, there is no requirement for 'Collect Digital Evidence' to occur. Multiple occurrences of 'Audit Evidence Access' are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Collect Digital Evidence', and multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' instances are allowed.
- **Chain Response[Analyze Digital Evidence, Generate Evidence Report] | | |**: Whenever an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Generate Evidence Report' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession ensures a direct and uninterrupted transition from 'Analyze Digital Evidence' to 'Generate Evidence Report'. Multiple 'Analyze Digital Evidence'-'Generate Evidence Report' pairs can exist in a process instance, each adhering to this immediate succession rule. Events of 'Generate Evidence Report' can occur elsewhere in the process but not between an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' and its corresponding 'Generate Evidence Report'.
- **Co-Existence[Secure Evidence Storage, Validate Evidence Integrity] | | |**: If 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity occurs, then 'Validate Evidence Integrity' must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa. This mutual obligation ensures that neither event can occur without the other. The events can occur multiple times and in any order relative to each other. If neither event occurs, the constraint is still considered satisfied.
- **Not Chain Succession[Report Security Breach, Dispose Digital Evidence] | | |**: A 'Report Security Breach' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event cannot be immediately preceded by a 'Report Security Breach' event. This constraint allows multiple occurrences of 'Report Security Breach' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence' in any order, provided they are not adjacent in the event sequence.
- **Not Co-Existence[Classify Digital Evidence, Dispose Digital Evidence] | | |**: The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity and the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This means that if 'Classify Digital Evidence' event occurs one or more times, 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must not occur at all in that process instance, and vice-versa. It is acceptable for neither event to occur. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusion between 'Classify Digital Evidence' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence', allowing for different process variants based on which event (if any) occurs.
- **Choice[Index Digital Evidence, Update Evidence Metadata] | | |**: At least one of the activities 'Index Digital Evidence' or 'Update Evidence Metadata' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if 'Index Digital Evidence' event occurs, 'Update Evidence Metadata' event occurs, or both occur. There is no restriction on the order or the number of times they occur. However, if neither 'Index Digital Evidence' nor 'Update Evidence Metadata' occurs, the constraint is violated.
- **Exclusive Choice[Authorize Evidence Release, Dispose Digital Evidence] | | |**: Exactly one of the activities 'Authorize Evidence Release' or 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if only 'Authorize Evidence Release' event occurs without any occurrence of 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event, or only 'Dispose Digital Evidence' occurs without any occurrence of 'Authorize Evidence Release'. The process is non-compliant if both 'Authorize Evidence Release' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence' occur, or if neither occurs. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusivity and a mandatory occurrence of one of the specified activities.
- **Existence[Analyze Digital Evidence] | |**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must occur at least once in the process.
- **Alternate Response[Review Evidence Report, Authorize Evidence Release] | | |**: If a 'Review Evidence Report' activity occurs, then an 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity must occur after that 'Review Evidence Report' event and before any subsequent 'Review Evidence Report' occurs; no other occurrence of 'Review Evidence Report' is allowed between a 'Review Evidence Report' and its corresponding 'Authorize Evidence Release'. This ensures that each 'Review Evidence Report' is individually responded to by an 'Authorize Evidence Release' in sequence.
- **Chain Response[Preserve Digital Evidence, Validate Evidence Integrity] | | |1,2,h**: Whenever a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession ensures a direct and uninterrupted transition from 'Preserve Digital Evidence' to 'Validate Evidence Integrity'. Additionally, the time condition '1,2,h' requires that the 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity must occur between 1 and 2 hours after the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity. Multiple 'Preserve Digital Evidence'-'Validate Evidence Integrity' pairs can exist in a process instance, each adhering to this immediate succession rule. Events of 'Validate Evidence Integrity' can occur elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' and its corresponding 'Validate Evidence Integrity'.


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the property transaction process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Collect Digital Evidence,' which involves gathering digital evidence from various sources for investigative purposes. This activity is linked to the 'Evidence Type' and 'Evidence Size' attributes, establishing the initial context for the digital evidence management process.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Dispose Digital Evidence,' which represents the secure and permanent disposal of digital evidence once it is no longer needed. This activity must be the last to occur in the process and is associated with the 'Disposal Method' and 'Retention Period' attributes, ensuring that evidence is disposed of in accordance with established retention policies.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that the digital evidence is examined and interpreted to extract relevant information for the investigation, adding depth and reliability to the overall process.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Chain Custody' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This sets up an initial context where the chain of custody must be documented to ensure traceability and accountability of digital evidence handling before any analysis can take place. Multiple 'Document Chain Custody' activities can occur, and any number of events can happen between a 'Document Chain Custody' and a subsequent 'Analyze Digital Evidence.'

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This defines an ordered flow where preservation of evidence logically leads to its analysis, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between, and a single 'Analyze Digital Evidence' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Preserve Digital Evidence' events.

2. If a 'Review Evidence Report' activity occurs, then an 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity must occur after that 'Review Evidence Report' event and before any subsequent 'Review Evidence Report' occurs. This ensures that each review of the evidence report is individually responded to by an authorization for release, maintaining a clear one-to-one activity pairing that supports balanced process flow.

#### **Succession Constraints**
A 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must be followed by a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity at some point later in the same process instance, and conversely, a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event can occur only if a 'Collect Digital Evidence' event has occurred before it. This bidirectional and ordered dependency establishes a causal link, ensuring that the collection of digital evidence logically leads to its preservation, creating a cohesive progression within the process. Multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' events are allowed, with each collection eventually leading to a preservation and each preservation being justified by a preceding collection.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraints**
If the 'Audit Evidence Access' activity occurs in a process instance, then the 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, provided that the activation condition 'Legal Hold is Yes' is satisfied. This establishes a dependency where the auditing of evidence access necessitates the prior or subsequent collection of digital evidence, enhancing coherence in the process. If 'Audit Evidence Access' does not occur, or if the 'Legal Hold' attribute is not 'Yes,' there is no requirement for 'Collect Digital Evidence' to occur.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
1. Whenever an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Generate Evidence Report' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession captures a direct cause-and-effect relationship, ensuring that the analysis of digital evidence directly results in the generation of a comprehensive report summarizing the findings. Multiple 'Analyze Digital Evidence'-'Generate Evidence Report' pairs can exist in a process instance, each adhering to this immediate succession rule.

2. Whenever a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession ensures a direct and uninterrupted transition from preservation to integrity validation. Additionally, the time condition requires that the 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity must occur between 1 and 2 hours after the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity, ensuring timely verification of evidence integrity.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraints**
If the 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity occurs, then the 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa. This mutual obligation ensures that neither event can occur without the other, establishing a mutual dependency that guarantees evidence is both securely stored and integrity-validated within the process flow. The events can occur multiple times and in any order relative to each other.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Choice Constraints**
At least one of the activities 'Index Digital Evidence' or 'Update Evidence Metadata' must occur in the process instance. This introduces alternative paths, allowing flexibility by permitting either the organization and cataloging of digital evidence or the modification of its metadata to reflect current status and attributes. There is no restriction on the order or the number of times they occur, but the process is non-compliant if neither activity occurs.

#### **Exclusive Choice Constraints**
Exactly one of the activities 'Authorize Evidence Release' or 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must occur in the process instance. This enforces mutually exclusive pathways, ensuring that the process either concludes with the authorized release of digital evidence to authorized parties or its secure and permanent disposal. The process is non-compliant if both activities occur or if neither occurs.

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity and the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This exclusion rule ensures that the categorization of digital evidence based on its type, sensitivity, and relevance does not co-occur with its disposal, maintaining process integrity by preventing conflicting events. If 'Classify Digital Evidence' occurs one or more times, 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must not occur at all, and vice-versa.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraints**
A 'Report Security Breach' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event cannot be immediately preceded by a 'Report Security Breach' event. This prevents direct succession, ensuring that the documentation and notification of security breaches do not immediately precede or follow the disposal of digital evidence, thereby refining the process model by excluding specific immediate sequences that could compromise procedural integrity.
