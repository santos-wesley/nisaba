#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Collect Digital Evidence**: The 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity represents the initial gathering of digital evidence from various sources.
- **Preserve Digital Evidence**: The 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity involves maintaining the original state of the collected digital evidence to prevent any alteration.
- **Analyze Digital Evidence**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity includes examining the preserved digital evidence to extract relevant information and insights.
- **Document Chain Custody**: The 'Document Chain Custody' activity entails recording the handling and transfer of digital evidence to ensure its integrity and admissibility.
- **Secure Evidence Storage**: The 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity focuses on storing the digital evidence in a protected environment to safeguard against unauthorized access.
- **Review Evidence Report**: The 'Review Evidence Report' activity involves assessing the generated report for accuracy, completeness, and compliance.
- **Authorize Evidence Release**: The 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity grants permission for the release of digital evidence to authorized parties.
- **Dispose Digital Evidence**: The 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity covers the secure and compliant disposal of digital evidence after its retention period.
- **Validate Evidence Integrity**: The 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity checks the digital evidence to confirm it has not been tampered with or altered.
- **Generate Evidence Report**: The 'Generate Evidence Report' activity produces a detailed report summarizing the findings from the analysis of digital evidence.
- **Classify Digital Evidence**: The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity categorizes the digital evidence based on its type and sensitivity level.
- **Index Digital Evidence**: The 'Index Digital Evidence' activity organizes the digital evidence for efficient retrieval and reference during investigations.
- **Audit Evidence Access**: The 'Audit Evidence Access' activity monitors and logs all accesses to the digital evidence for accountability and security purposes.
- **Report Security Breach**: The 'Report Security Breach' activity documents any unauthorized access or compromise of the digital evidence.
- **Update Evidence Metadata**: The 'Update Evidence Metadata' activity modifies the metadata associated with the digital evidence to reflect changes or additional information.

## Attributes
- **Evidence Type: Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File**: The attribute 'Evidence Type: Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File' specifies the category of the digital evidence being handled in the process.
- **Evidence Size: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Evidence Size: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the size of the digital evidence in megabytes or another unit, within the specified range.
- **Custody Status: Collected, Preserved, Analyzed, Released, Disposed**: The attribute 'Custody Status: Collected, Preserved, Analyzed, Released, Disposed' indicates the current stage of the digital evidence in the custody chain.
- **Integrity Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Integrity Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0' measures the level of integrity of the digital evidence, where 1.0 indicates perfect integrity.
- **Security Level: Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified**: The attribute 'Security Level: Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified' defines the sensitivity classification of the digital evidence.
- **Access Count: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Access Count: integer between 0 and 100' tracks the number of times the digital evidence has been accessed.
- **Legal Hold: Yes, No**: The attribute 'Legal Hold: Yes, No' specifies whether a legal hold is placed on the digital evidence, preventing its disposal.
- **Analysis Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0**: The attribute 'Analysis Time: float between 0.1 and 24.0' represents the duration in hours required to analyze the digital evidence.
- **Retention Period: integer between 30 and 3650**: The attribute 'Retention Period: integer between 30 and 3650' indicates the number of days the digital evidence must be retained before disposal.
- **Disposal Method: Shredding, Wiping, Deletion**: The attribute 'Disposal Method: Shredding, Wiping, Deletion' specifies the technique used to securely dispose of the digital evidence.

## Binds
- **bind Collect Digital Evidence: Evidence Type, Evidence Size**: The bind 'Collect Digital Evidence: Evidence Type, Evidence Size' specifies that the activity 'Collect Digital Evidence' requires the attributes 'Evidence Type' and 'Evidence Size'.
- **bind Preserve Digital Evidence: Custody Status, Integrity Score**: The bind 'Preserve Digital Evidence: Custody Status, Integrity Score' links the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Custody Status' and 'Integrity Score'.
- **bind Analyze Digital Evidence: Analysis Time, Security Level**: The bind 'Analyze Digital Evidence: Analysis Time, Security Level' associates the 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity with the attributes 'Analysis Time' and 'Security Level'.
- **bind Dispose Digital Evidence: Disposal Method, Retention Period**: The bind 'Dispose Digital Evidence: Disposal Method, Retention Period' connects the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity to the attributes 'Disposal Method' and 'Retention Period'.
- **bind Audit Evidence Access: Access Count, Legal Hold**: The bind 'Audit Evidence Access: Access Count, Legal Hold' ties the 'Audit Evidence Access' activity to the attributes 'Access Count' and 'Legal Hold'.

## Constraints
- **Init[Collect Digital Evidence] | |**: The 'Collect Digital Evidence' must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **End[Dispose Digital Evidence] | |**: The 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Succession[Collect Digital Evidence, Preserve Digital Evidence] | | |**: A 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must be followed by a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity at some point later in the same process instance. Conversely, a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event can occur only if a 'Collect Digital Evidence' event has occurred before it within the same process instance. This bidirectional and ordered dependency ensures that 'Collect Digital Evidence' impose obligations for future 'Preserve Digital Evidence', and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' cannot occur without prior 'Collect Digital Evidence'. Multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' and 'Preserve Digital Evidence' are allowed, with each 'Collect Digital Evidence' eventually leading to a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' and each 'Preserve Digital Evidence' being justified by a preceding 'Collect Digital Evidence'.
- **Response[Preserve Digital Evidence, Analyze Digital Evidence] | | |**: If a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must eventually occur after the 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Preserve Digital Evidence' can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Analyze Digital Evidence' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Preserve Digital Evidence'. If 'Preserve Digital Evidence' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Analyze Digital Evidence'.
- **Precedence[Document Chain Custody, Analyze Digital Evidence] | | |**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Chain Custody' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance.
- **Responded Existence[Audit Evidence Access, Collect Digital Evidence] |A.Legal Hold is Yes | |**: If 'Audit Evidence Access' activity occurs in a process instance with A.Legal Hold is Yes, then 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the 'Audit Evidence Access' event. If 'Audit Evidence Access' does not occur with A.Legal Hold is Yes, there is no requirement for 'Collect Digital Evidence' to occur. Multiple occurrences of 'Audit Evidence Access' with A.Legal Hold is Yes are satisfied by at least one occurrence of 'Collect Digital Evidence', and multiple 'Collect Digital Evidence' instances are allowed.
- **Chain Response[Analyze Digital Evidence, Generate Evidence Report] | | |**: Whenever an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Generate Evidence Report' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This immediate succession ensures a direct and uninterrupted transition from 'Analyze Digital Evidence' to 'Generate Evidence Report'. Multiple 'Analyze Digital Evidence'-'Generate Evidence Report' pairs can exist in a process instance, each adhering to this immediate succession rule. Events of 'Generate Evidence Report' can occur elsewhere in the process but not between an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' and its corresponding 'Generate Evidence Report'.
- **Co-Existence[Secure Evidence Storage, Validate Evidence Integrity] | | |**: If 'Secure Evidence Storage' activity occurs, then 'Validate Evidence Integrity' must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa.
- **Not Chain Succession[Report Security Breach, Dispose Digital Evidence] | | |**: A 'Report Security Breach' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity; there must be at least one other event between them. Similarly, a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event cannot be immediately preceded by a 'Report Security Breach' event. This constraint allows multiple occurrences of 'Report Security Breach' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence' in any order, provided they are not adjacent in the event sequence.
- **Not Co-Existence[Classify Digital Evidence, Dispose Digital Evidence] | | |**: The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity and the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This means that if 'Classify Digital Evidence' event occurs one or more times, 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must not occur at all in that process instance, and vice-versa. It is acceptable for neither event to occur. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusion between 'Classify Digital Evidence' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence', allowing for different process variants based on which event (if any) occurs.
- **Choice[Index Digital Evidence, Update Evidence Metadata] | | |**: At least one of the activities 'Index Digital Evidence' or 'Update Evidence Metadata' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if 'Index Digital Evidence' event occurs, 'Update Evidence Metadata' event occurs, or both occur. There is no restriction on the order or the number of times they occur. However, if neither 'Index Digital Evidence' nor 'Update Evidence Metadata' occurs, the constraint is violated.
- **Exclusive Choice[Authorize Evidence Release, Dispose Digital Evidence] | | |**: Exactly one of the activities 'Authorize Evidence Release' or 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must occur in the process instance. This means that the process is compliant if only 'Authorize Evidence Release' event occurs without any occurrence of 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event, or only 'Dispose Digital Evidence' occurs without any occurrence of 'Authorize Evidence Release'. The process is non-compliant if both 'Authorize Evidence Release' and 'Dispose Digital Evidence' occur, or if neither occurs. The constraint enforces a mutual exclusivity and a mandatory occurrence of one of the specified activities.
- **Existence[Analyze Digital Evidence] | |**: The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must occur at least once in the process.
- **Alternate Response[Review Evidence Report, Authorize Evidence Release] | | |**: If a 'Review Evidence Report' activity occurs, then an 'Authorize Evidence Release' activity must occur after that 'Review Evidence Report' event and before any subsequent 'Review Evidence Report' occurs no other occurrence of 'Review Evidence Report' is allowed between a 'Review Evidence Report' and its corresponding 'Authorize Evidence Release'. This ensures that each 'Review Evidence Report' is individually responded to by an 'Authorize Evidence Release' in sequence.
- **Chain Response[Preserve Digital Evidence, Validate Evidence Integrity] | | |1,2,h**: Whenever a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between, and the time between them must be between 1 and 2 hours. This immediate succession ensures a direct and uninterrupted transition from 'Preserve Digital Evidence' to 'Validate Evidence Integrity'. Multiple 'Preserve Digital Evidence'-'Validate Evidence Integrity' pairs can exist in a process instance, each adhering to this immediate succession rule. Events of 'Validate Evidence Integrity' can occur elsewhere in the process but not between a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' and its corresponding 'Validate Evidence Integrity'.


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for digital evidence management is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Collect Digital Evidence,' which involves the initial gathering of digital evidence from various sources and requires the attributes 'Evidence Type' (Document, Image, Video, Audio, Log File) and 'Evidence Size' (integer between 1 and 1000). This establishes the foundational context for all subsequent evidence handling activities.
#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Dispose Digital Evidence,' which covers the secure and compliant disposal of digital evidence after its retention period using the attributes 'Disposal Method' (Shredding, Wiping, Deletion) and 'Retention Period' (integer between 30 and 3650). This activity must be the last to occur in the process.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that preserved digital evidence is examined to extract relevant information and insights using the attributes 'Analysis Time' (float between 0.1 and 24.0) and 'Security Level' (Confidential, Restricted, Unclassified), adding depth and reliability to the overall evidence management workflow.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The activity 'Analyze Digital Evidence' is permitted to occur only if a 'Document Chain Custody' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This background condition ensures that the handling and transfer of digital evidence have been properly recorded to maintain its integrity and admissibility prior to any analytical examination.

#### **Response Constraints**
If a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity must eventually occur after it. This sequenced dependency maintains the process flow by ensuring that evidence whose custody status has been set to 'Preserved' and whose 'Integrity Score' (float between 0.0 and 1.0) has been recorded is subsequently subjected to detailed analysis.

#### **Succession Constraints**
A 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must be followed by a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity at some point later in the same process instance, while a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' event can occur only after a preceding 'Collect Digital Evidence' event. This cause-effect relationship creates a cohesive progression in which every collection of evidence (with its type and size) imposes an obligation for its subsequent preservation (updating custody status and integrity score), and vice versa, allowing multiple paired occurrences within an instance.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraints**
The activities 'Secure Evidence Storage' and 'Validate Evidence Integrity' must both occur within the same process instance. This conjunction ensures mutual dependency so that evidence placed in a protected storage environment is necessarily checked for tampering, and any integrity validation is paired with secure storage, thereby reinforcing the safeguarding of digital evidence.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
1. Whenever an 'Analyze Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Generate Evidence Report' activity in the next event position with no intervening events. This direct cause-and-effect linkage produces a detailed report summarizing analysis findings without delay.
2. Whenever a 'Preserve Digital Evidence' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Validate Evidence Integrity' activity in the next event position with no other events in between, and the time between them must be between 1 and 2 hours. This tightly coupled cause-effect succession confirms that evidence has not been altered immediately after preservation.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Choice Constraints**
At least one of the activities 'Index Digital Evidence' or 'Update Evidence Metadata' must occur in the process instance. This contrast introduces alternative paths, allowing flexibility so that evidence is either organized for efficient retrieval or its metadata is modified to reflect changes, ensuring the process cannot complete without one of these supportive actions.

#### **Exclusive Choice Constraints**
Exactly one of the activities 'Authorize Evidence Release' or 'Dispose Digital Evidence' must occur in the process instance. This alternative enforces mutually exclusive pathways: the process is compliant only when evidence is released to authorized parties or when it is disposed after its retention period, but never both and never neither.

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Classify Digital Evidence' activity and the 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This contrast sets a clear exclusion rule so that evidence categorization based on type and sensitivity level prevents subsequent disposal (or vice versa), thereby maintaining process integrity by avoiding conflicting evidence lifecycle states.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraints**
A 'Report Security Breach' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' activity, and a 'Dispose Digital Evidence' event cannot be immediately preceded by a 'Report Security Breach' event. This contrast prevents direct succession, ensuring that any documented unauthorized access or compromise is separated from final disposal by at least one other activity, thereby refining the model and preserving accountability.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraints**
If an 'Audit Evidence Access' activity occurs with the condition that 'Legal Hold is Yes,' then a 'Collect Digital Evidence' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance. This cause-effect dependency guarantees that any monitored and logged access under legal hold is justified by the initial collection of evidence, with multiple audit events satisfied by at least one collection.
