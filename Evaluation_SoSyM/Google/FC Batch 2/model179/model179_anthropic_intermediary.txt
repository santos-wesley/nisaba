## Activities
- **Request Return**: The 'Request Return' activity represents the initiation of a return request by a customer for a purchased item.
- **Approve Return**: The 'Approve Return' activity involves the review and authorization of a submitted return request.
- **Generate Return Label**: The 'Generate Return Label' activity includes the creation of a shipping label to be used for returning the item.
- **Ship Item Back**: The 'Ship Item Back' activity represents the shipping of the item by the customer back to the retailer or warehouse.
- **Receive Item**: The 'Receive Item' activity involves the receipt of the returned item at the retailer's or warehouse's facility.
- **Inspect Item**: The 'Inspect Item' activity includes the examination of the returned item to assess its condition.
- **Determine Refund**: The 'Determine Refund' activity involves calculating the refund amount owed to the customer based on the return.
- **Process Refund**: The 'Process Refund' activity represents the execution of the refund payment to the customer.
- **Restock Item**: The 'Restock Item' activity involves returning the inspected item back into inventory for resale.
- **Dispose Item**: The 'Dispose Item' activity includes the disposal of the returned item when it is not suitable for restocking.

## Attributes
- **Return Status: Requested, Approved, Rejected, Shipped, Received, Inspected, Refunded, Restocked, Disposed**: The attribute 'Return Status: Requested, Approved, Rejected, Shipped, Received, Inspected, Refunded, Restocked, Disposed' represents the current stage or state of a return within the return process.
- **Item Condition: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Item Condition: integer between 1 and 5' represents the physical condition rating assigned to the returned item during inspection.
- **Refund Amount: float between 0.0 and 1000.0**: The attribute 'Refund Amount: float between 0.0 and 1000.0' represents the monetary amount to be refunded to the customer for the returned item.
- **Days Since Purchase: integer between 0 and 365**: The attribute 'Days Since Purchase: integer between 0 and 365' represents the number of days that have elapsed since the item was originally purchased.
- **Return Reason: Defective, Damaged, Wrong Item, No Longer Needed**: The attribute 'Return Reason: Defective, Damaged, Wrong Item, No Longer Needed' specifies the reason provided by the customer for returning the item.
- **Inspection Score: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Inspection Score: integer between 1 and 10' represents the numeric score assigned to the returned item as a result of the inspection process.
