## Activities
- **Submit Benefits Enrollment**: The 'Submit Benefits Enrollment' activity represents the action of an employee submitting their enrollment for benefits.
- **Verify Employee Eligibility**: The 'Verify Employee Eligibility' activity involves checking whether an employee is eligible for the requested benefits.
- **Process Benefits Claim**: The 'Process Benefits Claim' activity represents the handling and processing of a benefits claim submitted by an employee.
- **Approve Benefits Claim**: The 'Approve Benefits Claim' activity involves approving a benefits claim that has been submitted and processed.
- **Deny Benefits Claim**: The 'Deny Benefits Claim' activity represents the rejection of a benefits claim that has been submitted and processed.
- **Update Benefits Record**: The 'Update Benefits Record' activity involves updating the benefits records of an employee to reflect changes in enrollment or claim status.
- **Audit Benefits Enrollment**: The 'Audit Benefits Enrollment' activity represents the auditing of benefits enrollment records to ensure compliance and accuracy.
- **Send Enrollment Confirmation**: The 'Send Enrollment Confirmation' activity involves sending a confirmation to the employee regarding their benefits enrollment submission.
- **Receive Employee Inquiry**: The 'Receive Employee Inquiry' activity represents the receipt of an inquiry from an employee regarding benefits, claims, eligibility, or payments.
- **Resolve Employee Inquiry**: The 'Resolve Employee Inquiry' activity involves addressing and resolving an inquiry raised by an employee.
- **Request Additional Information**: The 'Request Additional Information' activity represents the action of requesting further information from an employee regarding their benefits enrollment or claim.
- **Review Benefits Options**: The 'Review Benefits Options' activity involves reviewing the available benefits options for an employee during the enrollment process.
- **Calculate Premium Cost**: The 'Calculate Premium Cost' activity represents the computation of the premium cost for a selected benefit plan.
- **Process Premium Payment**: The 'Process Premium Payment' activity involves processing the payment of premiums for a benefits plan.
- **Generate Benefits Report**: The 'Generate Benefits Report' activity represents the generation of a report summarizing benefits enrollment, claims, and related data.

- **Inquiry Type: Enrollment, Claim, Eligibility, Payment**: The attribute 'Inquiry Type: Enrollment, Claim, Eligibility, Payment' specifies the types of inquiries an employee can raise, including enrollment, claim, eligibility, or payment-related inquiries.

- **Deduction Amount: float between 0.0 and 5000.0**: The attribute 'Deduction Amount: float between 0.0 and 5000.0' represents the amount deducted from an employee's compensation for a benefit plan, ranging from 0.0 to 5000.0.
