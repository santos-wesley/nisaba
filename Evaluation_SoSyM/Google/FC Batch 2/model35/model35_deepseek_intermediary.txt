## Activities
- **Submit Benefits Enrollment**: The 'Submit Benefits Enrollment' activity represents the process of an employee submitting their benefits enrollment form.
- **Verify Employee Eligibility**: The 'Verify Employee Eligibility' activity involves checking whether the employee meets the eligibility criteria for the selected benefits.
- **Process Benefits Claim**: The 'Process Benefits Claim' activity handles the processing of a submitted benefits claim.
- **Approve Benefits Claim**: The 'Approve Benefits Claim' activity represents the approval of a benefits claim.
- **Deny Benefits Claim**: The 'Deny Benefits Claim' activity represents the denial of a benefits claim.
- **Update Benefits Record**: The 'Update Benefits Record' activity involves updating the employee's benefits records in the system.
- **Audit Benefits Enrollment**: The 'Audit Benefits Enrollment' activity involves auditing the benefits enrollment process for compliance.
- **Send Enrollment Confirmation**: The 'Send Enrollment Confirmation' activity involves sending a confirmation notification to the employee regarding their enrollment.
- **Receive Employee Inquiry**: The 'Receive Employee Inquiry' activity represents receiving an inquiry from an employee regarding benefits.
- **Resolve Employee Inquiry**: The 'Resolve Employee Inquiry' activity involves addressing and resolving the employee's inquiry.
- **Request Additional Information**: The 'Request Additional Information' activity involves requesting further details from the employee to process their enrollment or claim.
- **Review Benefits Options**: The 'Review Benefits Options' activity involves reviewing the available benefits options with the employee.
- **Calculate Premium Cost**: The 'Calculate Premium Cost' activity involves computing the premium cost based on the selected benefit plan.
- **Process Premium Payment**: The 'Process Premium Payment' activity handles the processing of premium payments for the benefits.
- **Generate Benefits Report**: The 'Generate Benefits Report' activity involves generating a report summarizing the benefits enrollment and related activities.

- **Enrollment Status: Submitted, Verified, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Enrollment Status: Submitted, Verified, Approved, Rejected' specifies the possible statuses of a benefits enrollment.

- **Claim Status: Received, Processed, Approved, Denied, Paid**: The attribute 'Claim Status: Received, Processed, Approved, Denied, Paid' specifies the possible statuses of a benefits claim.
- **Premium Rate: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Premium Rate: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents the premium rate applied to calculate the cost of benefits.
- **Employee ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Employee ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to an employee.
- **Inquiry Type: Enrollment, Claim, Eligibility, Payment**: The attribute 'Inquiry Type: Enrollment, Claim, Eligibility, Payment' specifies the possible categories of employee inquiries.
- **Benefit Plan: Health, Dental, Vision, Life**: The attribute 'Benefit Plan: Health, Dental, Vision, Life' specifies the types of benefit plans available for enrollment.
- **Deduction Amount: float between 0.0 and 5000.0**: The attribute 'Deduction Amount: float between 0.0 and 5000.0' represents the amount deducted from the employee's paycheck for benefits.
