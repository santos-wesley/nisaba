**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Chemical Process Workflow**

This model describes a laboratory-scale chemical synthesis and product handling procedure with both control-flow constraints and data perspectives.

### Activities
The process consists of the following 10 activities:
- **Prepare Solution**
- **Heat Mixture**
- **Add Catalyst**
- **Monitor Reaction**
- **Cool Solution**
- **Filter Product**
- **Dry Product**
- **Analyze Sample**
- **Store Product**
- **Dispose Waste**

### Data Perspective (Bindings)
Each activity is associated with specific data attributes:

- **Prepare Solution**: Concentration (0.1–10.0), Solvent Type (Water, Ethanol, Toluene)
- **Heat Mixture**: Temperature (0.0–500.0 °C), Reaction Time (1–600)
- **Add Catalyst**: Catalyst Type (Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron)
- **Monitor Reaction**: Reaction Status (Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed), Reaction Time
- **Analyze Sample**: Purity (0–100%), Yield (0.0–100.0%)
- **Store Product**: Batch Number (1–1000)

The remaining activities (Cool Solution, Filter Product, Dry Product, Dispose Waste) have no data attributes defined.

### Control-Flow Constraints

#### Core Declarative Constraints:
- The process **must start** with **Prepare Solution**.
- **Prepare Solution** must be directly followed by **Heat Mixture** (Succession).
- **Heat Mixture** must eventually be followed by **Add Catalyst** (Response).
- **Add Catalyst** and **Monitor Reaction** are mutually dependent — if one occurs, the other must also occur (Responded Existence).
- **Monitor Reaction** must be immediately followed by **Cool Solution** (Chain Response).
- **Cool Solution** must occur before **Filter Product** (Precedence).
- **Filter Product** and **Dry Product** must occur strictly one after the other (Chain Succession).
- **Dry Product** must eventually lead to **Analyze Sample** (Response).
- After **Analyze Sample**, **Store Product** may occur, but not repeatedly in an alternating fashion (Alternate Response).
- **Dispose Waste** cannot occur after **Store Product** (Precedence).

#### Timed Response Constraints:
- After **Add Catalyst**, **Monitor Reaction** must occur **within 10 minutes**.
- After **Monitor Reaction**, **Cool Solution** must occur **within 5 minutes**.
- After **Cool Solution**, **Filter Product** must occur **within 2 hours**.
- After **Dry Product**, **Analyze Sample** must occur **within 1 day**.
- After **Store Product**, **Dispose Waste** must occur **within 1 hour**.

---

This model represents a **partially ordered, data-aware chemical workflow** that enforces a logical sequence of laboratory steps while allowing some flexibility (e.g., when to dispose of waste), but with strict timing requirements between critical reaction and post-processing steps.
