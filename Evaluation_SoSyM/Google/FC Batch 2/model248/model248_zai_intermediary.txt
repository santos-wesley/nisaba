## Activities
- **Prepare Solution**: The 'Prepare Solution' activity represents the preparation of a chemical solution by combining a solute with a specified solvent at a given concentration.
- **Heat Mixture**: The 'Heat Mixture' activity involves applying heat to the prepared mixture to facilitate the chemical reaction.
- **Add Catalyst**: The 'Add Catalyst' activity involves introducing a catalyst into the heated mixture to accelerate or enable the chemical reaction.
- **Monitor Reaction**: The 'Monitor Reaction' activity includes observing and tracking the status and progress of the chemical reaction over time.
- **Cool Solution**: The 'Cool Solution' activity involves reducing the temperature of the solution after the reaction has been monitored.
- **Filter Product**: The 'Filter Product' activity represents the separation of the desired product from the solution using a filtration process.
- **Dry Product**: The 'Dry Product' activity involves removing residual moisture or solvent from the filtered product.
- **Analyze Sample**: The 'Analyze Sample' activity includes testing and evaluating the purity and yield of the obtained product sample.
- **Store Product**: The 'Store Product' activity involves storing the final product with an assigned batch number for inventory and traceability purposes.
- **Dispose Waste**: The 'Dispose Waste' activity represents the safe and proper disposal of any waste generated during the chemical process.

## Attributes
- **Temperature: float between 0.0 and 500.0**: The attribute 'Temperature: float between 0.0 and 500.0' represents the temperature value applied during the heating of the mixture, measured in degrees.
- **Reaction Time: integer between 1 and 600**: The attribute 'Reaction Time: integer between 1 and 600' represents the duration of the chemical reaction, measured in time units.
- **Catalyst Type: Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron**: The attribute 'Catalyst Type: Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron' specifies the types of catalysts available for addition during the reaction.
- **Concentration: float between 0.1 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Concentration: float between 0.1 and 10.0' represents the concentration level of the solute in the prepared solution.
- **Solvent Type: Water, Ethanol, Toluene**: The attribute 'Solvent Type: Water, Ethanol, Toluene' specifies the types of solvents available for preparing the solution.
- **Yield: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Yield: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the yield percentage of the product obtained from the chemical process.
- **Purity: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Purity: integer between 0 and 100' represents the purity percentage of the analyzed product sample.
- **Reaction Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed**: The attribute 'Reaction Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed' specifies the possible statuses of the chemical reaction during monitoring.
- **Batch Number: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Batch Number: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the identification number assigned to a stored product batch for traceability.
