#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Prepare Solution**: The 'Prepare Solution' activity represents the preparation of a chemical solution prior to further processing.
- **Heat Mixture**: The 'Heat Mixture' activity involves heating the prepared mixture to reach the required reaction conditions.
- **Add Catalyst**: The 'Add Catalyst' activity involves introducing a catalyst into the mixture to facilitate the chemical reaction.
- **Monitor Reaction**: The 'Monitor Reaction' activity includes observing and tracking the progress and status of the ongoing chemical reaction.
- **Cool Solution**: The 'Cool Solution' activity represents the cooling down of the reacted solution after the reaction process.
- **Filter Product**: The 'Filter Product' activity involves separating the solid product from the liquid solution.
- **Dry Product**: The 'Dry Product' activity involves removing residual moisture from the filtered product.
- **Analyze Sample**: The 'Analyze Sample' activity involves testing a sample of the product to determine its quality characteristics.
- **Store Product**: The 'Store Product' activity represents the storage of the finished product for future use or distribution.
- **Dispose Waste**: The 'Dispose Waste' activity involves the proper disposal of waste materials generated during the process.

## Attributes
- **Temperature: Float between 0.0 and 500.0**: The attribute 'Temperature: Float between 0.0 and 500.0' represents the temperature applied during the heating process.
- **Reaction Time: Integer between 1 and 600**: The attribute 'Reaction Time: Integer between 1 and 600' represents the duration of the chemical reaction.
- **Catalyst Type: Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron**: The attribute 'Catalyst Type: Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron' specifies the type of catalyst used to facilitate the reaction.
- **Concentration: Float between 0.1 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Concentration: Float between 0.1 and 10.0' represents the concentration of the prepared solution.
- **Solvent Type: Water, Ethanol, Toluene**: The attribute 'Solvent Type: Water, Ethanol, Toluene' specifies the type of solvent used in preparing the solution.
- **Yield: Float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Yield: Float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the percentage yield obtained from the process.
- **Purity: Integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Purity: Integer between 0 and 100' represents the purity percentage of the analyzed sample.
- **Reaction Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed**: The attribute 'Reaction Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed' specifies the current status of the chemical reaction being monitored.
- **Batch Number: Integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Batch Number: Integer between 1 and 1000' represents the identification number assigned to a specific production batch.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the chemical production process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Prepare Solution,' which establishes the initial context by preparing a chemical solution with a specified 'Concentration' and 'Solvent Type' prior to any further processing. This foundational step sets the stage for all subsequent activities in the chemical production process.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Store Product,' bringing the process to a coherent close. This activity must be the last to occur, ensuring that the finished product, having passed through all preceding stages of reaction, filtration, drying, and analysis, is properly stored for future use or distribution.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraints**
The 'Monitor Reaction' activity must occur at least once within the process. This ensures that the progress and status of the chemical reaction are consistently observed and tracked, adding depth and reliability by confirming that the reaction is proceeding as expected before moving forward.

#### **Absence Constraints**
In contrast, the 'Dispose Waste' activity must not occur more than once in the process. This exclusion reinforces process integrity by preventing redundant or excessive waste disposal actions, ensuring that waste management remains a controlled, singular event within the production cycle.

#### **Exactly Constraints**
The 'Analyze Sample' activity must occur exactly once in the process. This fixed boundary ensures precision within the process model, guaranteeing that the product's quality characteristics, such as 'Purity,' are assessed through a single, definitive testing event rather than repeated or omitted evaluations.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraints**
If the 'Add Catalyst' activity occurs, then the 'Heat Mixture' activity must also occur somewhere in the process, establishing a dependency between the two. This cause-effect relationship ensures that the introduction of a catalyst is always accompanied by the necessary heating conditions, enhancing coherence in the reaction setup.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
1. The activity 'Heat Mixture' is permitted to occur only if a 'Prepare Solution' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that 'Concentration' is greater than 0.1. This sets up an initial context where the solution must be properly prepared and concentrated before the heating process can begin.
2. The activity 'Filter Product' is permitted to occur only if a 'Cool Solution' activity has occurred before it. This background condition guides the logical sequence, ensuring that the reacted solution is sufficiently cooled before the solid product is separated from the liquid.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If the 'Add Catalyst' activity occurs with the condition 'Catalyst Type is Platinum,' then the 'Monitor Reaction' activity must occur afterward with the condition 'Reaction Status is Ongoing.' This defines an ordered flow, ensuring that once the catalyst is introduced, the reaction's progress is actively tracked, maintaining a cohesive temporal structure.
2. If the 'Filter Product' activity occurs, then the 'Dry Product' activity must occur afterward. This sequence ensures that the filtered product undergoes moisture removal in due course, preserving the logical flow of post-reaction processing.

#### **Succession Constraints**
Whenever a 'Cool Solution' activity occurs, it must be followed by a 'Filter Product' activity, and conversely, 'Filter Product' must be preceded by 'Cool Solution.' This establishes a causal link, ensuring that each activity follows logically from the previous one, creating a cohesive progression from cooling to filtration within the model.

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Alternate Precedence Constraints**
Each occurrence of the 'Dry Product' activity must be preceded by a 'Filter Product' activity, without any intervening 'Dry Product' occurrences in between. This condition-consequence relationship ensures order consistency, requiring that filtration always precedes drying in a balanced, non-overlapping manner.

#### **Alternate Response Constraints**
Each occurrence of the 'Add Catalyst' activity must be followed by a unique 'Monitor Reaction' activity, with no intervening 'Add Catalyst' occurrences before it. This promotes balance by ensuring a clear one-to-one pairing between catalyst addition and reaction monitoring, preventing ambiguous overlaps.

#### **Alternate Succession Constraints**
The 'Prepare Solution' and 'Heat Mixture' activities must alternate strictly: each 'Prepare Solution' must be followed by a 'Heat Mixture' before another 'Prepare Solution' occurs, and each 'Heat Mixture' must be preceded by a 'Prepare Solution' before another 'Heat Mixture' occurs. This enforces an ordered structure with unique activity pairings, enhancing overall process coherence.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Precedence Constraints**
The 'Analyze Sample' activity must be immediately preceded by the 'Dry Product' activity, with no intervening events. This establishes immediate dependency, ensuring that quality testing occurs directly after moisture removal, making the sequence explicit and precise.

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
Whenever the 'Heat Mixture' activity occurs with the condition 'Temperature is greater than 100.0,' it must be immediately followed by the 'Add Catalyst' activity. This captures an immediate succession, ensuring a direct cause-and-effect relationship where reaching the required temperature is instantly followed by catalyst introduction.

#### **Chain Succession Constraints**
Whenever the 'Filter Product' activity occurs, it must be immediately succeeded by the 'Dry Product' activity, and 'Dry Product' must be immediately preceded by 'Filter Product.' This enforces a tightly coupled sequence, ensuring that filtration and drying occur back-to-back with no intervening activities.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Analyze Sample' activity and the 'Store Product' activity are mutually dependent: if one occurs, the other must occur as well. This conjunction ensures that a sample is always analyzed before the product is stored, and that storage only follows a completed analysis, establishing a reliable mutual dependency within the process flow.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Choice Constraints**
Either the 'Analyze Sample' activity or the 'Dispose Waste' activity may occur following the reaction stages, introducing alternative paths. This contrast allows flexibility, permitting either activity to occur depending on whether the product meets quality standards or must instead be discarded.

#### **Exclusive Choice Constraints**
Exactly one of the 'Store Product' or 'Dispose Waste' activities must occur within the process instance, but not both. This alternative relationship enforces mutually exclusive pathways, ensuring that the final outcome of the batch is either successful storage or waste disposal, never both simultaneously.

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Dispose Waste' activity and the 'Store Product' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance if the condition 'Reaction Status is Failed' is met. This exclusion rule ensures that a failed reaction's byproduct is not mistakenly stored, maintaining process integrity by preventing conflicting outcomes.

### **Negative Dependency Constraints**

#### **Not Responded Existence Constraints**
If the 'Dispose Waste' activity occurs, then the 'Store Product' activity must not occur in the same process instance. This contrast prevents an unwanted dependency, ensuring that waste disposal and product storage remain disallowed contingent occurrences, refining the model's logical boundaries.

### **Negative Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Precedence Constraints**
The 'Store Product' activity must not precede the 'Analyze Sample' activity. This restriction ensures that the designated order—analysis before storage—is preserved, preventing premature storage of an unverified product.

#### **Not Response Constraints**
If the 'Dispose Waste' activity occurs, the 'Heat Mixture' activity must not occur afterward within the same process instance. This contrast prevents an inappropriate continuation of the reaction process once waste has been disposed of, maintaining the integrity of the model's intended progression.

#### **Not Succession Constraints**
The 'Store Product' activity must not be followed at any point by the 'Add Catalyst' activity. This restriction preserves the model's defined flow, ensuring that once a product is stored, no further reactive processing can be reintroduced into that batch.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Precedence Constraints**
The 'Analyze Sample' activity must not be immediately preceded by the 'Cool Solution' activity. This enforces a non-immediate order restriction, ensuring that cooling and sample analysis remain separated by the necessary filtration and drying steps.

#### **Not Chain Response Constraints**
The 'Prepare Solution' activity must not be immediately followed by the 'Add Catalyst' activity. This contrast prevents an inappropriate immediate succession, preserving the structural integrity of the sequence by requiring heating to occur before catalyst introduction.

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraints**
The 'Monitor Reaction' activity and the 'Store Product' activity must not immediately precede or follow each other. This contrast prevents a direct succession between these two activities, refining the process model by excluding an immediate transition from reaction monitoring straight to final storage without intervening cooling, filtration, drying, and analysis steps.
