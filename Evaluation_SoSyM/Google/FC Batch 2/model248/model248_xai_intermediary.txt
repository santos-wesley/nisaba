## Activities
- **Prepare Solution**: The 'Prepare Solution' activity represents the initial preparation of the chemical solution before heating.
- **Heat Mixture**: The 'Heat Mixture' activity involves heating the prepared mixture to initiate the reaction.
- **Add Catalyst**: The 'Add Catalyst' activity includes adding a catalyst to accelerate the chemical reaction.
- **Monitor Reaction**: The 'Monitor Reaction' activity consists of observing and tracking the progress of the ongoing reaction.
- **Cool Solution**: The 'Cool Solution' activity entails cooling the reacted mixture to a suitable temperature.
- **Filter Product**: The 'Filter Product' activity involves filtering the cooled solution to separate the product.
- **Dry Product**: The 'Dry Product' activity includes drying the filtered product to remove any remaining moisture.
- **Analyze Sample**: The 'Analyze Sample' activity represents analyzing the dried product sample for quality metrics.
- **Store Product**: The 'Store Product' activity involves storing the analyzed product in appropriate conditions.
- **Dispose Waste**: The 'Dispose Waste' activity consists of safely disposing of any waste generated during the process.

## Attributes
- **Temperature: float between 0.0 and 500.0**: The attribute 'Temperature: float between 0.0 and 500.0' represents the temperature level applied during the heating of the mixture.
- **Reaction Time: integer between 1 and 600**: The attribute 'Reaction Time: integer between 1 and 600' represents the duration in minutes for which the reaction is allowed to proceed.
- **Catalyst Type: Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron**: The attribute 'Catalyst Type: Platinum, Palladium, Nickel, Iron' specifies the type of catalyst used to facilitate the chemical reaction.
- **Concentration: float between 0.1 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Concentration: float between 0.1 and 10.0' represents the concentration level of the solution prepared initially.
- **Solvent Type: Water, Ethanol, Toluene**: The attribute 'Solvent Type: Water, Ethanol, Toluene' specifies the type of solvent used in preparing the solution.
- **Yield: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Yield: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the percentage yield obtained from the chemical process.
- **Purity: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Purity: integer between 0 and 100' represents the purity percentage of the analyzed product sample.
- **Reaction Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed**: The attribute 'Reaction Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed, Failed' indicates the current status of the monitored reaction.
- **Batch Number: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Batch Number: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique batch identification number for the stored product.
