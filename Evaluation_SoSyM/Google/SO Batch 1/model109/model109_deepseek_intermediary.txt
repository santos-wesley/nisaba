## Activities
- **Assess Client Needs**: The 'Assess Client Needs' activity represents the initial evaluation to understand the client's requirements and circumstances.
- **Develop Service Plan**: The 'Develop Service Plan' activity involves creating a tailored plan outlining the services to be provided to the client.
- **Obtain Funding Approval**: The 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity involves securing financial authorization for the services required by the client.
- **Provide Counseling Services**: The 'Provide Counseling Services' activity includes delivering therapeutic support to the client.
- **Coordinate Support Services**: The 'Coordinate Support Services' activity involves organizing additional support resources such as housing or job training for the client.
- **Monitor Client Progress**: The 'Monitor Client Progress' activity involves tracking and evaluating the client's development and outcomes over time.
- **Adjust Service Plan**: The 'Adjust Service Plan' activity involves modifying the existing service plan based on the client's evolving needs.
- **Conduct Home Visit**: The 'Conduct Home Visit' activity involves visiting the client's home environment to assess their living conditions.
- **Close Client File**: The 'Close Client File' activity represents the final step of concluding the client's case and archiving their records.
- **Refer to Specialist**: The 'Refer to Specialist' activity involves directing the client to a specialized professional for additional support.

## Attributes
- **Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High' specifies the assessed risk category of the client.
- **Service Plan Duration: integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Service Plan Duration: integer between 1 and 12' represents the length of the service plan in months.
- **Funding Amount Requested: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Funding Amount Requested: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the monetary amount requested for funding approval.
- **Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family**: The attribute 'Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family' specifies the type of counseling session to be provided.
- **Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare**: The attribute 'Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare' specifies the type of support service to be coordinated.
- **Progress Score: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Progress Score: integer between 0 and 100' represents the client's progress measured on a scale from 0 to 100.
- **Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs**: The attribute 'Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs' specifies the reason for adjusting the service plan.
- **Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative' indicates the result of the home visit assessment.
- **File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined**: The attribute 'File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined' specifies the reason for closing the client's file.
- **Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid**: The attribute 'Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid' specifies the type of specialist to whom the client is referred.
