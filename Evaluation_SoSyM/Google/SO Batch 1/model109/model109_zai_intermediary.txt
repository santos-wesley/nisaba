## Activities
- **Assess Client Needs**: The 'Assess Client Needs' activity represents the initial evaluation phase where the social worker identifies and documents the specific needs and circumstances of a client.
- **Develop Service Plan**: The 'Develop Service Plan' activity involves creating a structured plan outlining the services and interventions to be provided to the client based on the assessed needs.
- **Obtain Funding Approval**: The 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity represents the process of securing financial resources or approvals necessary to fund the services planned for the client.
- **Provide Counseling Services**: The 'Provide Counseling Services' activity involves delivering therapeutic counseling sessions to the client, which may be individual, group, or family-based.
- **Coordinate Support Services**: The 'Coordinate Support Services' activity entails organizing and managing additional support services such as housing, job training, or childcare for the client.
- **Monitor Client Progress**: The 'Monitor Client Progress' activity involves regularly tracking and evaluating the client's advancement toward the goals outlined in the service plan.
- **Adjust Service Plan**: The 'Adjust Service Plan' activity represents the modification of the existing service plan in response to the client's progress, setbacks, or newly identified needs.
- **Conduct Home Visit**: The 'Conduct Home Visit' activity involves visiting the client's home to assess their living conditions and gather contextual information relevant to their service plan.
- **Close Client File**: The 'Close Client File' activity represents the formal termination of services for a client, whether due to goals being met, the client relocating, or services being declined.
- **Refer to Specialist**: The 'Refer to Specialist' activity involves directing the client to a specialized professional, such as a psychiatrist, addiction counselor, or legal aid provider, for targeted assistance.

## Attributes
- **Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High' specifies the assessed risk level of the client, categorized as Low, Medium, or High.
- **Service Plan Duration: integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Service Plan Duration: integer between 1 and 12' represents the duration of the service plan in months, ranging from 1 to 12 months.
- **Funding Amount Requested: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Funding Amount Requested: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the monetary amount requested for funding the client's services, ranging from 100.0 to 10000.0.
- **Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family**: The attribute 'Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family' specifies the modality of counseling provided to the client, which can be Individual, Group, or Family.
- **Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare**: The attribute 'Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare' specifies the type of support service coordinated for the client, which can be Housing, Job Training, or Childcare.
- **Progress Score: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Progress Score: integer between 0 and 100' represents a numerical score indicating the client's progress toward their service plan goals, ranging from 0 to 100.
- **Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs**: The attribute 'Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs' specifies the reason for adjusting the service plan, categorized as Progress, Setback, or New Needs.
- **Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative' specifies the result of a home visit, categorized as Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
- **File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined**: The attribute 'File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined' specifies the reason for closing a client's file, categorized as Goals Met, Client Moved, or Services Declined.
- **Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid**: The attribute 'Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid' specifies the type of specialist to whom the client is referred, which can be a Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, or Legal Aid provider.
