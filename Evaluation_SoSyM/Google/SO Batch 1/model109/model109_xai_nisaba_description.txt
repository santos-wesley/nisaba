#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Assess Client Needs**: The 'Assess Client Needs' activity represents the initial evaluation phase where the client's requirements and risk factors are thoroughly assessed to inform subsequent service planning.
- **Develop Service Plan**: The 'Develop Service Plan' activity involves creating a tailored service plan based on the assessed client needs, outlining the duration and specific interventions required.
- **Obtain Funding Approval**: The 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity includes requesting and securing approval for the necessary funding amount to support the client's service plan.
- **Provide Counseling Services**: The 'Provide Counseling Services' activity entails delivering counseling sessions of a specified type to address the client's emotional and psychological needs.
- **Coordinate Support Services**: The 'Coordinate Support Services' activity focuses on arranging and linking the client with appropriate support services such as housing, job training, or childcare.
- **Monitor Client Progress**: The 'Monitor Client Progress' activity involves regularly tracking and evaluating the client's advancement using a progress score to ensure the service plan remains effective.
- **Adjust Service Plan**: The 'Adjust Service Plan' activity requires modifying the existing service plan in response to factors like progress, setbacks, or new needs identified during monitoring.
- **Conduct Home Visit**: The 'Conduct Home Visit' activity consists of performing on-site visits to the client's home to observe conditions and outcomes directly.
- **Close Client File**: The 'Close Client File' activity marks the formal conclusion of the client's case, documenting the reason for closure such as goals met or services declined.
- **Refer to Specialist**: The 'Refer to Specialist' activity involves directing the client to a specialized professional like a psychiatrist, addiction counselor, or legal aid based on specific needs.

## Attributes
- **Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High' specifies the assessed level of risk associated with the client, which can be Low, Medium, or High to guide the intensity of interventions.
- **Service Plan Duration: integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Service Plan Duration: integer between 1 and 12' represents the planned length of the service plan in months, ranging from 1 to 12 to accommodate varying client needs.
- **Funding Amount Requested: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Funding Amount Requested: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' indicates the monetary value requested for funding the client's services, with values between 100.0 and 10000.0 to cover program costs.
- **Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family**: The attribute 'Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family' defines the format of counseling provided, which can be Individual, Group, or Family to best suit the client's situation.
- **Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare**: The attribute 'Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare' categorizes the type of additional support coordinated for the client, selectable from Housing, Job Training, or Childcare.
- **Progress Score: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Progress Score: integer between 0 and 100' quantifies the client's advancement on a scale from 0 to 100, used to monitor effectiveness and decide on plan adjustments.
- **Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs**: The attribute 'Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs' explains the rationale for modifying the service plan, with possible values of Progress, Setback, or New Needs.
- **Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative' records the result of the home visit, categorized as Positive, Neutral, or Negative to inform further actions.
- **File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined**: The attribute 'File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined' details why the client file is being closed, with options including Goals Met, Client Moved, or Services Declined.
- **Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid**: The attribute 'Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid' identifies the category of specialist to whom the client is referred, such as Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, or Legal Aid.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for client service management is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Assess Client Needs,' which involves the initial evaluation of the client's requirements and risk factors using the 'Client Risk Level' attribute. This activity must be the first to occur in the process, establishing the foundational context for all subsequent service planning and interventions.
#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Close Client File,' which marks the formal conclusion of the client's case by documenting the 'File Closure Reason' such as goals met, client moved, or services declined. This activity must be the last to occur in the process.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The activity 'Develop Service Plan' is permitted to occur only if an 'Assess Client Needs' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. The 'Develop Service Plan' activity involves creating a tailored service plan using the 'Service Plan Duration' attribute, ensuring that all planning is grounded in a prior thorough assessment of client needs and risk factors.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If an 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity occurs, then a 'Provide Counseling Services' activity must eventually occur after it. The 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity secures the 'Funding Amount Requested,' while 'Provide Counseling Services' delivers sessions of a specified 'Counseling Type' to address the client's emotional and psychological needs, ensuring that funding directly leads to the delivery of core counseling interventions.
2. If an 'Assess Client Needs' activity occurs with the condition that 'Client Risk Level is High,' then a 'Conduct Home Visit' activity must eventually occur after it within 24 hours. The 'Conduct Home Visit' activity performs on-site observation using the 'Home Visit Outcome' attribute, guaranteeing timely in-person assessment for high-risk clients.
3. If a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity occurs with the condition that 'Progress Score < 50,' then an 'Adjust Service Plan' activity must eventually occur after it within 7 days. The 'Monitor Client Progress' activity tracks advancement using the 'Progress Score,' while 'Adjust Service Plan' modifies the plan based on the 'Plan Adjustment Reason,' ensuring that unsatisfactory progress triggers timely plan revisions.
4. If a 'Develop Service Plan' activity occurs with the condition that 'Service Plan Duration > 6,' then a 'Refer to Specialist' activity must eventually occur after it within 30 days. The 'Refer to Specialist' activity directs the client to an appropriate 'Specialist Type' such as a psychiatrist or addiction counselor, ensuring that longer-term plans incorporate necessary specialized expertise.
5. If a 'Conduct Home Visit' activity occurs with the condition that 'Home Visit Outcome is Negative,' then an 'Adjust Service Plan' activity must eventually occur after it within 3 days. This ensures that unfavorable on-site observations promptly lead to modifications in the service approach using the 'Plan Adjustment Reason.'

#### **Succession Constraints**
A 'Provide Counseling Services' activity must be followed by a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity at some point later in the same process instance, while a 'Monitor Client Progress' event can occur only if a 'Provide Counseling Services' event has occurred before it. This bidirectional causal link, using the 'Counseling Type' for counseling and 'Progress Score' for monitoring, ensures that each delivery of counseling logically leads to progress evaluation and that monitoring is always justified by prior counseling, creating a cohesive progression in client support.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraints**
An 'Adjust Service Plan' activity cannot be immediately followed by a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity, and a 'Monitor Client Progress' event cannot be immediately preceded by an 'Adjust Service Plan' event. There must be at least one other event between them. This exclusion, involving the 'Plan Adjustment Reason' for adjustments and 'Progress Score' for monitoring, prevents direct adjacency between plan modifications and progress tracking, thereby maintaining separation and allowing for necessary intermediate actions within the process sequence.
