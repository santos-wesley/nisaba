#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Assess Client Needs**: The 'Assess Client Needs' activity represents the evaluation of a client's needs to determine appropriate services.
- **Develop Service Plan**: The 'Develop Service Plan' activity involves creating a structured plan of services tailored to the client's needs.
- **Obtain Funding Approval**: The 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity involves securing approval for the funding required to provide services to the client.
- **Provide Counseling Services**: The 'Provide Counseling Services' activity involves delivering counseling support to the client.
- **Coordinate Support Services**: The 'Coordinate Support Services' activity involves organizing and arranging additional support services for the client.
- **Monitor Client Progress**: The 'Monitor Client Progress' activity involves tracking and evaluating the client's progress over time.
- **Adjust Service Plan**: The 'Adjust Service Plan' activity involves modifying the client's service plan based on new information or circumstances.
- **Conduct Home Visit**: The 'Conduct Home Visit' activity involves visiting the client's home to assess their living situation and needs.
- **Close Client File**: The 'Close Client File' activity involves finalizing and closing the client's case file.
- **Refer to Specialist**: The 'Refer to Specialist' activity involves referring the client to a specialist for additional expert services.

## Attributes
- **Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Client Risk Level: Low, Medium, High' represents the assessed level of risk associated with the client.
- **Service Plan Duration: Integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Service Plan Duration: Integer between 1 and 12' represents the duration, in months, of the client's service plan.
- **Funding Amount Requested: Float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Funding Amount Requested: Float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the amount of funding requested for the client's services.
- **Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family**: The attribute 'Counseling Type: Individual, Group, Family' specifies the type of counseling provided to the client.
- **Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare**: The attribute 'Support Service Type: Housing, Job Training, Childcare' specifies the type of support service coordinated for the client.
- **Progress Score: Integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Progress Score: Integer between 0 and 100' represents a numeric score indicating the client's progress.
- **Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs**: The attribute 'Plan Adjustment Reason: Progress, Setback, New Needs' specifies the reason for adjusting the client's service plan.
- **Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative**: The attribute 'Home Visit Outcome: Positive, Neutral, Negative' represents the outcome assessment of a home visit.
- **File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined**: The attribute 'File Closure Reason: Goals Met, Client Moved, Services Declined' specifies the reason for closing the client's file.
- **Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid**: The attribute 'Specialist Type: Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, Legal Aid' specifies the type of specialist to whom the client is referred.

## Constraints


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the client services process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Assess Client Needs,' which involves evaluating the client's circumstances to determine the appropriate services required, with the assessment capturing the client's 'Client Risk Level' as Low, Medium, or High. This initial evaluation establishes the foundation upon which all subsequent service decisions are built, setting the tone for the entire client engagement.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Close Client File,' which involves finalizing and closing the client's case, documented through the 'File Closure Reason' attribute capturing whether goals were met, the client moved, or services were declined. This activity must be the last to occur, bringing the client's journey through the service process to a definitive and well-documented close.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Monitor Client Progress' activity must occur at least once during the process, with a corresponding 'Progress Score' recorded between 0 and 100. This requirement adds depth to the process by ensuring that the client's development is tracked and evaluated at some point, reinforcing accountability throughout service delivery.

#### **Absence Constraint**
In contrast to activities that must occur, the 'Refer to Specialist' activity is constrained such that it cannot occur more than twice within the same process instance. This exclusion rule reinforces process integrity by preventing excessive or redundant specialist referrals, ensuring that referrals remain purposeful and targeted.

#### **Exactly Constraint**
Summarizing the precision required for planning activities, the 'Develop Service Plan' activity must occur exactly once in the process, capturing a 'Service Plan Duration' between 1 and 12 months. This fixed boundary ensures that each client receives a single, clearly defined service plan, avoiding duplication or conflicting planning efforts.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraint**
Establishing a cause-effect dependency, if the 'Adjust Service Plan' activity occurs, then the 'Monitor Client Progress' activity must also occur somewhere in the process, whether before or after. This ensures that any adjustment to the service plan is coherently linked to an underlying progress evaluation, reflecting a data-driven approach to client care.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
1. Setting up essential groundwork, the activity 'Develop Service Plan' is permitted to occur only if an 'Assess Client Needs' activity has occurred before it, with the condition that the 'Client Risk Level' has been properly assessed. This background establishes that no service plan can be created without first understanding the client's needs and risk profile.
2. Similarly, the activity 'Provide Counseling Services' is permitted to occur only if a 'Develop Service Plan' activity has occurred before it. This ensures that counseling delivery is always grounded in a structured, pre-established plan tailored to the client.
3. The activity 'Coordinate Support Services' is permitted to occur only if an 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity has occurred before it, with the 'Funding Amount Requested' having been secured. This sets the necessary financial context before additional support services can be arranged.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. Following a clear sequence, if the 'Assess Client Needs' activity occurs with a 'Client Risk Level' of High, then the 'Conduct Home Visit' activity must occur afterward, capturing a 'Home Visit Outcome' of Positive, Neutral, or Negative. This ensures that high-risk clients receive timely in-person evaluation following their initial assessment.
2. If the 'Develop Service Plan' activity occurs, then the 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity must occur afterward, with a 'Funding Amount Requested' between 100.0 and 10000.0. This maintains a cohesive temporal structure, ensuring that funding is secured to support the plan that was developed.

#### **Succession Constraints**
Establishing a causal link between monitoring and adjustment, whenever a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity occurs with a recorded 'Progress Score,' it must eventually be followed by an 'Adjust Service Plan' activity capturing a 'Plan Adjustment Reason' of Progress, Setback, or New Needs, and conversely, any 'Adjust Service Plan' activity must be preceded by a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity. This creates a cohesive progression where plan modifications are always grounded in observed client outcomes.

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Alternate Response Constraint**
Promoting balanced pairing, each occurrence of the 'Refer to Specialist' activity, capturing a 'Specialist Type' of Psychiatrist, Addiction Counselor, or Legal Aid, must be followed by a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity before another referral can occur. This condition-consequence relationship ensures that each specialist referral is followed up with progress tracking before any subsequent referral takes place.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraint**
Capturing an immediate succession requirement, whenever the 'Conduct Home Visit' activity occurs with a 'Home Visit Outcome' of Negative, it must be immediately followed by the 'Adjust Service Plan' activity with a 'Plan Adjustment Reason' of Setback, with no intervening activities. This cause-and-effect relationship ensures that negative home visit findings trigger swift, direct corrective action in the service plan.

#### **Chain Precedence Constraint**
Establishing explicit immediate dependency, the 'Close Client File' activity must be directly preceded by a 'Monitor Client Progress' activity, with no other activities occurring between them. This ensures that the final closure of a client's file is always immediately grounded in the most recent progress evaluation.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraint**
Reflecting mutual dependency, the 'Coordinate Support Services' activity and the 'Obtain Funding Approval' activity must occur together within the same process instance. This ensures that whenever support services—such as Housing, Job Training, or Childcare—are arranged, corresponding funding approval is also present, and vice versa.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Choice Constraint**
Introducing flexibility, the process allows either the 'Provide Counseling Services' activity or the 'Refer to Specialist' activity to occur, permitting alternative paths for delivering client support depending on the nature of their needs.

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraint**
Setting an exclusion rule to maintain process integrity, the 'Close Client File' activity with a 'File Closure Reason' of Goals Met and the 'Adjust Service Plan' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. This prevents the contradictory scenario of modifying a plan after the client's goals have already been successfully met and the file closed.

### **Negative Dependency Constraints**

#### **Not Responded Existence Constraint**
Refining the model by disallowing specific contingencies, if the 'Close Client File' activity occurs with a 'File Closure Reason' of Client Moved, then the 'Refer to Specialist' activity must not occur in the same process instance. This ensures that specialist referrals are not inappropriately triggered after a client has already relocated away from the service area.

### **Negative Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Precedence Constraint**
Preserving the intended sequence, the 'Close Client File' activity must not occur before the 'Develop Service Plan' activity. This restriction ensures that a client's file cannot be closed prior to the establishment of a formal service plan, maintaining logical process integrity.

#### **Not Response Constraint**
Maintaining the model's intended progression, if the 'Close Client File' activity occurs, then the 'Assess Client Needs' activity must not occur afterward within the same process instance. This ensures that once a client's file has been closed, no further needs assessments are inappropriately initiated for that case.

### **Negative Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Chain Succession Constraint**
Excluding specific immediate sequences to refine the process model, the 'Close Client File' activity and the 'Assess Client Needs' activity must not immediately precede or follow each other. This ensures a clear structural separation between the closing of one client engagement and the initiation of a new needs assessment, preserving procedural clarity.
