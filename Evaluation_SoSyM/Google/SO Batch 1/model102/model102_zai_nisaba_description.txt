#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Submit Timesheet**: The 'Submit Timesheet' activity represents the action of an employee submitting their timesheet for payroll processing.
- **Approve Timesheet**: The 'Approve Timesheet' activity involves reviewing and approving a submitted timesheet for further payroll processing.
- **Calculate Gross Pay**: The 'Calculate Gross Pay' activity computes the gross pay based on the hours worked and the pay rate.
- **Calculate Deductions**: The 'Calculate Deductions' activity determines the tax and insurance deductions from the gross pay.
- **Calculate Net Pay**: The 'Calculate Net Pay' activity computes the net pay by subtracting all deductions from the gross pay.
- **Generate Paystub**: The 'Generate Paystub' activity creates a paystub document detailing the employee's earnings and deductions.
- **Process Payment**: The 'Process Payment' activity executes the payment to the employee based on the calculated net pay and selected payment method.
- **Update Payroll Records**: The 'Update Payroll Records' activity updates the payroll system records with the latest payment information.
- **Review Payroll Report**: The 'Review Payroll Report' activity involves reviewing the payroll report to ensure accuracy and compliance.
- **Distribute Paystub**: The 'Distribute Paystub' activity delivers the generated paystub to the employee.

## Attributes
- **Hours Worked: integer between 0 and 80**: The attribute 'Hours Worked: integer between 0 and 80' represents the number of hours an employee has worked, ranging from 0 to 80 hours.
- **Pay Rate: float between 10.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Pay Rate: float between 10.0 and 100.0' represents the hourly pay rate of an employee, ranging from 10.0 to 100.0.
- **Gross Pay: float between 0.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Gross Pay: float between 0.0 and 10000.0' represents the total gross pay before deductions, ranging from 0.0 to 10000.0.
- **Tax Deduction: float between 0.0 and 5000.0**: The attribute 'Tax Deduction: float between 0.0 and 5000.0' represents the amount deducted for taxes from the gross pay, ranging from 0.0 to 5000.0.
- **Insurance Deduction: float between 0.0 and 1000.0**: The attribute 'Insurance Deduction: float between 0.0 and 1000.0' represents the amount deducted for insurance from the gross pay, ranging from 0.0 to 1000.0.
- **Net Pay: float between 0.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Net Pay: float between 0.0 and 10000.0' represents the final net pay after all deductions, ranging from 0.0 to 10000.0.
- **Timesheet Status: Submitted, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Timesheet Status: Submitted, Approved, Rejected' specifies the possible statuses of a timesheet, which can be 'Submitted', 'Approved', or 'Rejected'.
- **Payment Method: Direct Deposit, Check**: The attribute 'Payment Method: Direct Deposit, Check' specifies the method of payment to the employee, which can be 'Direct Deposit' or 'Check'.
- **Employee ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Employee ID: integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identification number of an employee, ranging from 1000 to 9999.
