# Payroll Processing Model Description

This model describes a payroll processing workflow with ten activities, each carrying specific data attributes, along with control-flow rules governing their execution order.

## Activities and Data

The process involves the following activities, each associated with relevant data fields:

- **Submit Timesheet**: records the Employee ID, Hours Worked, and Timesheet Status.
- **Approve Timesheet**: records the Employee ID and Timesheet Status.
- **Calculate Gross Pay**: uses Hours Worked and Pay Rate to determine Gross Pay.
- **Calculate Deductions**: uses Gross Pay to determine Tax Deduction and Insurance Deduction.
- **Calculate Net Pay**: uses Gross Pay, Tax Deduction, and Insurance Deduction to determine Net Pay.
- **Generate Paystub**: no bound data attributes.
- **Process Payment**: records the Employee ID, Net Pay, and Payment Method.
- **Update Payroll Records**: no bound data attributes.
- **Review Payroll Report**: no bound data attributes.
- **Distribute Paystub**: no bound data attributes.

### Data Types and Ranges

- **Employee ID**: integer between 1000 and 9999
- **Hours Worked**: integer between 0 and 80
- **Pay Rate**: float between 10.0 and 100.0
- **Gross Pay**: float between 0.0 and 10000.0
- **Tax Deduction**: float between 0.0 and 5000.0
- **Insurance Deduction**: float between 0.0 and 1000.0
- **Net Pay**: float between 0.0 and 10000.0
- **Timesheet Status**: one of Submitted, Approved, Rejected
- **Payment Method**: one of Direct Deposit, Check

## Control-Flow Rules

1. **Process initiation**: The case must begin with **Submit Timesheet**.

2. **Sequential dependencies (Response relations)**: Each of the following pairs enforces that once the first activity occurs, the second activity must eventually follow it later in the trace:
   - After **Submit Timesheet**, an **Approve Timesheet** must eventually occur.
   - After **Approve Timesheet**, a **Calculate Gross Pay** must eventually occur.
   - After **Calculate Gross Pay**, a **Calculate Deductions** must eventually occur.
   - After **Calculate Deductions**, a **Calculate Net Pay** must eventually occur.
   - After **Calculate Net Pay**, a **Process Payment** must eventually occur.
   - After **Process Payment**, an **Update Payroll Records** must eventually occur.
   - After **Update Payroll Records**, a **Review Payroll Report** must eventually occur.
   - After **Process Payment**, a **Distribute Paystub** must eventually occur.

3. **Conditional exception (data-aware constraint)**: Normally, **Approve Timesheet** would not need to be followed by another **Submit Timesheet**. However, this restriction is lifted specifically when the Timesheet Status is **Rejected** — in that case, a new **Submit Timesheet** is allowed (and expected) to follow, enabling resubmission of a corrected timesheet.

4. **Timing constraint**: The response relation between **Process Payment** and **Distribute Paystub** must be satisfied within a time window of 24 hours — that is, paystub distribution must occur within 24 hours after payment is processed.

## Summary

This model enforces a **linear, sequential payroll process**: timesheets are submitted, approved (or rejected and resubmitted), pay is calculated (gross, deductions, net), payments are processed, records updated, and paystubs distributed within a strict 24-hour window—while payroll reports are reviewed as the final step.
