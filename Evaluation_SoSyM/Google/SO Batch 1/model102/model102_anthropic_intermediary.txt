## Activities
- **Submit Timesheet**: The 'Submit Timesheet' activity represents an employee submitting their worked hours for a given period.
- **Approve Timesheet**: The 'Approve Timesheet' activity involves a manager or supervisor reviewing and approving a submitted timesheet.
- **Calculate Gross Pay**: The 'Calculate Gross Pay' activity involves computing the total earnings of an employee before deductions, based on hours worked and pay rate.
- **Calculate Deductions**: The 'Calculate Deductions' activity involves determining the tax and insurance deductions to be subtracted from an employee's gross pay.
- **Calculate Net Pay**: The 'Calculate Net Pay' activity involves computing the final take-home pay for an employee after all deductions have been subtracted from the gross pay.
- **Generate Paystub**: The 'Generate Paystub' activity involves creating a detailed pay statement document for the employee reflecting earnings and deductions.
- **Process Payment**: The 'Process Payment' activity involves executing the actual payment transaction to the employee based on the calculated net pay.
- **Update Payroll Records**: The 'Update Payroll Records' activity involves recording the completed payroll transaction details into the payroll system.
- **Review Payroll Report**: The 'Review Payroll Report' activity involves examining the payroll report for accuracy and compliance after records have been updated.
- **Distribute Paystub**: The 'Distribute Paystub' activity involves delivering the generated paystub to the employee.

## Attributes
- **Hours Worked: integer between 0 and 80**: The attribute 'Hours Worked: Integer between 0 and 80' represents the number of hours an employee has worked in the given pay period.
- **Pay Rate: float between 10.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Pay Rate: Float between 10.0 and 100.0' represents the hourly rate of pay for the employee.
- **Gross Pay: float between 0.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Gross Pay: Float between 0.0 and 10000.0' represents the total earnings of the employee before any deductions.
- **Tax Deduction: float between 0.0 and 5000.0**: The attribute 'Tax Deduction: Float between 0.0 and 5000.0' represents the amount deducted from gross pay for taxes.
- **Insurance Deduction: float between 0.0 and 1000.0**: The attribute 'Insurance Deduction: Float between 0.0 and 1000.0' represents the amount deducted from gross pay for insurance coverage.
- **Net Pay: float between 0.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Net Pay: Float between 0.0 and 10000.0' represents the final take-home pay for the employee after all deductions are subtracted.
- **Timesheet Status: Submitted, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Timesheet Status: Submitted, Approved, Rejected' specifies the current approval state of a submitted timesheet.
- **Payment Method: Direct Deposit, Check**: The attribute 'Payment Method: Direct Deposit, Check' specifies the method by which the employee's payment is delivered.
- **Employee ID: integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Employee ID: Integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to an employee.
