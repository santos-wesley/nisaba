## Activities
- **Schedule Train**: The 'Schedule Train' activity represents the scheduling of a train, including its timing and version details.
- **Allocate Resources**: The 'Allocate Resources' activity involves assigning resources, such as tracks, to a train.
- **Monitor Train**: The 'Monitor Train' activity includes tracking the current status of a train during its operation.
- **Manage Delay**: The 'Manage Delay' activity involves handling and addressing delays experienced by a train.
- **Perform Maintenance**: The 'Perform Maintenance' activity represents carrying out maintenance tasks on a train.
- **Inspect Track**: The 'Inspect Track' activity involves examining a track to assess its condition and safety.
- **Dispatch Train**: The 'Dispatch Train' activity represents releasing a train for its journey.
- **Receive Train**: The 'Receive Train' activity involves accepting a train upon its arrival, including passenger handling.
- **Update Schedule**: The 'Update Schedule' activity includes modifying an existing train schedule.
- **Cancel Train**: The 'Cancel Train' activity represents the cancellation of a scheduled train along with the reason for cancellation.

## Attributes
- **Train ID: Integer between 1000 and 9999**: The attribute 'Train ID: Integer between 1000 and 9999' represents the unique identification number assigned to a train.
- **Train Status: On Time, Delayed, Cancelled, Completed**: The attribute 'Train Status: On Time, Delayed, Cancelled, Completed' specifies the current operational status of a train.
- **Delay Duration: Float between 0.0 and 300.0**: The attribute 'Delay Duration: Float between 0.0 and 300.0' represents the amount of time, in minutes, that a train has been delayed.
- **Track Number: Integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Track Number: Integer between 1 and 10' represents the identifier of the track allocated or inspected.
- **Maintenance Type: Routine, Emergency, Scheduled**: The attribute 'Maintenance Type: Routine, Emergency, Scheduled' specifies the category of maintenance performed on a train.
- **Inspection Score: Float between 0.0 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Inspection Score: Float between 0.0 and 10.0' represents the numeric score given during a track inspection to assess its condition.
- **Priority Level: High, Medium, Low**: The attribute 'Priority Level: High, Medium, Low' specifies the priority assigned to a train when dispatched.
- **Passenger Count: Integer between 0 and 1500**: The attribute 'Passenger Count: Integer between 0 and 1500' represents the number of passengers on a received train.
- **Reason Cancel: Weather, Maintenance, Technical Issue**: The attribute 'Reason Cancel: Weather, Maintenance, Technical Issue' specifies the cause behind the cancellation of a train.
- **Schedule Version: Integer between 1 and 100**: The attribute 'Schedule Version: Integer between 1 and 100' represents the version number of a train's schedule.
