**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Train Operations Management**

This model describes a railway operations process using a combination of control-flow, data, and temporal constraints. The process revolves around the lifecycle of a train from initial scheduling to final reception or cancellation.

### Activities
The process consists of the following 10 activities:
- **Schedule Train**
- **Allocate Resources**
- **Monitor Train**
- **Manage Delay**
- **Perform Maintenance**
- **Inspect Track**
- **Dispatch Train**
- **Receive Train**
- **Update Schedule**
- **Cancel Train**

### Data Perspectives
Each activity is associated with specific data parameters:

- **Schedule Train**: Train ID, Schedule Version
- **Allocate Resources**: Train ID, Track Number
- **Monitor Train**: Train ID, Train Status
- **Manage Delay**: Train ID, Delay Duration
- **Perform Maintenance**: Train ID, Maintenance Type
- **Inspect Track**: Track Number, Inspection Score
- **Dispatch Train**: Train ID, Priority Level
- **Receive Train**: Train ID, Passenger Count
- **Update Schedule**: Train ID, Schedule Version
- **Cancel Train**: Train ID, Reason Cancel

**Data Domains:**
- Train ID: integer ∈ [1000, 9999]
- Train Status: {On Time, Delayed, Cancelled, Completed}
- Delay Duration: float ∈ [0.0, 300.0]
- Track Number: integer ∈ [1, 10]
- Maintenance Type: {Routine, Emergency, Scheduled}
- Inspection Score: float ∈ [0.0, 10.0]
- Priority Level: {High, Medium, Low}
- Passenger Count: integer ∈ [0, 1500]
- Reason Cancel: {Weather, Maintenance, Technical Issue}
- Schedule Version: integer ∈ [1, 100]

### Constraints

**Control-Flow & Temporal Constraints:**

1. **Init[Schedule Train]**  
   The process must always start with scheduling a train.

2. **Precedence[Dispatch Train, Allocate Resources]**  
   Allocate Resources cannot occur before Dispatch Train has occurred.

3. **Response[Monitor Train, Manage Delay] (with condition)**  
   If a train is **Delayed** (i.e., `A.Train Status is Delayed`), then Manage Delay must eventually occur after Monitor Train.

4. **Chain Precedence[Dispatch Train, Perform Maintenance]**  
   Perform Maintenance must be immediately preceded by Dispatch Train (no other activity in between).

5. **Succession[Inspect Track, Allocate Resources]**  
   Inspect Track must be followed by Allocate Resources, and Allocate Resources must be preceded by Inspect Track (strong bidirectional relation).

6. **Responded Existence[Dispatch Train, Receive Train]**  
   If a train is dispatched, it must eventually be received (somewhere in the trace).

7. **Not Co-Existence[Dispatch Train, Cancel Train]**  
   A train cannot be both dispatched and cancelled in the same process instance.

8. **Not Chain Succession[Update Schedule, Schedule Train]**  
   Update Schedule cannot directly follow Schedule Train (they cannot be executed immediately one after another).

9. **Choice[Dispatch Train, Cancel Train]**  
   At least one of Dispatch Train or Cancel Train must occur.

10. **Absence2[Cancel Train]**  
    Cancel Train can occur **at most twice** in any trace.

---

This model enforces a structured yet flexible railway operations workflow, ensuring safety, resource coordination, and proper handling of delays and cancellations while maintaining strong data awareness.
