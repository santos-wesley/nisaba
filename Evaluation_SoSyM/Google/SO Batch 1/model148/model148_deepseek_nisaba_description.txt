#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Conduct Risk Assessment**: The 'Conduct Risk Assessment' activity represents the process of evaluating potential risks to the organization.
- **Develop Compliance Plan**: The 'Develop Compliance Plan' activity involves creating a plan to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements.
- **Implement Controls**: The 'Implement Controls' activity involves putting in place measures to mitigate identified risks.
- **Monitor Compliance**: The 'Monitor Compliance' activity represents the ongoing observation of compliance with regulations and internal policies.
- **Report Violations**: The 'Report Violations' activity involves documenting and reporting any breaches of compliance.
- **Investigate Violations**: The 'Investigate Violations' activity involves examining reported breaches to determine their cause and impact.
- **Take Corrective Action**: The 'Take Corrective Action' activity involves implementing measures to address and rectify compliance violations.
- **Review Compliance Plan**: The 'Review Compliance Plan' activity involves evaluating the effectiveness of the compliance plan.
- **Approve Compliance Plan**: The 'Approve Compliance Plan' activity involves formally authorizing the compliance plan.
- **Train Employees**: The 'Train Employees' activity involves providing training to staff on compliance-related topics.

## Attributes
- **Risk Level: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Risk Level: Low, Medium, High' specifies the level of risk associated with a particular assessment.
- **Compliance Status: Compliant, Non-Compliant, Pending**: The attribute 'Compliance Status: Compliant, Non-Compliant, Pending' indicates the current compliance state of an entity.
- **Violation Severity: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Violation Severity: integer between 1 and 5' represents the severity level of a compliance violation on a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Financial Impact: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Financial Impact: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the monetary impact of a violation in dollars.
- **Control Type: Preventative, Detective, Corrective**: The attribute 'Control Type: Preventative, Detective, Corrective' specifies the type of control implemented.
- **Training Hours: integer between 0 and 40**: The attribute 'Training Hours: integer between 0 and 40' represents the number of hours of training provided to employees.
- **Reporting Channel: Email, Phone, Online Portal**: The attribute 'Reporting Channel: Email, Phone, Online Portal' specifies the method used to report a violation.
- **Assessment Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Assessment Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the score resulting from a risk assessment.
- **Plan Status: Draft, Approved, Implemented**: The attribute 'Plan Status: Draft, Approved, Implemented' indicates the current status of a compliance plan.
- **Number of Violations: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Number of Violations: integer between 0 and 100' represents the count of violations that have been reported.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the compliance management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Conduct Risk Assessment,' which involves evaluating potential risks to the organization and requires the attributes 'Risk Level' and 'Assessment Score.' This initial step sets the foundation for all subsequent compliance activities.

### **Frequency Constraints**
#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Train Employees' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that staff receive training on compliance-related topics, adding depth and reliability to the process by fostering a knowledgeable workforce.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**
#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Develop Compliance Plan' activity occurs, then an 'Approve Compliance Plan' activity must eventually occur after it. This sequence ensures that any compliance plan developed is formally authorized, creating a cohesive flow from planning to approval.
2. If a 'Report Violations' activity occurs, then an 'Investigate Violations' activity must eventually occur after it. This ordered flow ensures that reported breaches are examined, maintaining a structured response to compliance issues.
3. If an 'Investigate Violations' activity occurs, then a 'Take Corrective Action' activity must eventually occur after it. This sequence ensures that investigations lead to remedial measures, reinforcing the process's temporal structure.
4. If a 'Conduct Risk Assessment' activity occurs with the condition 'Risk Level is High,' then an 'Implement Controls' activity must occur within 0 to 30 days. This ensures that high-risk assessments promptly trigger the implementation of controls, maintaining timely risk mitigation.
5. If a 'Monitor Compliance' activity occurs with the condition 'Compliance Status is Non-Compliant,' then a 'Take Corrective Action' activity must occur within 0 to 7 days. This ensures that non-compliance is swiftly addressed, preserving process integrity.
6. If a 'Report Violations' activity occurs with the condition 'Number of Violations > 5,' then a 'Review Compliance Plan' activity must occur within 0 to 14 days. This ensures that a high volume of violations triggers a timely review of the compliance plan.
7. If an 'Investigate Violations' activity occurs with the condition 'Financial Impact > 10000,' then a 'Review Compliance Plan' activity must occur within 0 to 3 days. This ensures that financially significant violations prompt an urgent review of the compliance plan.

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**
#### **Alternate Succession Constraints**
Each occurrence of an 'Approve Compliance Plan' activity must eventually be followed by a 'Review Compliance Plan' activity, and each occurrence of a 'Review Compliance Plan' must be preceded by an 'Approve Compliance Plan,' with no intermediate occurrences of either activity between them. Additionally, the 'Review Compliance Plan' activity must occur within 0 to 365 days after the 'Approve Compliance Plan' activity. This enforces an ordered structure with unique pairings, ensuring that each approval is followed by a distinct review, enhancing process coherence.
