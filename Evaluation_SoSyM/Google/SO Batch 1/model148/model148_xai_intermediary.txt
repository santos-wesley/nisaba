## Activities
- **Conduct Risk Assessment**: The 'Conduct Risk Assessment' activity represents the initial evaluation of potential risks in the compliance process.
- **Develop Compliance Plan**: The 'Develop Compliance Plan' activity involves creating a structured plan to address identified risks and ensure regulatory adherence.
- **Implement Controls**: The 'Implement Controls' activity focuses on putting in place specific measures to mitigate risks as outlined in the compliance plan.
- **Monitor Compliance**: The 'Monitor Compliance' activity entails ongoing observation and assessment to ensure that controls are effective and the organization remains compliant.
- **Report Violations**: The 'Report Violations' activity includes documenting and communicating any instances of non-compliance or breaches identified during monitoring.
- **Investigate Violations**: The 'Investigate Violations' activity involves a thorough examination of reported violations to determine their causes and impacts.
- **Take Corrective Action**: The 'Take Corrective Action' activity consists of implementing remedies to address and resolve identified violations.
- **Review Compliance Plan**: The 'Review Compliance Plan' activity is the process of evaluating the existing compliance plan for effectiveness and necessary updates.
- **Approve Compliance Plan**: The 'Approve Compliance Plan' activity requires formal authorization of the compliance plan by relevant authorities.
- **Train Employees**: The 'Train Employees' activity involves educating staff on compliance policies, procedures, and their responsibilities.

## Attributes
- **Risk Level: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Risk Level: Low, Medium, High' indicates the severity categorization of identified risks in the compliance assessment.
- **Compliance Status: Compliant, Non-Compliant, Pending**: The attribute 'Compliance Status: Compliant, Non-Compliant, Pending' reflects the current state of adherence to regulatory requirements.
- **Violation Severity: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Violation Severity: integer between 1 and 5' quantifies the seriousness of a compliance violation on a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Financial Impact: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Financial Impact: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' measures the monetary consequences resulting from a compliance violation.
- **Control Type: Preventative, Detective, Corrective**: The attribute 'Control Type: Preventative, Detective, Corrective' classifies the type of control measures implemented to manage risks.
- **Training Hours: integer between 0 and 40**: The attribute 'Training Hours: integer between 0 and 40' specifies the duration of compliance training provided to employees, ranging from 0 to 40 hours.
- **Reporting Channel: Email, Phone, Online Portal**: The attribute 'Reporting Channel: Email, Phone, Online Portal' denotes the method used to report compliance violations.
- **Assessment Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Assessment Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the numerical evaluation score from the risk assessment, on a scale from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Plan Status: Draft, Approved, Implemented**: The attribute 'Plan Status: Draft, Approved, Implemented' tracks the progress stage of the compliance plan.
- **Number of Violations: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Number of Violations: integer between 0 and 100' counts the instances of compliance breaches, ranging from 0 to 100.
