#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Monitor Seismic Activity**: The 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity involves continuously tracking and recording seismic events to detect earthquakes and other ground movements.
- **Issue Tsunami Alert**: The 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity entails issuing alerts to warn populations and authorities about an impending tsunami threat.
- **Evacuate Coastal Areas**: The 'Evacuate Coastal Areas' activity involves coordinating and executing the evacuation of populations from coastal zones at risk of tsunami impact.
- **Dispatch Emergency Services**: The 'Dispatch Emergency Services' activity includes mobilizing and deploying emergency response teams to affected areas.
- **Establish Emergency Shelters**: The 'Establish Emergency Shelters' activity involves setting up temporary shelters to accommodate evacuees and displaced individuals.
- **Assess Infrastructure Damage**: The 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity entails evaluating the extent of damage to infrastructure such as roads, bridges, buildings, and utilities following a disaster.
- **Provide Medical Assistance**: The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity includes delivering medical care and treatment to individuals injured during the disaster.
- **Distribute Relief Supplies**: The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity involves distributing essential supplies such as food, water, and medical materials to affected populations.
- **Restore Communication Networks**: The 'Restore Communication Networks' activity entails repairing and re-establishing communication infrastructure to ensure connectivity in affected areas.
- **Conduct Search and Rescue**: The 'Conduct Search and Rescue' activity involves locating and rescuing individuals who are trapped, missing, or in distress due to the disaster.

## Attributes
- **Alert Level: Watch, Warning, Cancel**: The attribute 'Alert Level: Watch, Warning, Cancel' specifies the level of tsunami alert issued, which can be 'Watch', 'Warning', or 'Cancel'.
- **Magnitude: float between 0.0 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Magnitude: float between 0.0 and 10.0' represents the seismic magnitude of an earthquake measured on a scale from 0.0 to 10.0.
- **Estimated Arrival Time: integer between 0 and 360**: The attribute 'Estimated Arrival Time: integer between 0 and 360' represents the estimated time of tsunami arrival in minutes, ranging from 0 to 360.
- **Number of Evacuees: integer between 0 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Number of Evacuees: integer between 0 and 1000000' represents the count of individuals evacuated from coastal areas, ranging from 0 to 1,000,000.
- **Damage Severity: Minor, Moderate, Severe**: The attribute 'Damage Severity: Minor, Moderate, Severe' specifies the severity level of infrastructure damage, categorized as 'Minor', 'Moderate', or 'Severe'.
- **Number of Injuries: integer between 0 and 5000**: The attribute 'Number of Injuries: integer between 0 and 5000' represents the count of individuals injured during the disaster, ranging from 0 to 5,000.
- **Number of Shelters: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Number of Shelters: integer between 0 and 100' represents the number of emergency shelters established, ranging from 0 to 100.
- **Communication Status: Operational, Partially Operational, Down**: The attribute 'Communication Status: Operational, Partially Operational, Down' specifies the operational state of communication networks, which can be 'Operational', 'Partially Operational', or 'Down'.
- **Number of Rescued: integer between 0 and 1000**: The attribute 'Number of Rescued: integer between 0 and 1000' represents the count of individuals rescued during search and rescue operations, ranging from 0 to 1,000.
- **Supply Cost: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Supply Cost: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the cost of relief supplies distributed, measured as a floating-point value from 0.0 to 1,000,000.0.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the tsunami emergency response process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Monitor Seismic Activity,' which involves continuously tracking and recording seismic events to detect earthquakes and other ground movements. This activity requires the 'Magnitude' attribute, representing the seismic magnitude of an earthquake measured on a scale from 0.0 to 10.0. Establishing this initial context is critical, as seismic monitoring serves as the foundational trigger for the entire emergency response workflow.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The activity 'Dispatch Emergency Services' is permitted to occur only if a 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity, which includes delivering medical care and treatment to individuals injured during the disaster, is bound to the 'Number of Injuries' attribute representing the count of injured individuals ranging from 0 to 5,000. This precedence requirement sets up an initial context where medical assistance must be established before emergency services can be dispatched, guiding the logical sequence within the model. Any 'Dispatch Emergency Services' events that occur without prior 'Provide Medical Assistance' are violations of the constraint. Multiple 'Provide Medical Assistance' events can occur, and any number of events can happen between a medical assistance event and a subsequent dispatch.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If an 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity occurs, then an 'Evacuate Coastal Areas' activity must eventually occur after the alert event at some point in the same process instance. The 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity entails issuing alerts to warn populations and authorities about an impending tsunami threat and is bound to the 'Alert Level' (Watch, Warning, or Cancel) and 'Estimated Arrival Time' (0 to 360 minutes) attributes. The 'Evacuate Coastal Areas' activity involves coordinating and executing the evacuation of populations from coastal zones and is bound to the 'Number of Evacuees' attribute ranging from 0 to 1,000,000. This defines an ordered flow where evacuation follows alert issuance, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure in the model. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between, and a single evacuation can fulfill the obligation for all preceding alerts.

2. If a 'Restore Communication Networks' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'Communication Status' is 'Down', then a 'Restore Communication Networks' activity must eventually occur after that event with the correlation condition that 'Communication Status' is 'Operational' at some point in the same process instance. The 'Restore Communication Networks' activity entails repairing and re-establishing communication infrastructure to ensure connectivity in affected areas. This conditional response ensures that when communication networks are detected as down, a subsequent restoration effort must bring them to operational status. Multiple restoration events with 'Down' status can be pending simultaneously, and a single event with 'Operational' status can fulfill all preceding obligations.

3. If a 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'Magnitude' is greater than 7, then an 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity must eventually occur after that monitoring event at some point in the same process instance. This conditional response establishes that significant seismic events—those with magnitude exceeding 7.0—necessitate the issuance of a tsunami alert, creating a critical cause-and-effect link between detection and warning. Multiple monitoring events with magnitude greater than 7 can be pending at the same time, and a single alert can fulfill the obligation for all preceding high-magnitude events.

4. If an 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity occurs, then an 'Evacuate Coastal Areas' activity must eventually occur after the alert event within 0 to 60 minutes in the same process instance. This time-bounded response refines the general alert-evacuation relationship by imposing a strict 60-minute deadline, ensuring that evacuation is executed within the critical window before tsunami arrival. There is no requirement on what events may occur in between, and a single evacuation can fulfill the obligation for all preceding alerts within the time constraint.

5. If a 'Dispatch Emergency Services' activity occurs, then a 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity must eventually occur after the dispatch event within 0 to 24 hours in the same process instance. This time-bounded response ensures that medical assistance follows the dispatch of emergency services within one day, maintaining the process's momentum and ensuring timely care delivery. Multiple dispatch events can be pending simultaneously, and a single medical assistance event can fulfill all preceding obligations within the 24-hour window.

6. If an 'Establish Emergency Shelters' activity occurs, then a 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity must eventually occur after the shelter establishment event within 0 to 48 hours in the same process instance. The 'Establish Emergency Shelters' activity involves setting up temporary shelters to accommodate evacuees and is bound to the 'Number of Shelters' attribute ranging from 0 to 100. The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity involves distributing essential supplies such as food, water, and medical materials and is bound to the 'Supply Cost' attribute ranging from 0.0 to 1,000,000.0. This time-bounded response ensures that relief supplies reach shelters within two days of their establishment, maintaining the humanitarian flow of the process.

7. If an 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity occurs, then a 'Restore Communication Networks' activity must eventually occur after the assessment event within 0 to 72 hours in the same process instance. The 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity entails evaluating the extent of damage to infrastructure and is bound to the 'Damage Severity' attribute categorized as Minor, Moderate, or Severe. This time-bounded response ensures that communication network restoration follows damage assessment within three days, maintaining connectivity recovery efforts in the process's defined flow.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Not Co-Existence Constraints**
The 'Conduct Search and Rescue' activity and the 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity cannot both occur within the same process instance. The 'Conduct Search and Rescue' activity involves locating and rescuing individuals who are trapped, missing, or in distress and is bound to the 'Number of Rescued' attribute ranging from 0 to 1,000. The 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity evaluates the extent of damage to infrastructure such as roads, bridges, buildings, and utilities. This exclusion enforces a mutual exclusion between search and rescue operations and infrastructure damage assessment, maintaining process integrity by preventing conflicting events. If 'Conduct Search and Rescue' occurs one or more times, 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' must not occur at all in that process instance, and vice versa. It is acceptable for neither event to occur, allowing for different process variants based on which event, if any, takes place.
