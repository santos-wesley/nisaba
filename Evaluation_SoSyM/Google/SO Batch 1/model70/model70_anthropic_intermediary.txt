## Activities
- **Monitor Seismic Activity**: The 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity represents the continuous monitoring of seismic events to detect potential earthquakes or tsunami-triggering activity.
- **Issue Tsunami Alert**: The 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity involves issuing an official alert to warn of a potential tsunami threat.
- **Evacuate Coastal Areas**: The 'Evacuate Coastal Areas' activity involves the evacuation of people from coastal regions at risk of tsunami impact.
- **Dispatch Emergency Services**: The 'Dispatch Emergency Services' activity involves sending emergency response teams to affected areas.
- **Establish Emergency Shelters**: The 'Establish Emergency Shelters' activity involves setting up shelters to house displaced individuals during the emergency.
- **Assess Infrastructure Damage**: The 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity involves evaluating the extent of damage to infrastructure caused by the disaster.
- **Provide Medical Assistance**: The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity involves delivering medical care and treatment to affected individuals.
- **Distribute Relief Supplies**: The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity involves the distribution of essential supplies to affected populations.
- **Restore Communication Networks**: The 'Restore Communication Networks' activity involves repairing and reinstating communication infrastructure affected by the disaster.
- **Conduct Search and Rescue**: The 'Conduct Search and Rescue' activity involves searching for and rescuing individuals trapped or stranded due to the disaster.

## Attributes
- **Magnitude: Float between 0.0 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Magnitude: Float between 0.0 and 10.0' represents the measured strength of a seismic event.
- **Alert Level: Watch, Warning, Cancel**: The attribute 'Alert Level: Watch, Warning, Cancel' specifies the level of the tsunami alert issued.
- **Estimated Arrival Time: Integer between 0 and 360**: The attribute 'Estimated Arrival Time: Integer between 0 and 360' represents the estimated time, in minutes, for the tsunami to arrive at the coast.
- **Number of Evacuees: Integer between 0 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Number of Evacuees: Integer between 0 and 1000000' represents the number of people evacuated from coastal areas.
- **Damage Severity: Minor, Moderate, Severe**: The attribute 'Damage Severity: Minor, Moderate, Severe' specifies the level of severity of the infrastructure damage assessed.
- **Number of Injuries: Integer between 0 and 5000**: The attribute 'Number of Injuries: Integer between 0 and 5000' represents the number of individuals injured due to the disaster.
- **Number of Shelters: Integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Number of Shelters: Integer between 0 and 100' represents the number of emergency shelters established.
- **Communication Status: Operational, Partially Operational, Down**: The attribute 'Communication Status: Operational, Partially Operational, Down' specifies the current operational state of communication networks.
- **Number of Rescued: Integer between 0 and 1000**: The attribute 'Number of Rescued: Integer between 0 and 1000' represents the number of individuals rescued during search and rescue operations.
- **Supply Cost: Float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Supply Cost: Float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the monetary cost of relief supplies distributed.
