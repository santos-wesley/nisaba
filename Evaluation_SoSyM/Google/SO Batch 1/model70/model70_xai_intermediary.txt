## Activities
- **Monitor Seismic Activity**: The 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity represents the continuous observation and recording of seismic events to detect potential earthquakes or tsunamis.
- **Issue Tsunami Alert**: The 'Issue Tsunami Alert' activity involves notifying relevant authorities and the public about an impending tsunami based on detected seismic activity.
- **Evacuate Coastal Areas**: The 'Evacuate Coastal Areas' activity includes organizing and executing the relocation of people from coastal regions to safer inland locations.
- **Dispatch Emergency Services**: The 'Dispatch Emergency Services' activity entails sending out rescue teams, medical personnel, and other emergency responders to affected areas.
- **Establish Emergency Shelters**: The 'Establish Emergency Shelters' activity involves setting up temporary housing and facilities for displaced individuals.
- **Assess Infrastructure Damage**: The 'Assess Infrastructure Damage' activity includes evaluating the extent of damage to buildings, roads, and other critical infrastructure post-disaster.
- **Provide Medical Assistance**: The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity represents delivering healthcare services and treatment to injured or affected people.
- **Distribute Relief Supplies**: The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity involves allocating food, water, clothing, and other essential items to those in need.
- **Restore Communication Networks**: The 'Restore Communication Networks' activity includes repairing and re-establishing phone, internet, and other communication systems.
- **Conduct Search and Rescue**: The 'Conduct Search and Rescue' activity entails locating and saving individuals trapped or lost due to the disaster.

## Attributes
- **Alert Level: Watch, Warning, Cancel**: The attribute 'Alert Level: Watch, Warning, Cancel' specifies the severity level of the tsunami alert issued.
- **Magnitude: float between 0.0 and 10.0**: The attribute 'Magnitude: float between 0.0 and 10.0' represents the measured strength of the seismic activity on the Richter scale.
- **Estimated Arrival Time: integer between 0 and 360**: The attribute 'Estimated Arrival Time: integer between 0 and 360' indicates the projected time in minutes until the tsunami reaches the coast.
- **Number of Evacuees: integer between 0 and 1000000**: The attribute 'Number of Evacuees: integer between 0 and 1000000' quantifies the count of people evacuated from coastal areas.
- **Damage Severity: Minor, Moderate, Severe**: The attribute 'Damage Severity: Minor, Moderate, Severe' categorizes the level of harm inflicted on infrastructure and buildings.
- **Number of Injuries: integer between 0 and 5000**: The attribute 'Number of Injuries: integer between 0 and 5000' measures the total number of people injured in the disaster.
- **Number of Shelters: integer between 0 and 100**: The attribute 'Number of Shelters: integer between 0 and 100' counts the temporary shelters established for evacuees.
- **Communication Status: Operational, Partially Operational, Down**: The attribute 'Communication Status: Operational, Partially Operational, Down' describes the current state of communication infrastructure.
- **Number of Rescued: integer between 0 and 1000**: The attribute 'Number of Rescued: integer between 0 and 1000' tracks the number of individuals successfully rescued.
- **Supply Cost: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Supply Cost: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the total monetary expenditure on relief supplies distributed.
