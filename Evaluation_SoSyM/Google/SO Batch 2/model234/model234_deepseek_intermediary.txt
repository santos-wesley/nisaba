## Activities
- **activity Identify Risk**: The 'Identify Risk' activity involves recognizing and documenting potential risks that could affect the project or organization.
- **activity Assess Impact**: The 'Assess Impact' activity evaluates the potential consequences and severity of identified risks.
- **activity Evaluate Probability**: The 'Evaluate Probability' activity determines the likelihood of each identified risk occurring.
- **activity Prioritize Risks**: The 'Prioritize Risks' activity ranks risks based on their impact and probability to determine which require immediate attention.
- **activity Develop Mitigation Plan**: The 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity involves creating strategies and actions to address and reduce identified risks.
- **activity Implement Mitigation**: The 'Implement Mitigation' activity executes the planned mitigation strategies to manage risks.
- **activity Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity involves ongoing tracking and observation of identified risks and their status.
- **activity Review Risk Assessment**: The 'Review Risk Assessment' activity involves re-evaluating the risk landscape and the effectiveness of current risk management efforts.
- **activity Update Risk Register**: The 'Update Risk Register' activity involves modifying the risk register to reflect the latest information on risks and their status.
- **activity Communicate Risks**: The 'Communicate Risks' activity involves sharing risk-related information with relevant stakeholders and risk owners.
- **activity Approve Mitigation Plan**: The 'Approve Mitigation Plan' activity involves obtaining formal approval for the proposed mitigation strategies.
- **activity Document Lessons Learned**: The 'Document Lessons Learned' activity captures insights and knowledge gained from the risk management process for future reference.
- **activity Escalate Critical Risks**: The 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity involves reporting high-severity risks to higher management or decision-makers for immediate action.
- **activity Conduct Risk Workshop**: The 'Conduct Risk Workshop' activity involves organizing collaborative sessions with stakeholders to identify and discuss risks.
- **activity Validate Risk Controls**: The 'Validate Risk Controls' activity involves testing and confirming that implemented risk controls are effective.

## Attributes
- **Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational**: The attribute 'Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational' specifies the classification of risks based on their nature and source.
- **Impact Score: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Impact Score: integer between 1 and 10' represents the severity rating of a risk's potential consequences on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Probability: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Probability: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents the likelihood of a risk occurring, expressed as a decimal value between 0.0 and 1.0.
- **Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted**: The attribute 'Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted' indicates the current state of a risk within the risk management process.
- **Mitigation Cost: float between 0.0 and 100000.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Cost: float between 0.0 and 100000.0' represents the financial cost associated with implementing a mitigation strategy.
- **Review Frequency: integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Review Frequency: integer between 1 and 12' represents the number of times risk reviews are conducted within a given period.
- **Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams**: The attribute 'Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams' specifies the individuals responsible for managing and overseeing specific risks.
- **Risk ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Risk ID: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each risk.
- **Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance**: The attribute 'Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance' specifies the approach taken to address a risk.
- **Exposure Value: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Exposure Value: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the quantified level of risk exposure, calculated as a monetary value.
