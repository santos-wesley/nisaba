#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Identify Risk**: The 'Identify Risk' activity represents the process of recognizing and documenting potential risks that could affect the project or organization.
- **Assess Impact**: The 'Assess Impact' activity involves evaluating the potential consequences and severity of an identified risk.
- **Evaluate Probability**: The 'Evaluate Probability' activity involves determining the likelihood of a risk occurring.
- **Prioritize Risks**: The 'Prioritize Risks' activity involves ranking identified risks based on factors such as impact and probability to determine which require the most attention.
- **Develop Mitigation Plan**: The 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity involves creating a strategy and set of actions to reduce or eliminate the impact of a risk.
- **Implement Mitigation**: The 'Implement Mitigation' activity involves executing the actions defined in the mitigation plan to address a risk.
- **Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity involves continuously tracking identified risks and their mitigation efforts over time.
- **Review Risk Assessment**: The 'Review Risk Assessment' activity involves reassessing previously evaluated risks to verify accuracy and relevance.
- **Update Risk Register**: The 'Update Risk Register' activity involves recording changes or new information regarding risks into the risk register.
- **Communicate Risks**: The 'Communicate Risks' activity involves informing relevant stakeholders about identified risks and their current status.
- **Approve Mitigation Plan**: The 'Approve Mitigation Plan' activity involves obtaining formal authorization for the proposed risk mitigation strategy.
- **Document Lessons Learned**: The 'Document Lessons Learned' activity involves recording insights and knowledge gained from the risk management process for future reference.
- **Escalate Critical Risks**: The 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity involves raising critical risks to higher management or decision-makers for urgent attention.
- **Conduct Risk Workshop**: The 'Conduct Risk Workshop' activity involves organizing a collaborative session to identify, discuss, and analyze risks with stakeholders.
- **Validate Risk Controls**: The 'Validate Risk Controls' activity involves verifying that the controls put in place to manage risks are functioning as intended.

## Attributes
- **Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational**: The attribute 'Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational' specifies the classification category to which a risk belongs.
- **Impact Score: Integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Impact Score: Integer between 1 and 10' represents the severity level of a risk's potential impact.
- **Probability: Float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Probability: Float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents the likelihood of a risk occurring, expressed as a decimal value.
- **Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted**: The attribute 'Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted' specifies the current state of a risk within the risk management process.
- **Mitigation Cost: Float between 0.0 and 100000.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Cost: Float between 0.0 and 100000.0' represents the monetary cost associated with implementing a risk mitigation strategy.
- **Review Frequency: Integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Review Frequency: Integer between 1 and 12' represents how often, in months, a risk should be reviewed.
- **Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams**: The attribute 'Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams' specifies the individual responsible for managing a particular risk.
- **Risk ID: Integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Risk ID: Integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to a risk.
- **Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance**: The attribute 'Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance' specifies the approach chosen to address a given risk.
- **Exposure Value: Float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Exposure Value: Float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the calculated monetary exposure resulting from a risk.

## Interleaved Description
# The MP-Declare process model for the risk management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Identify Risk,' which establishes the foundational context for the entire risk management workflow by recognizing and documenting potential risks that could affect the project or organization. This activity must be the very first to occur in every process instance, setting the stage upon which all subsequent risk management activities unfold.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Absence Constraints**
In contrast to activities that are encouraged to recur, the 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity is restricted from occurring more than twice within the process. This exclusion reinforces process integrity by ensuring that escalation, while necessary for critical matters, does not become an unchecked or overused mechanism, thereby preserving the seriousness and urgency associated with each escalation event.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Cause-Effect Relations (Response-based Dependencies)**
Building upon the initial identification of risk, several dependency relationships establish necessary causal links across the process. When an 'Identify Risk' activity occurs with a 'Risk Category' of 'Financial,' this necessitates that a 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity with a 'Mitigation Cost' greater than 1000 must eventually follow, ensuring that financial risks are met with adequately funded mitigation strategies. Similarly, if an 'Identify Risk' activity occurs with a 'Risk Status' of 'Open,' this triggers the requirement that an 'Implement Mitigation' activity must occur within 0 to 30 days, ensuring swift action on unresolved risks. Furthermore, when an 'Assess Impact' activity reveals an 'Impact Score' greater than 7, this necessitates that an 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity occur within 0 to 24 hours, establishing an urgent cause-and-effect relationship between severe impact assessments and executive attention.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
Setting up the logical sequence of the model, several precedence relationships guide the order in which activities may occur. The 'Identify Risk' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Assess Impact' activity has occurred before it, establishing the initial context for how risks are understood before they are formally logged. Building on this, the 'Evaluate Probability' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Prioritize Risks' activity has occurred before it, and correspondingly, the 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Implement Mitigation' activity has occurred before it, each guiding the logical progression of risk analysis and response within the model.

#### **Response Constraints**
Following this established context, a cohesive temporal structure defines how activities lead into one another. If an 'Assess Impact' activity occurs, then an 'Evaluate Probability' activity must eventually occur afterward, ensuring that impact assessments are always followed by likelihood determinations. In sequence, if a 'Prioritize Risks' activity occurs, then a 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity must eventually follow, and if an 'Implement Mitigation' activity occurs, then a 'Monitor Risks' activity must eventually occur afterward, ensuring continuous oversight of implemented mitigations. Additionally, if a 'Review Risk Assessment' activity occurs, then an 'Update Risk Register' activity must eventually follow, maintaining an ordered flow of reassessment and documentation. Reinforcing the temporal rigor of the model, if a 'Monitor Risks' activity occurs, then a 'Review Risk Assessment' activity must eventually occur within 90 to 180 days, ensuring that monitoring efforts are periodically and formally reassessed within a defined timeframe.

#### **Succession Constraints**
Establishing a causal link that ensures cohesive progression, a 'Monitor Risks' activity must be followed by a 'Review Risk Assessment' activity at some later point, and conversely, a 'Review Risk Assessment' event cannot occur without a prior 'Monitor Risks' event, binding these two activities in a mutually justified sequence. In the same manner, an 'Update Risk Register' activity must be followed by a 'Communicate Risks' activity, and a 'Communicate Risks' event can occur only if an 'Update Risk Register' event has preceded it, ensuring that stakeholders are only informed once the risk register accurately reflects the current state of affairs, thereby creating a logically dependent progression from documentation to communication.
