#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Identify Risk**: The 'Identify Risk' activity represents the process of identifying potential risks within the organization or project.
- **Assess Impact**: The 'Assess Impact' activity involves evaluating the potential consequences and severity of identified risks.
- **Evaluate Probability**: The 'Evaluate Probability' activity consists of determining the likelihood of identified risks occurring.
- **Prioritize Risks**: The 'Prioritize Risks' activity entails ranking identified risks based on their impact and probability to determine their priority for mitigation.
- **Develop Mitigation Plan**: The 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity involves creating a structured plan to address and mitigate identified risks.
- **Implement Mitigation**: The 'Implement Mitigation' activity represents the execution of the mitigation plan to reduce or eliminate identified risks.
- **Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity consists of continuously tracking identified risks and their mitigation status over time.
- **Review Risk Assessment**: The 'Review Risk Assessment' activity involves re-evaluating the risk assessment to ensure it remains accurate and up to date.
- **Update Risk Register**: The 'Update Risk Register' activity entails modifying the risk register to reflect the current status of risks and any changes in their assessment.
- **Communicate Risks**: The 'Communicate Risks' activity involves sharing information about identified risks and their status with relevant stakeholders.
- **Approve Mitigation Plan**: The 'Approve Mitigation Plan' activity represents the formal authorization of a proposed mitigation plan before its implementation.
- **Document Lessons Learned**: The 'Document Lessons Learned' activity consists of recording insights and experiences gained from the risk management process for future reference.
- **Escalate Critical Risks**: The 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity involves raising awareness of high-severity risks to higher levels of management for immediate attention and action.
- **Conduct Risk Workshop**: The 'Conduct Risk Workshop' activity represents organizing and facilitating a collaborative session to identify, assess, and discuss risks with stakeholders.
- **Validate Risk Controls**: The 'Validate Risk Controls' activity entails verifying that implemented risk controls are effective and functioning as intended.

## Attributes
- **Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational**: The attribute 'Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational' specifies the classification of risks into categories such as Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, and Reputational.
- **Impact Score: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Impact Score: integer between 1 and 10' represents the severity of a risk's impact on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Probability: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Probability: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents the likelihood of a risk occurring, expressed as a floating-point value between 0.0 and 1.0.
- **Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted**: The attribute 'Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted' specifies the possible states of a risk, including Open, Mitigated, Closed, and Accepted.
- **Mitigation Cost: float between 0.0 and 100000.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Cost: float between 0.0 and 100000.0' represents the financial cost associated with implementing a mitigation strategy, ranging from 0.0 to 100000.0.
- **Review Frequency: integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Review Frequency: integer between 1 and 12' specifies how often a risk should be reviewed, expressed as an integer between 1 and 12.
- **Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams**: The attribute 'Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams' specifies the individuals responsible for managing a risk, including John Smith, Alice Johnson, and Bob Williams.
- **Risk ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Risk ID: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each risk, ranging from 1 to 1000.
- **Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance**: The attribute 'Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance' specifies the possible approaches to handling a risk, including Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, and Acceptance.
- **Exposure Value: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Exposure Value: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' represents the quantified exposure of a risk, expressed as a floating-point value between 0.0 and 1000000.0.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the risk management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Identify Risk,' which involves identifying potential risks within the organization or project. This activity requires the attributes 'Risk Category' and 'Risk ID' to be specified, establishing the initial context for the entire risk management process.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Absence Constraints**
The 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity must not occur 3 times in the process. This restriction highlights an exclusion by specifying that escalating critical risks to higher levels of management is permissible but limited, thereby reinforcing process integrity and preventing excessive escalations.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
1. The 'Identify Risk' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Assess Impact' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This sets up an initial context where evaluating the potential consequences and severity of risks, linked to the 'Impact Score' and 'Risk ID' attributes, must logically guide the identification of risks.
2. The 'Evaluate Probability' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Prioritize Risks' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This ensures that the likelihood of risks occurring, associated with the 'Probability' and 'Risk ID' attributes, is evaluated only after risks have been ranked based on their impact and probability.
3. The 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity is permitted to occur only if an 'Implement Mitigation' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This establishes a context where creating a structured plan to address risks, linked to the 'Risk ID', 'Mitigation Strategy', and 'Mitigation Cost' attributes, is guided by prior mitigation execution efforts.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If an 'Assess Impact' activity occurs, then an 'Evaluate Probability' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This defines an ordered flow where determining the likelihood of risks follows the evaluation of their consequences, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure in the assessment phase.
2. If a 'Prioritize Risks' activity occurs, then a 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This ensures that ranking risks based on their impact and probability, linked to the 'Risk ID' and 'Exposure Value' attributes, is followed by the creation of a structured mitigation plan.
3. If an 'Implement Mitigation' activity occurs, then a 'Monitor Risks' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This establishes an ordered flow where the execution of the mitigation plan, associated with the 'Risk ID' and 'Risk Status' attributes, is followed by continuous tracking of risks and their mitigation status.
4. If a 'Review Risk Assessment' activity occurs, then an 'Update Risk Register' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This ensures that re-evaluating the risk assessment is followed by modifying the risk register to reflect the current status of risks, maintaining an up-to-date record.
5. If an 'Identify Risk' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'A.Risk Category is Financial', then a 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity must eventually occur after it, with the correlation condition that 'T.Mitigation Cost > 1000'. This ensures that financial risks trigger the development of a mitigation plan with a substantial associated cost, maintaining financial oversight.
6. If an 'Identify Risk' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'A.Risk Status is Open', then an 'Implement Mitigation' activity must eventually occur after it within a time constraint of 0 to 30 days. This ensures that newly identified open risks are promptly addressed through mitigation execution within a specified timeframe.
7. If a 'Monitor Risks' activity occurs, then a 'Review Risk Assessment' activity must eventually occur after it within a time constraint of 90 to 180 days. This ensures that continuous risk tracking, linked to the 'Risk ID' and 'Review Frequency' attributes, is followed by a formal review of the risk assessment within a defined period.
8. If an 'Assess Impact' activity occurs with the activation condition that 'A.Impact Score > 7', then an 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity must eventually occur after it within a time constraint of 0 to 24 hours. This ensures that high-severity risks are escalated to higher levels of management for immediate attention and action within a strict timeframe.

#### **Succession Constraints**
1. A 'Monitor Risks' activity must be followed by a 'Review Risk Assessment' activity at some point later in the same process instance, and conversely, a 'Review Risk Assessment' event can occur only if a 'Monitor Risks' event has occurred before it. This bidirectional and ordered dependency establishes a causal link, ensuring that continuous risk tracking logically leads to a formal review, and reviews are justified by prior monitoring.
2. An 'Update Risk Register' activity must be followed by a 'Communicate Risks' activity at some point later in the same process instance, and conversely, a 'Communicate Risks' event can occur only if an 'Update Risk Register' event has occurred before it. This establishes a causal link where modifying the risk register, linked to the 'Risk ID' and 'Risk Status' attributes, directly leads to sharing information about risks with relevant stakeholders, and communication is justified by prior register updates.
