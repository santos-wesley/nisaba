## Activities
- **Identify Risk**: The 'Identify Risk' activity represents the initial step in the risk management process where potential risks are recognized and documented.
- **Assess Impact**: The 'Assess Impact' activity involves evaluating the potential consequences or effects of the identified risks on the organization.
- **Evaluate Probability**: The 'Evaluate Probability' activity focuses on determining the likelihood of each identified risk occurring.
- **Prioritize Risks**: The 'Prioritize Risks' activity entails ranking the risks based on their assessed impact and probability to focus on the most critical ones first.
- **Develop Mitigation Plan**: The 'Develop Mitigation Plan' activity includes creating strategies and plans to reduce or manage the prioritized risks effectively.
- **Implement Mitigation**: The 'Implement Mitigation' activity involves executing the developed mitigation plans to address the risks.
- **Monitor Risks**: The 'Monitor Risks' activity consists of continuously tracking the status and effectiveness of the implemented risk mitigations.
- **Review Risk Assessment**: The 'Review Risk Assessment' activity entails periodically re-evaluating the risks and the assessment process for accuracy and relevance.
- **Update Risk Register**: The 'Update Risk Register' activity involves recording the latest information about risks, their status, and any changes in the central risk register.
- **Communicate Risks**: The 'Communicate Risks' activity focuses on informing relevant stakeholders about the risks, their status, and mitigation efforts.
- **Approve Mitigation Plan**: The 'Approve Mitigation Plan' activity requires obtaining formal approval for the developed mitigation strategies before implementation.
- **Document Lessons Learned**: The 'Document Lessons Learned' activity involves recording insights and experiences gained from managing risks for future reference and improvement.
- **Escalate Critical Risks**: The 'Escalate Critical Risks' activity entails bringing high-priority or severe risks to the attention of higher management or relevant authorities.
- **Conduct Risk Workshop**: The 'Conduct Risk Workshop' activity includes organizing and facilitating sessions with stakeholders to identify, assess, and plan for risks collaboratively.
- **Validate Risk Controls**: The 'Validate Risk Controls' activity involves verifying that the risk control measures are effective and functioning as intended.

## Attributes
- **Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational**: The attribute 'Risk Category: Financial, Operational, Compliance, Strategic, Reputational' classifies the type of risk being managed, such as financial risks related to monetary losses, operational risks from internal processes, compliance risks from regulatory requirements, strategic risks affecting business goals, or reputational risks impacting the organization's image.
- **Impact Score: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Impact Score: integer between 1 and 10' quantifies the severity of the risk's potential consequences on a scale from 1 (minimal impact) to 10 (catastrophic impact).
- **Probability: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Probability: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents the likelihood of the risk occurring, expressed as a decimal value where 0.0 indicates impossibility and 1.0 indicates certainty.
- **Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted**: The attribute 'Risk Status: Open, Mitigated, Closed, Accepted' indicates the current state of the risk, whether it is still active (Open), has been addressed through actions (Mitigated), is no longer relevant (Closed), or has been acknowledged without further action (Accepted).
- **Mitigation Cost: float between 0.0 and 100000.0**: The attribute 'Mitigation Cost: float between 0.0 and 100000.0' specifies the estimated or actual financial expenditure required to implement the risk mitigation measures, ranging from no cost to up to 100000.0.
- **Review Frequency: integer between 1 and 12**: The attribute 'Review Frequency: integer between 1 and 12' defines how often the risk should be monitored or reviewed, in months, within a range of 1 to 12.
- **Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams**: The attribute 'Risk Owner: John Smith, Alice Johnson, Bob Williams' identifies the individual responsible for managing a specific risk, limited to the named personnel: John Smith, Alice Johnson, or Bob Williams.
- **Risk ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Risk ID: integer between 1 and 1000' serves as a unique identifier for each risk, assigned as an integer value from 1 to 1000.
- **Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance**: The attribute 'Mitigation Strategy: Avoidance, Transference, Mitigation, Acceptance' outlines the approach chosen to handle the risk, such as avoiding the risk altogether (Avoidance), shifting it to a third party (Transference), reducing its impact or likelihood (Mitigation), or accepting it as is (Acceptance).
- **Exposure Value: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Exposure Value: float between 0.0 and 1000000.0' calculates the potential financial exposure of the risk, computed typically as Impact Score multiplied by Probability, ranging from 0.0 to 1000000.0.
