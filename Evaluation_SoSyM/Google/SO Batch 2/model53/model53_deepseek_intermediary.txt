## Activities
- **Write Play Script**: The 'Write Play Script' activity represents the process of writing the script for the theatrical production.
- **Cast Actors**: The 'Cast Actors' activity involves selecting and assigning actors to roles in the play.
- **Design Set**: The 'Design Set' activity involves creating the visual design and layout for the stage set.
- **Build Set**: The 'Build Set' activity involves constructing the physical stage set based on the design.
- **Design Costumes**: The 'Design Costumes' activity involves creating the design concepts for the costumes to be worn in the play.
- **Sew Costumes**: The 'Sew Costumes' activity involves the actual sewing and fabrication of the costumes.
- **Rehearse Play**: The 'Rehearse Play' activity involves practicing the performance with the cast and crew.
- **Promote Play**: The 'Promote Play' activity involves marketing and advertising the play to attract audiences.
- **Sell Tickets**: The 'Sell Tickets' activity involves selling tickets to audience members for the performances.
- **Perform Play**: The 'Perform Play' activity represents the actual staging and performance of the play.
- **Review Performance**: The 'Review Performance' activity involves evaluating and critiquing the quality of the performance.
- **Pay Royalties**: The 'Pay Royalties' activity involves compensating the playwright or rights holders for the performance.
- **Strike Set**: The 'Strike Set' activity involves dismantling and removing the stage set after the performance run.
- **Store Costumes**: The 'Store Costumes' activity involves packing and storing the costumes for future use.
- **Archive Script**: The 'Archive Script' activity involves storing and preserving the play script for future reference.

## Attributes
- **Actor Count: integer between 2 and 20**: The attribute 'Actor Count: integer between 2 and 20' represents the number of actors cast in the production.
- **Budget Amount: float between 1000.0 and 50000.0**: The attribute 'Budget Amount: float between 1000.0 and 50000.0' represents the monetary budget allocated for building the set.
- **Play Genre: Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy**: The attribute 'Play Genre: Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy' specifies the genre category of the play being produced.
- **Set Complexity: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Set Complexity: integer between 1 and 10' represents the complexity level of the stage set design.
- **Ticket Price: float between 5.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Ticket Price: float between 5.0 and 100.0' represents the price at which tickets are sold to the audience.
- **Performance Quality: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor**: The attribute 'Performance Quality: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor' represents the quality rating of the performed play.
- **Royalty Percentage: integer between 5 and 20**: The attribute 'Royalty Percentage: integer between 5 and 20' represents the percentage of revenue paid as royalties.
- **Costume Count: integer between 5 and 50**: The attribute 'Costume Count: integer between 5 and 50' represents the number of costumes to be designed for the production.
- **Promotion Spend: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Promotion Spend: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the amount of money spent on promoting the play.
- **Rehearsal Hours: integer between 10 and 100**: The attribute 'Rehearsal Hours: integer between 10 and 100' represents the total number of hours spent rehearsing the play.
