**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Theatrical Production Process**

This model describes the end-to-end lifecycle of producing and closing a theatrical play, incorporating both **control-flow constraints** and **data-aware (multi-perspective) conditions**.

### Core Activities
The process includes the following 15 activities:

- **Write Play Script**
- **Cast Actors**
- **Design Set**
- **Build Set**
- **Design Costumes**
- **Sew Costumes**
- **Rehearse Play**
- **Promote Play**
- **Sell Tickets**
- **Perform Play**
- **Review Performance**
- **Pay Royalties**
- **Strike Set**
- **Store Costumes**
- **Archive Script**

### Data Perspectives (Bindings)
Each activity is linked to relevant data parameters:

- **Write Play Script** → *Play Genre* (Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy)
- **Cast Actors** → *Actor Count* (integer 2–20)
- **Design Set** → *Set Complexity* (integer 1–10)
- **Build Set** → *Budget Amount* (float 1,000.0–50,000.0)
- **Design Costumes** → *Costume Count* (integer 5–50)
- **Promote Play** → *Promotion Spend* (float 100.0–10,000.0)
- **Rehearse Play** → *Rehearsal Hours* (integer 10–100)
- **Sell Tickets** → *Ticket Price* (float 5.0–100.0)
- **Perform Play** → *Performance Quality* (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor)
- **Pay Royalties** → *Royalty Percentage* (integer 5–20)

### Declarative Constraints

#### **Mandatory & Existence Constraints**
- **Write Play Script** must be executed **at least once** at the beginning of the process (Init).
- **Perform Play** must occur **at least once** in every valid trace (Existence).

#### **Control-Flow Constraints**
- **Cast Actors** and **Rehearse Play** must both occur, and **Cast Actors** must precede **Rehearse Play** (Response + Precedence).
- **Design Set** must precede **Build Set** (Precedence).
- **Promote Play** and **Sell Tickets** are mutually dependent — if one occurs, the other must also occur (Co-Existence).
- After **Review Performance**, **Pay Royalties** must **not** occur (Not Response).
- **Write Play Script** must eventually be followed by **Sell Tickets** (Response), but only when the play is a **Comedy** and the **Ticket Price is ≤ $50**.

#### **Timed/Data-Aware Response Constraints**
The model also includes several **timed response constraints** with deadlines:

- After **Perform Play**:
  - Pay Royalties must occur within **7 days**.
  - Strike Set must occur within **1 day**.
  - Store Costumes must occur within **1 day**.

- After **Write Play Script**, the **Archive Script** activity must occur within **30 days**.

- After **Cast Actors**, **Rehearse Play** must begin within **1 hour**.

- After **Design Costumes**, **Sew Costumes** must begin within **24 hours**.

- After **Design Set**, **Build Set** must begin within **7 days**.

---

**Summary**: This is a **multi-perspective Declare model** for theater production that combines traditional declarative workflow constraints with data conditions (e.g., genre and pricing) and temporal deadlines, ensuring that artistic, logistical, and financial aspects of a play production are properly coordinated.
