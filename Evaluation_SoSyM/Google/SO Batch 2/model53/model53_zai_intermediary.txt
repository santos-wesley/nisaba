## Activities
- **Write Play Script**: The 'Write Play Script' activity represents the process of creating and drafting the script for a theatrical play.
- **Cast Actors**: The 'Cast Actors' activity involves selecting and assigning actors to the various roles in the play.
- **Design Set**: The 'Design Set' activity encompasses planning and creating the conceptual design for the stage set used in the performance.
- **Build Set**: The 'Build Set' activity involves the physical construction and assembly of the stage set based on the design specifications.
- **Design Costumes**: The 'Design Costumes' activity represents the process of conceptualizing and planning the costumes for the actors in the play.
- **Sew Costumes**: The 'Sew Costumes' activity involves the actual fabrication and tailoring of the costumes designed for the performance.
- **Rehearse Play**: The 'Rehearse Play' activity encompasses the practice sessions where actors rehearse their roles and refine the performance.
- **Promote Play**: The 'Promote Play' activity involves marketing and advertising efforts to generate audience interest and awareness for the play.
- **Sell Tickets**: The 'Sell Tickets' activity represents the process of offering and selling admission tickets to the audience for the play performances.
- **Perform Play**: The 'Perform Play' activity involves the live theatrical presentation of the play before an audience.
- **Review Performance**: The 'Review Performance' activity encompasses evaluating and critiquing the play's performance, typically by critics or the production team.
- **Pay Royalties**: The 'Pay Royalties' activity involves disbursing royalty payments to the playwright or rights holders for the use of the script.
- **Strike Set**: The 'Strike Set' activity represents the process of dismantling and removing the stage set after the performance run is complete.
- **Store Costumes**: The 'Store Costumes' activity involves properly packing and storing the costumes after the production has concluded.
- **Archive Script**: The 'Archive Script' activity encompasses preserving and filing the play script for future reference or reuse.

## Attributes
- **Actor Count: integer between 2 and 20**: The attribute 'Actor Count: integer between 2 and 20' represents the number of actors cast in the play, ranging from a minimum of 2 to a maximum of 20.
- **Budget Amount: float between 1000.0 and 50000.0**: The attribute 'Budget Amount: float between 1000.0 and 50000.0' represents the financial budget allocated for building the set, ranging from 1000.0 to 50000.0.
- **Play Genre: Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy**: The attribute 'Play Genre: Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy' specifies the genre category of the play, which can be one of Comedy, Drama, Musical, or Tragedy.
- **Set Complexity: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Set Complexity: integer between 1 and 10' represents the level of complexity of the stage set design, ranging from 1 (simplest) to 10 (most complex).
- **Ticket Price: float between 5.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Ticket Price: float between 5.0 and 100.0' represents the price of admission tickets for the play, ranging from 5.0 to 100.0.
- **Performance Quality: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor**: The attribute 'Performance Quality: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor' specifies the quality rating of the play's performance, which can be Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
- **Royalty Percentage: integer between 5 and 20**: The attribute 'Royalty Percentage: integer between 5 and 20' represents the percentage of revenue paid as royalties to the rights holder, ranging from 5 to 20 percent.
- **Costume Count: integer between 5 and 50**: The attribute 'Costume Count: integer between 5 and 50' represents the number of costumes designed for the play, ranging from a minimum of 5 to a maximum of 50.
- **Promotion Spend: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Promotion Spend: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the amount of money spent on promoting the play, ranging from 100.0 to 10000.0.
- **Rehearsal Hours: integer between 10 and 100**: The attribute 'Rehearsal Hours: integer between 10 and 100' represents the total number of hours dedicated to rehearsing the play, ranging from 10 to 100 hours.
