## Activities
- **Write Play Script**: The 'Write Play Script' activity represents the initial creation of the script for the theatrical production.
- **Cast Actors**: The 'Cast Actors' activity involves selecting and assigning performers to roles in the play.
- **Design Set**: The 'Design Set' activity focuses on conceptualizing the stage environment and scenery for the performance.
- **Build Set**: The 'Build Set' activity entails the physical construction of the stage sets based on the design.
- **Design Costumes**: The 'Design Costumes' activity includes creating designs for the clothing and attire worn by the actors.
- **Sew Costumes**: The 'Sew Costumes' activity involves the actual fabrication and sewing of the costume pieces.
- **Rehearse Play**: The 'Rehearse Play' activity consists of practicing the scenes and performances with the cast.
- **Promote Play**: The 'Promote Play' activity covers marketing and advertising efforts to attract audiences.
- **Sell Tickets**: The 'Sell Tickets' activity manages the sale of tickets for the play's performances.
- **Perform Play**: The 'Perform Play' activity is the live execution of the theatrical show for an audience.
- **Review Performance**: The 'Review Performance' activity includes evaluating and critiquing the quality of the show after it has been performed.
- **Pay Royalties**: The 'Pay Royalties' activity handles the payment of fees to the script's author or rights holders.
- **Strike Set**: The 'Strike Set' activity means dismantling and removing the stage sets after the performances conclude.
- **Store Costumes**: The 'Store Costumes' activity involves cleaning, packing, and storing the costumes for future use.
- **Archive Script**: The 'Archive Script' activity is the process of preserving and storing the final version of the play script.

## Attributes
- **Actor Count: integer between 2 and 20**: The attribute 'Actor Count: integer between 2 and 20' specifies the number of actors to be cast in the play, ranging from a minimum of 2 to a maximum of 20.
- **Budget Amount: float between 1000.0 and 50000.0**: The attribute 'Budget Amount: float between 1000.0 and 50000.0' represents the financial allocation for building the set, with values from 1000.0 to 50000.0.
- **Play Genre: Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy**: The attribute 'Play Genre: Comedy, Drama, Musical, Tragedy' indicates the genre of the play, which can be one of Comedy, Drama, Musical, or Tragedy.
- **Set Complexity: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Set Complexity: integer between 1 and 10' measures the intricacy of the set design on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Ticket Price: float between 5.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Ticket Price: float between 5.0 and 100.0' defines the cost of a ticket for the play, ranging from 5.0 to 100.0.
- **Performance Quality: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor**: The attribute 'Performance Quality: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor' evaluates the overall quality of the performance, categorized as Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
- **Royalty Percentage: integer between 5 and 20**: The attribute 'Royalty Percentage: integer between 5 and 20' sets the percentage of royalties to be paid, between 5 and 20.
- **Costume Count: integer between 5 and 50**: The attribute 'Costume Count: integer between 5 and 50' indicates the number of costumes to be designed and sewn, from 5 to 50.
- **Promotion Spend: float between 100.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Promotion Spend: float between 100.0 and 10000.0' represents the amount spent on promoting the play, ranging from 100.0 to 10000.0.
- **Rehearsal Hours: integer between 10 and 100**: The attribute 'Rehearsal Hours: integer between 10 and 100' specifies the total hours allocated for rehearsing the play, between 10 and 100.
