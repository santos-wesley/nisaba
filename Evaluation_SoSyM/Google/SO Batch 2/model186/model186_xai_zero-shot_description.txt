**Natural Language Description:**

This Multi-Perspective Declare model describes the core processes of an animal welfare and rescue organization. It captures both the operational workflow for handling animal abuse cases and various supporting activities such as donations, volunteering, staff training, and facility maintenance.

### Core Animal Rescue and Adoption Process

The process begins when someone **Reports Animal Abuse**. Every abuse report **must** be followed by an **Investigation**. If the investigation reveals **High** severity abuse, the organization is obligated to **Rescue the Abused Animal**.

Once rescued, the animal’s condition determines the next steps:
- If the animal is **Injured**, it **must** receive **Veterinary Care** immediately after rescue.
- After veterinary care, the organization must **Find a Foster Home** (subject to current shelter capacity).

For animals placed into adoption:
- **Adoption Screening** must occur **before** any adoption can be finalized.
- Only if the screening result is **Approved** can the organization **Finalize the Animal Adoption**.
- Once adopted, the organization is required to **Monitor the Animal’s Welfare** post-adoption.

Additionally:
- If an animal is **Sick**, the organization has a **choice** between providing **Veterinary Care** or, in extreme cases, **Euthanizing the Animal**.

### Supporting Activities and Constraints

- **Public Education** on animal welfare must occur at least once (Existence constraint).
- Every **Report of Animal Abuse** should eventually be linked to a **Donation** being processed (Responded Existence).
- **Volunteer Applications** that fail the background check **cannot** be approved (Not Response constraint).
- Animals that have received veterinary care **must** be scheduled for a follow-up checkup within 30 days.
- The shelter **must** inspect its own facility at least once every 90 days.
- **Adoption screening** that results in approval must lead to finalization of the adoption within 7 days.

### Data Perspectives

The model tracks several key data attributes:
- **Abuse Severity** (Low, Medium, High)
- **Animal Species** (Dog, Cat, Other)
- **Animal Age** (0–25 years)
- **Animal Health** (Healthy, Injured, Sick)
- **Shelter Capacity** (0–500)
- **Adoption Status** (Pending, Approved, Rejected)
- **Donation Amount** ($1.00 – $10,000.00)
- **Volunteer Background Check** (Pass/Fail)
- **Checkup Frequency** (every 7–365 days)

This model ensures that animal abuse reports are taken seriously, that injured or sick animals receive timely care, that adoptions follow a rigorous screening process, and that the organization maintains regular facility inspections, public education, and post-adoption monitoring — all while respecting shelter capacity and ethical decision-making regarding animal euthanasia.
