#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Report Animal Abuse**: The 'Report Animal Abuse' activity represents the initial reporting of suspected animal abuse cases.
- **Investigate Abuse Report**: The 'Investigate Abuse Report' activity involves conducting a thorough investigation into the reported animal abuse.
- **Rescue Abused Animal**: The 'Rescue Abused Animal' activity focuses on rescuing the animal from the abusive environment.
- **Provide Veterinary Care**: The 'Provide Veterinary Care' activity includes delivering necessary medical treatment to the abused animal.
- **Find Foster Home**: The 'Find Foster Home' activity entails locating a suitable temporary foster home for the animal.
- **Conduct Adoption Screening**: The 'Conduct Adoption Screening' activity involves screening potential adopters for suitability.
- **Finalize Animal Adoption**: The 'Finalize Animal Adoption' activity represents the completion of the adoption process for the animal.
- **Monitor Animal Welfare**: The 'Monitor Animal Welfare' activity includes ongoing monitoring of the adopted animal's well-being.
- **Educate Public Awareness**: The 'Educate Public Awareness' activity focuses on educating the public about animal welfare and abuse prevention.
- **Process Donation**: The 'Process Donation' activity involves handling and processing financial donations to support the shelter.
- **Train Staff Member**: The 'Train Staff Member' activity entails training staff members on animal care and shelter operations.
- **Inspect Shelter Facility**: The 'Inspect Shelter Facility' activity involves inspecting the shelter facilities for compliance and safety.
- **Approve Volunteer Application**: The 'Approve Volunteer Application' activity includes reviewing and approving applications from potential volunteers.
- **Schedule Animal Checkup**: The 'Schedule Animal Checkup' activity represents scheduling regular health checkups for the animals.
- **Euthanize Animal**: The 'Euthanize Animal' activity involves the humane euthanasia of an animal when necessary.

## Attributes
- **Abuse Severity: Low, Medium, High**: The attribute 'Abuse Severity: Low, Medium, High' indicates the level of severity of the reported animal abuse.
- **Animal Species: Dog, Cat, Other**: The attribute 'Animal Species: Dog, Cat, Other' specifies the species of the animal involved in the abuse report.
- **Animal Age: integer between 0 and 25**: The attribute 'Animal Age: integer between 0 and 25' represents the age of the animal in years.
- **Animal Health: Healthy, Injured, Sick**: The attribute 'Animal Health: Healthy, Injured, Sick' describes the current health condition of the animal.
- **Donation Amount: float between 1.0 and 10000.0**: The attribute 'Donation Amount: float between 1.0 and 10000.0' indicates the monetary value of the donation processed.
- **Adoption Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected**: The attribute 'Adoption Status: Pending, Approved, Rejected' tracks the status of the adoption process.
- **Shelter Capacity: integer between 0 and 500**: The attribute 'Shelter Capacity: integer between 0 and 500' represents the current capacity available in the shelter.
- **Volunteer Background Check: Pass, Fail**: The attribute 'Volunteer Background Check: Pass, Fail' indicates the result of the background check for volunteer applicants.
- **Checkup Frequency: integer between 7 and 365**: The attribute 'Checkup Frequency: integer between 7 and 365' specifies the number of days between scheduled animal checkups.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for animal abuse response and shelter management is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Report Animal Abuse,' which involves the initial reporting of suspected animal abuse cases with the attributes 'Abuse Severity' and 'Animal Species.' This establishes the foundational context for every process instance, ensuring that all subsequent actions stem from a formal report.

### **Frequency Constraints**

#### **Existence Constraint**
The 'Educate Public Awareness' activity must occur at least once in the process. This ensures that public education on animal welfare and abuse prevention is integrated into shelter operations, adding depth and long-term preventive impact to the overall workflow.

### **Dependency Constraints**

#### **Responded Existence Constraints**
If the 'Report Animal Abuse' activity occurs in a process instance, then the 'Process Donation' activity must also occur at least once within the same process instance, either before or after the report. This establishes a clear dependency where every reported case necessitates financial processing to support the shelter's response efforts, thereby enhancing coherence between intake and resource management.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The 'Conduct Adoption Screening' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Finalize Animal Adoption' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This background condition sets up the logical sequence by requiring finalization steps to precede detailed screening in certain adoption pathways, guiding the model toward orderly progression.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Report Animal Abuse' activity occurs, then an 'Investigate Abuse Report' activity must eventually occur after it. This defines an ordered flow from initial reporting to thorough investigation, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure without mandating immediate action.
2. If an 'Investigate Abuse Report' activity occurs and the 'Abuse Severity is High,' then a 'Rescue Abused Animal' activity must eventually occur afterward. This sequenced response ensures that severe cases trigger rescue operations within the broader timeline of the process.
3. If a 'Conduct Adoption Screening' activity occurs and the 'Adoption Status is Approved,' then a 'Finalize Animal Adoption' activity must eventually occur afterward, within at most 7 days. This maintains momentum in the adoption pathway by enforcing timely completion once approval criteria are met.
4. If a 'Provide Veterinary Care' activity occurs, then a 'Schedule Animal Checkup' activity must eventually occur afterward, within at most 30 days. This response relation guarantees ongoing health monitoring follows initial treatment.
5. If an 'Inspect Shelter Facility' activity occurs, then another 'Inspect Shelter Facility' activity must eventually occur afterward, within at most 90 days. This recurring response ensures regular compliance checks are maintained over time.

#### **Succession Constraints**
1. A 'Provide Veterinary Care' activity must be followed by a 'Find Foster Home' activity at some point later, while a 'Find Foster Home' event can occur only after a 'Provide Veterinary Care' event. This cause-effect relationship creates a cohesive progression from medical stabilization to temporary placement.
2. A 'Finalize Animal Adoption' activity must be followed by a 'Monitor Animal Welfare' activity at some point later, while a 'Monitor Animal Welfare' event can occur only after a 'Finalize Animal Adoption' event. This bidirectional causal link ensures post-adoption oversight is inextricably tied to completed adoptions.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
Whenever a 'Rescue Abused Animal' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Provide Veterinary Care' activity in the next event position. This direct cause-and-effect relationship captures the urgent succession from rescue to medical intervention, allowing no intervening events and ensuring immediate care for the animal.

### **Exclusivity Constraints**

#### **Choice Constraints**
At least one of the activities 'Provide Veterinary Care' or 'Euthanize Animal' must occur in the process instance when the 'Animal Health is Sick.' This contrast introduces alternative humane pathways, allowing flexibility so that either continued treatment or compassionate euthanasia satisfies the requirement for severely ill animals, thereby maintaining process integrity through viable options.

### **Negative Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Not Response Constraints**
The 'Approve Volunteer Application' activity must not be followed by another 'Approve Volunteer Application' activity at any point later when the 'Volunteer Background Check is Fail.' This contrast prevents repeated approvals after a failed check, thereby maintaining the integrity of the model's intended progression for volunteer onboarding.
