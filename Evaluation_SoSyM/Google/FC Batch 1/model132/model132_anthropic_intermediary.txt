## Activities
- **Monitor Seismic Activity**: The 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity represents the continuous observation and detection of seismic events to identify potential disaster risks.
- **Assess Disaster Risk**: The 'Assess Disaster Risk' activity involves evaluating the level of risk posed by a detected disaster situation.
- **Issue Early Warning**: The 'Issue Early Warning' activity includes disseminating alerts to notify affected populations about an impending disaster.
- **Activate Emergency Response**: The 'Activate Emergency Response' activity represents the initiation of emergency response protocols in reaction to a disaster event.
- **Deploy Rescue Teams**: The 'Deploy Rescue Teams' activity involves sending rescue personnel to affected areas to conduct search and rescue operations.
- **Provide Medical Assistance**: The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity includes delivering medical care and treatment to individuals affected by the disaster.
- **Distribute Relief Supplies**: The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity represents the delivery of essential goods such as food, water, and medicine to affected populations.
- **Evacuate Affected Population**: The 'Evacuate Affected Population' activity involves relocating individuals from dangerous areas to safer locations.
- **Establish Temporary Shelters**: The 'Establish Temporary Shelters' activity includes setting up temporary housing facilities for displaced individuals.
- **Conduct Damage Assessment**: The 'Conduct Damage Assessment' activity represents the evaluation of the extent of damage caused by the disaster.

## Attributes
- **Seismic Magnitude: Integer between 0 and 10**: The attribute 'Seismic Magnitude: Integer between 0 and 10' represents the measured intensity of a seismic event on a numerical scale.
- **Risk Score: Float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Risk Score: Float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the calculated level of disaster risk associated with a given situation.
- **Warning Level: Low, Medium, High, Critical**: The attribute 'Warning Level: Low, Medium, High, Critical' specifies the severity classification of an early warning issued to the population.
- **Response Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Response Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed' specifies the current state of the emergency response process.
- **Teams Deployed: Integer between 0 and 50**: The attribute 'Teams Deployed: Integer between 0 and 50' represents the number of rescue teams sent to the affected area.
- **Injured Count: Integer between 0 and 10000**: The attribute 'Injured Count: Integer between 0 and 10000' represents the number of individuals injured as a result of the disaster.
- **Supplies Distributed: Integer between 0 and 20000**: The attribute 'Supplies Distributed: Integer between 0 and 20000' represents the quantity of relief supplies delivered to affected populations.
- **People Evacuated: Integer between 0 and 50000**: The attribute 'People Evacuated: Integer between 0 and 50000' represents the number of individuals relocated from the affected area.
- **Shelters Established: Integer between 0 and 20**: The attribute 'Shelters Established: Integer between 0 and 20' represents the number of temporary shelters set up for displaced individuals.
- **Damage Estimate: Float between 0.0 and 1000000000.0**: The attribute 'Damage Estimate: Float between 0.0 and 1000000000.0' represents the estimated monetary cost of the damage caused by the disaster.
