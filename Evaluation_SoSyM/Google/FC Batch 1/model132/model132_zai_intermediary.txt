## Activities
- **Monitor Seismic Activity**: The 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity represents the continuous monitoring and tracking of seismic events and ground movements to detect potential earthquakes or geological disturbances.
- **Assess Disaster Risk**: The 'Assess Disaster Risk' activity involves evaluating the level of risk associated with detected seismic activity, determining the potential impact on affected areas and populations.
- **Issue Early Warning**: The 'Issue Early Warning' activity entails disseminating early warning notifications to relevant authorities and the public about an impending or potential disaster based on seismic monitoring data.
- **Activate Emergency Response**: The 'Activate Emergency Response' activity represents the formal activation of emergency response protocols and mobilization of resources to address an unfolding disaster situation.
- **Deploy Rescue Teams**: The 'Deploy Rescue Teams' activity involves dispatching trained rescue personnel and teams to the affected areas to conduct search, rescue, and recovery operations.
- **Provide Medical Assistance**: The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity encompasses delivering emergency medical care and treatment to individuals injured during the disaster event.
- **Distribute Relief Supplies**: The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity entails the allocation and distribution of essential supplies such as food, water, medicine, and other necessities to affected populations.
- **Evacuate Affected Population**: The 'Evacuate Affected Population' activity involves organizing and executing the safe evacuation of people from areas at risk or impacted by the disaster.
- **Establish Temporary Shelters**: The 'Establish Temporary Shelters' activity represents setting up temporary housing and shelter facilities to accommodate displaced individuals and families affected by the disaster.
- **Conduct Damage Assessment**: The 'Conduct Damage Assessment' activity entails evaluating and documenting the extent of physical, structural, and economic damage caused by the disaster event.

## Attributes
- **Seismic Magnitude: integer between 0 and 10**: The attribute 'Seismic Magnitude: integer between 0 and 10' represents the measured magnitude of seismic activity on a scale from 0 to 10, indicating the intensity of ground movement detected during monitoring.
- **Risk Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Risk Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents a numerical assessment of the disaster risk level, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0, used to determine the severity of potential impact.
- **Warning Level: Low, Medium, High, Critical**: The attribute 'Warning Level: Low, Medium, High, Critical' specifies the severity level of an early warning issued, categorized as Low, Medium, High, or Critical to indicate the urgency of the threat.
- **Response Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Response Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed' indicates the current phase of the emergency response, categorized as Initiated, Ongoing, or Completed to track the progress of response operations.
- **Teams Deployed: integer between 0 and 50**: The attribute 'Teams Deployed: integer between 0 and 50' represents the number of rescue teams dispatched to the affected area, ranging from 0 to 50 teams.
- **Injured Count: integer between 0 and 10000**: The attribute 'Injured Count: integer between 0 and 10000' represents the total number of individuals injured during the disaster event, ranging from 0 to 10,000.
- **Supplies Distributed: integer between 0 and 20000**: The attribute 'Supplies Distributed: integer between 0 and 20000' represents the quantity of relief supplies distributed to affected populations, ranging from 0 to 20,000 units.
- **People Evacuated: integer between 0 and 50000**: The attribute 'People Evacuated: integer between 0 and 50000' represents the number of individuals safely evacuated from affected or at-risk areas, ranging from 0 to 50,000 people.
- **Shelters Established: integer between 0 and 20**: The attribute 'Shelters Established: integer between 0 and 20' represents the number of temporary shelter facilities set up to house displaced individuals, ranging from 0 to 20 shelters.
- **Damage Estimate: float between 0.0 and 1000000000.0**: The attribute 'Damage Estimate: float between 0.0 and 1000000000.0' represents the estimated monetary value of damage caused by the disaster, ranging from 0.0 to 1,000,000,000.0.
