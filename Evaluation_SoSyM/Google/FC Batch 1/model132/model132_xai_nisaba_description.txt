#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Monitor Seismic Activity**: The 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity represents the continuous observation and recording of seismic events to detect potential earthquakes or tremors.
- **Assess Disaster Risk**: The 'Assess Disaster Risk' activity involves evaluating the potential impact and likelihood of a disaster based on collected data.
- **Issue Early Warning**: The 'Issue Early Warning' activity includes alerting relevant authorities and the public about an impending disaster to allow for timely preparations.
- **Activate Emergency Response**: The 'Activate Emergency Response' activity entails initiating formal emergency protocols and mobilizing resources for disaster management.
- **Deploy Rescue Teams**: The 'Deploy Rescue Teams' activity focuses on sending specialized teams to affected areas to perform search and rescue operations.
- **Provide Medical Assistance**: The 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity involves delivering healthcare services and first aid to injured individuals in the disaster zone.
- **Distribute Relief Supplies**: The 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity includes allocating essential items such as food, water, and blankets to those impacted by the disaster.
- **Evacuate Affected Population**: The 'Evacuate Affected Population' activity entails relocating people from dangerous areas to safer locations.
- **Establish Temporary Shelters**: The 'Establish Temporary Shelters' activity involves setting up provisional housing facilities for displaced individuals.
- **Conduct Damage Assessment**: The 'Conduct Damage Assessment' activity comprises surveying and quantifying the extent of destruction caused by the disaster.

## Attributes
- **Seismic Magnitude: integer between 0 and 10**: The attribute 'Seismic Magnitude: integer between 0 and 10' represents the measured strength of seismic activity on a scale from 0 to 10.
- **Risk Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Risk Score: float between 0.0 and 100.0' indicates the calculated level of disaster risk, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Warning Level: Low, Medium, High, Critical**: The attribute 'Warning Level: Low, Medium, High, Critical' specifies the severity category of the early warning issued.
- **Response Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed**: The attribute 'Response Status: Initiated, Ongoing, Completed' tracks the current state of the emergency response efforts.
- **Teams Deployed: integer between 0 and 50**: The attribute 'Teams Deployed: integer between 0 and 50' quantifies the number of rescue teams sent to the field, ranging from 0 to 50.
- **Injured Count: integer between 0 and 10000**: The attribute 'Injured Count: integer between 0 and 10000' records the number of people reported as injured, from 0 to 10000.
- **Supplies Distributed: integer between 0 and 20000**: The attribute 'Supplies Distributed: integer between 0 and 20000' measures the quantity of relief supplies provided, ranging from 0 to 20000.
- **People Evacuated: integer between 0 and 50000**: The attribute 'People Evacuated: integer between 0 and 50000' counts the number of individuals successfully moved to safety, from 0 to 50000.
- **Shelters Established: integer between 0 and 20**: The attribute 'Shelters Established: integer between 0 and 20' indicates the number of temporary shelters set up, ranging from 0 to 20.
- **Damage Estimate: float between 0.0 and 1000000000.0**: The attribute 'Damage Estimate: float between 0.0 and 1000000000.0' represents the estimated monetary value of damages in currency units, from 0.0 to 1000000000.0.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the disaster management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Monitor Seismic Activity,' which involves the continuous observation and recording of seismic events using the 'Seismic Magnitude' attribute. This establishes the initial context for all subsequent disaster response actions.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If the 'Monitor Seismic Activity' activity occurs, then the 'Issue Early Warning' activity must eventually occur after it. This defines an ordered flow where seismic monitoring leads to alerting relevant authorities and the public based on the 'Warning Level' attribute, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure for timely preparations.
2. If the 'Activate Emergency Response' activity occurs with the 'Response Status' attribute, then the 'Deploy Rescue Teams' activity must eventually occur after it. This sequence ensures that formal emergency protocols lead to sending specialized teams quantified by the 'Teams Deployed' attribute.
3. If the 'Deploy Rescue Teams' activity occurs, then the 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity must eventually occur after it. This ordered flow ensures that rescue operations are followed by delivering healthcare services tracked via the 'Injured Count' attribute.
4. If the 'Provide Medical Assistance' activity occurs, then the 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity must eventually occur after it within a time frame between 0 and 24 hours. This maintains process momentum by ensuring healthcare delivery is followed by allocating essential items such as food, water, and blankets measured by the 'Supplies Distributed' attribute.
5. If the 'Assess Disaster Risk' activity occurs with a 'Risk Score' greater than 70, then the 'Evacuate Affected Population' activity must eventually occur after it. This sequence ensures that high-risk evaluations, based on the calculated 'Risk Score' attribute, lead to relocating people from dangerous areas using the 'People Evacuated' attribute.
6. If the 'Assess Disaster Risk' activity occurs with a 'Risk Score' greater than 70, then the 'Establish Temporary Shelters' activity must eventually occur after it. This ordered flow ensures that elevated risk assessments lead to setting up provisional housing facilities quantified by the 'Shelters Established' attribute.

#### **Succession Constraints**
A 'Conduct Damage Assessment' activity must be followed by a 'Distribute Relief Supplies' activity at some point later, while a 'Distribute Relief Supplies' event can occur only after a preceding 'Conduct Damage Assessment.' This establishes a causal link between surveying destruction via the 'Damage Estimate' attribute and allocating relief supplies, creating a cohesive progression that prevents relief distribution without quantified damage evaluation.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
Whenever an 'Issue Early Warning' activity occurs with its 'Warning Level' attribute, it must be immediately followed by an 'Activate Emergency Response' activity with its 'Response Status' attribute. This captures an immediate cause-and-effect relationship, ensuring a direct and uninterrupted transition from alerting authorities to initiating formal emergency protocols with no intervening events.
