## Activities
- **Detect Fraudulent Activity**: The 'Detect Fraudulent Activity' activity represents the process of identifying potentially fraudulent transactions or behaviors.
- **Verify Customer Identity**: The 'Verify Customer Identity' activity involves confirming the identity of a customer to ensure legitimacy of the transaction or request.
- **Review Transaction Details**: The 'Review Transaction Details' activity includes examining the specifics of a transaction to assess its validity and characteristics.
- **Approve Transaction**: The 'Approve Transaction' activity represents the authorization of a transaction to proceed.
- **Decline Transaction**: The 'Decline Transaction' activity represents the rejection of a transaction, preventing it from proceeding.
- **Report Suspicious Activity**: The 'Report Suspicious Activity' activity involves formally documenting and reporting behavior or transactions deemed suspicious.
- **Update Account Security**: The 'Update Account Security' activity includes modifying or enhancing the security settings associated with a customer account.
- **Notify Customer**: The 'Notify Customer' activity represents informing the customer about relevant events, decisions, or actions related to their account or transactions.
- **Investigate Security Breach**: The 'Investigate Security Breach' activity involves examining and analyzing a security incident to determine its cause and impact.
- **Implement Security Patch**: The 'Implement Security Patch' activity represents the application of a fix or update to address a security vulnerability.
