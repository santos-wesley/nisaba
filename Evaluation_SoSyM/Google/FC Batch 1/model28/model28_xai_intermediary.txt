## Activities
- **Submit Application**: The 'Submit Application' activity represents the initial submission of an application for an arrangement.
- **Review Application**: The 'Review Application' activity involves evaluating the submitted application based on criteria such as review score.
- **Approve Application**: The 'Approve Application' activity signifies the official approval of the application for the arrangement.
- **Reject Application**: The 'Reject Application' activity indicates the rejection of the application for the arrangement.
- **Implement Arrangement**: The 'Implement Arrangement' activity entails putting the approved arrangement into practice, including setting hours per week.
- **Monitor Arrangement**: The 'Monitor Arrangement' activity involves overseeing the ongoing arrangement to ensure compliance and effectiveness.
- **Adjust Arrangement**: The 'Adjust Arrangement' activity allows for modifications to the arrangement, such as changing hours per week.
- **Terminate Arrangement**: The 'Terminate Arrangement' activity concludes the arrangement, often due to specific status changes.

## Attributes
- **Arrangement Type: Remote Work, Flextime, Compressed Workweek, Job Sharing**: The attribute 'Arrangement Type: Remote Work, Flextime, Compressed Workweek, Job Sharing' specifies the different types of work arrangements that can be applied for.
- **Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000' represents a unique identifier for each application, ranging from 1 to 1000.
- **Application Status: Submitted, Reviewed, Approved, Rejected, Implemented, Terminated**: The attribute 'Application Status: Submitted, Reviewed, Approved, Rejected, Implemented, Terminated' tracks the current state of the application throughout the process.
- **Hours Per Week: float between 10.0 and 40.0**: The attribute 'Hours Per Week: float between 10.0 and 40.0' indicates the number of working hours per week for the arrangement, within the range of 10.0 to 40.0.
- **Review Score: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Review Score: integer between 1 and 5' represents the score given during the review of the application, on a scale from 1 to 5.
