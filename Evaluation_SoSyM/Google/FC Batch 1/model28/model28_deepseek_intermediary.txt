## Activities
- **Submit Application**: The 'Submit Application' activity represents the submission of a flexible work arrangement application by an employee.
- **Review Application**: The 'Review Application' activity involves evaluating the submitted application for completeness and eligibility.
- **Approve Application**: The 'Approve Application' activity represents the formal approval of a flexible work arrangement application.
- **Reject Application**: The 'Reject Application' activity represents the formal rejection of a flexible work arrangement application.
- **Implement Arrangement**: The 'Implement Arrangement' activity involves putting the approved flexible work arrangement into effect.
- **Monitor Arrangement**: The 'Monitor Arrangement' activity involves overseeing and tracking the ongoing flexible work arrangement.
- **Adjust Arrangement**: The 'Adjust Arrangement' activity involves making modifications to the existing flexible work arrangement.
- **Terminate Arrangement**: The 'Terminate Arrangement' activity represents the ending of a flexible work arrangement.

## Attributes
- **Arrangement Type: Remote Work, Flextime, Compressed Workweek, Job Sharing**: The attribute 'Arrangement Type: Remote Work, Flextime, Compressed Workweek, Job Sharing' specifies the types of flexible work arrangements available for selection.
- **Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Application ID: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the unique identification number assigned to each application.
- **Application Status: Submitted, Reviewed, Approved, Rejected, Implemented, Terminated**: The attribute 'Application Status: Submitted, Reviewed, Approved, Rejected, Implemented, Terminated' tracks the current status of an application throughout its lifecycle.
- **Hours Per Week: float between 10.0 and 40.0**: The attribute 'Hours Per Week: float between 10.0 and 40.0' represents the number of hours per week allocated to the flexible work arrangement.
- **Review Score: integer between 1 and 5**: The attribute 'Review Score: integer between 1 and 5' represents the score assigned during the review of an application.
