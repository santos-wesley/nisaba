#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Gather Intelligence**: The 'Gather Intelligence' activity represents the process of collecting intelligence from various sources.
- **Analyze Data**: The 'Analyze Data' activity involves examining and interpreting the gathered intelligence data.
- **Assess Credibility**: The 'Assess Credibility' activity entails evaluating the reliability and trustworthiness of the gathered intelligence and its sources.
- **Identify Threats**: The 'Identify Threats' activity focuses on detecting and classifying potential threats based on the analyzed intelligence data.
- **Develop Strategies**: The 'Develop Strategies' activity involves formulating plans and approaches to address identified threats.
- **Implement Countermeasures**: The 'Implement Countermeasures' activity represents the execution of actions and measures designed to mitigate identified threats.
- **Monitor Activity**: The 'Monitor Activity' activity involves continuously observing and tracking activities to detect any changes or emerging threats.
- **Report Findings**: The 'Report Findings' activity includes compiling and presenting the results of intelligence analysis and monitoring efforts.
- **Review Intelligence**: The 'Review Intelligence' activity entails re-evaluating previously gathered and analyzed intelligence to ensure accuracy and relevance.
- **Disseminate Information**: The 'Disseminate Information' activity involves sharing intelligence findings and reports with relevant stakeholders and authorized parties.

## Attributes
- **Source Type: Human, Signal, Open Source, Cyber**: The attribute 'Source Type: Human, Signal, Open Source, Cyber' specifies the types of sources from which intelligence is gathered, including Human, Signal, Open Source, and Cyber.
- **Threat Level: integer between 1 and 10**: The attribute 'Threat Level: integer between 1 and 10' represents the severity level of an identified threat on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Confidence Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0**: The attribute 'Confidence Score: float between 0.0 and 1.0' represents the degree of confidence in the analysis results, ranging from 0.0 to 1.0.
- **Report Status: Draft, Review, Final, Disseminated**: The attribute 'Report Status: Draft, Review, Final, Disseminated' specifies the possible statuses of an intelligence report, including Draft, Review, Final, and Disseminated.
- **Number of Sources: integer between 1 and 50**: The attribute 'Number of Sources: integer between 1 and 50' represents the count of intelligence sources used, ranging from 1 to 50.
- **Risk Factor: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Risk Factor: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the quantified risk associated with an identified threat, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Analysis Technique: Link Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Pattern Recognition**: The attribute 'Analysis Technique: Link Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Pattern Recognition' specifies the methods used for analyzing intelligence data, including Link Analysis, Statistical Analysis, and Pattern Recognition.
- **Days to Respond: integer between 1 and 365**: The attribute 'Days to Respond: integer between 1 and 365' represents the number of days allocated for implementing countermeasures, ranging from 1 to 365.
- **Cost of Countermeasures: float between 100.0 and 1000000.0**: The attribute 'Cost of Countermeasures: float between 100.0 and 1000000.0' represents the financial cost of implementing countermeasures, ranging from 100.0 to 1000000.0.
- **Geographic Region: North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, South America**: The attribute 'Geographic Region: North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, South America' specifies the geographic regions relevant to the intelligence report, including North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, and South America.

## Binds
- **Gather Intelligence: Source Type, Number of Sources**: The bind 'Gather Intelligence: Source Type, Number of Sources' specifies that the activity 'Gather Intelligence' requires the attributes 'Source Type' and 'Number of Sources'.
- **Analyze Data: Analysis Technique, Confidence Score**: The bind 'Analyze Data: Analysis Technique, Confidence Score' links the 'Analyze Data' activity with the attributes 'Analysis Technique' and 'Confidence Score'.
- **Identify Threats: Threat Level, Risk Factor**: The bind 'Identify Threats: Threat Level, Risk Factor' links the 'Identify Threats' activity with the attributes 'Threat Level' and 'Risk Factor'.
- **Implement Countermeasures: Cost of Countermeasures, Days to Respond**: The bind 'Implement Countermeasures: Cost of Countermeasures, Days to Respond' links the 'Implement Countermeasures' activity with the attributes 'Cost of Countermeasures' and 'Days to Respond'.
- **Report Findings: Report Status, Geographic Region**: The bind 'Report Findings: Report Status, Geographic Region' links the 'Report Findings' activity with the attributes 'Report Status' and 'Geographic Region'.

## Constraints
- **Init[Gather Intelligence] | |**: The 'Gather Intelligence' activity must be the first activity to occur in the process.
- **Response[Gather Intelligence, Analyze Data] | | |**: If a 'Gather Intelligence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Data' activity must eventually occur after the 'Gather Intelligence' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Gather Intelligence' can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Analyze Data' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Gather Intelligence'. If 'Gather Intelligence' does not occur, the constraint imposes no obligation on the occurrence of 'Analyze Data'.
- **Succession[Analyze Data, Identify Threats] | | |**: An 'Analyze Data' activity must be followed by an 'Identify Threats' activity at some point later in the same process instance. Conversely, an 'Identify Threats' event can occur only if an 'Analyze Data' event has occurred before it within the same process instance. This bidirectional and ordered dependency ensures that 'Analyze Data' impose obligations for future 'Identify Threats', and 'Identify Threats' cannot occur without prior 'Analyze Data'. Multiple 'Analyze Data' and 'Identify Threats' are allowed, with each 'Analyze Data' eventually leading to an 'Identify Threats' and each 'Identify Threats' being justified by a preceding 'Analyze Data'.
- **Precedence[Develop Strategies, Identify Threats] | | |**: The 'Identify Threats' activity is permitted to occur only if a 'Develop Strategies' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance.
- **Chain Precedence[Implement Countermeasures, Develop Strategies] | | |**: The 'Develop Strategies' activity can occur only if it is immediately preceded by an 'Implement Countermeasures' activity, with no other activities occurring in between.
- **Chain Response[Implement Countermeasures, Monitor Activity] | | |**: Whenever an 'Implement Countermeasures' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Monitor Activity' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. The 'Monitor Activity' activity is allowed to happen isolated.
- **Alternate Response[Monitor Activity, Report Findings] | | |**: If a 'Monitor Activity' activity occurs, then a 'Report Findings' activity must occur after that 'Monitor Activity' event and before any subsequent 'Monitor Activity' occurs no other occurrence of 'Monitor Activity' is allowed between a 'Monitor Activity' and 'Report Findings'.
- **Alternate Precedence[Disseminate Information, Review Intelligence] | | |**: The 'Review Intelligence' activity can occur only if it was immediately preceded by a 'Disseminate Information' activity, and no other 'Disseminate Information' event is allowed between the 'Disseminate Information' and the 'Review Intelligence'.
- **End[Disseminate Information] | |**: The 'Disseminate Information' activity must be the last activity to occur in the process.
- **Co-Existence[Gather Intelligence, Analyze Data] | | |**: If 'Gather Intelligence' activity occurs, then 'Analyze Data' must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa.


## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the intelligence and threat management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**

#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Gather Intelligence,' which involves collecting intelligence from various sources. This activity requires the specification of the 'Source Type' (Human, Signal, Open Source, Cyber) and the 'Number of Sources' (an integer between 1 and 50), establishing the initial context and foundational data collection parameters for the entire process.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Disseminate Information,' which involves sharing intelligence findings and reports with relevant stakeholders and authorized parties. This activity must be the last to occur in the process, ensuring that all intelligence analysis, monitoring, and review efforts are properly compiled and distributed before the process instance is closed.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The activity 'Identify Threats' is permitted to occur only if a 'Develop Strategies' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This sets up an initial context where strategies for addressing potential threats must be formulated before the threats themselves are detected and classified based on the analyzed intelligence data, which includes evaluating the 'Threat Level' (an integer between 1 and 10) and the 'Risk Factor' (a float between 0.0 and 100.0).

#### **Response Constraints**
If a 'Gather Intelligence' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Data' activity must eventually occur after the 'Gather Intelligence' event at some point in the same process instance. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between. Multiple 'Gather Intelligence' activities can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Analyze Data' activity can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Gather Intelligence' activities. This defines an ordered flow where data examination and interpretation—utilizing specific 'Analysis Techniques' (Link Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Pattern Recognition) and producing a 'Confidence Score' (a float between 0.0 and 1.0)—follow the intelligence gathering phase, ensuring a cohesive temporal structure in the model.

#### **Succession Constraints**
An 'Analyze Data' activity must be followed by an 'Identify Threats' activity at some point later in the same process instance. Conversely, an 'Identify Threats' event can occur only if an 'Analyze Data' event has occurred before it within the same process instance. This bidirectional and ordered dependency establishes a causal link, ensuring that 'Analyze Data' imposes obligations for future 'Identify Threats,' and 'Identify Threats' cannot occur without prior 'Analyze Data.' Multiple 'Analyze Data' and 'Identify Threats' activities are allowed, with each 'Analyze Data' eventually leading to an 'Identify Threats' and each 'Identify Threats' being justified by a preceding 'Analyze Data,' creating a cohesive progression within the model.

### **Mutual Dependency Constraints**

#### **Co-Existence Constraints**
If the 'Gather Intelligence' activity occurs, then the 'Analyze Data' activity must also occur in the same process instance, and vice-versa. This mutual dependency ensures that both activities occur together within the process flow, meaning that intelligence cannot be gathered without subsequent analysis, and analysis cannot be performed without prior intelligence gathering.

### **Immediate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Chain Precedence Constraints**
The 'Develop Strategies' activity can occur only if it is immediately preceded by an 'Implement Countermeasures' activity, with no other activities occurring in between. This establishes an immediate dependency where the execution of actions and measures designed to mitigate identified threats—requiring the specification of the 'Cost of Countermeasures' (a float between 100.0 and 1000000.0) and the 'Days to Respond' (an integer between 1 and 365)—directly precedes the formulation of plans and approaches to address those threats, making the sequence explicit and precise.

#### **Chain Response Constraints**
Whenever an 'Implement Countermeasures' activity occurs, it must be immediately followed by a 'Monitor Activity' activity in the next event position, with no other events in between. This captures an immediate cause-and-effect relationship where the execution of countermeasures directly triggers the continuous observation and tracking of activities to detect any changes or emerging threats. The 'Monitor Activity' activity is also allowed to happen in isolation.

### **Alternate Ordering Constraints**

#### **Alternate Response Constraints**
If a 'Monitor Activity' activity occurs, then a 'Report Findings' activity must occur after that 'Monitor Activity' event and before any subsequent 'Monitor Activity' occurs. No other occurrence of 'Monitor Activity' is allowed between a 'Monitor Activity' and 'Report Findings.' This promotes balance by ensuring each occurrence of the monitoring activation is followed by a unique reporting target, with no intervening activations, supporting clear one-to-one activity pairing. The 'Report Findings' activity involves compiling and presenting results and requires the specification of the 'Report Status' (Draft, Review, Final, Disseminated) and the 'Geographic Region' (North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, South America).

#### **Alternate Precedence Constraints**
The 'Review Intelligence' activity can occur only if it was immediately preceded by a 'Disseminate Information' activity, and no other 'Disseminate Information' event is allowed between the 'Disseminate Information' and the 'Review Intelligence.' This ensures order consistency, requiring each occurrence of the target activity (re-evaluating previously gathered and analyzed intelligence to ensure accuracy and relevance) to be preceded by the specified activity (sharing intelligence findings) without overlap, promoting a balanced process flow.
