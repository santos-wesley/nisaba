**Multi-Perspective Declare Model: Intelligence Cycle for Threat Management**

This model describes a structured **intelligence and counter-threat process** consisting of 10 activities. It captures both the control-flow (temporal ordering) of the process and multiple data perspectives attached to the activities.

### Process Activities
- **Gather Intelligence**
- **Analyze Data**
- **Assess Credibility**
- **Identify Threats**
- **Develop Strategies**
- **Implement Countermeasures**
- **Monitor Activity**
- **Report Findings**
- **Review Intelligence**
- **Disseminate Information**

### Data Perspectives (Attributes)

**Bound to specific activities:**

- **Gather Intelligence**: 
  - *Source Type* (Human, Signal, Open Source, Cyber)
  - *Number of Sources* (integer 1–50)

- **Analyze Data**: 
  - *Analysis Technique* (Link Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Pattern Recognition)
  - *Confidence Score* (float 0.0–1.0)

- **Identify Threats**: 
  - *Threat Level* (integer 1–10)
  - *Risk Factor* (float 0.0–100.0)

- **Implement Countermeasures**: 
  - *Cost of Countermeasures* (float 100.0–1,000,000.0)
  - *Days to Respond* (integer 1–365)

- **Report Findings**: 
  - *Report Status* (Draft, Review, Final, Disseminated)
  - *Geographic Region* (North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, South America)

### Declarative Constraints

**Control-flow constraints:**

- The process **must start** with `Gather Intelligence`.
- `Gather Intelligence` must be **directly followed** by `Analyze Data` (Response).
- `Analyze Data` must be **directly followed** by `Identify Threats` (Succession).
- `Develop Strategies` can only occur **after** `Identify Threats` (Precedence).
- `Develop Strategies` must be **immediately followed** by `Implement Countermeasures` (Chain Precedence).
- `Implement Countermeasures` must be **immediately followed** by `Monitor Activity` (Chain Response).
- After `Monitor Activity`, `Report Findings` can occur, but only **once per instance** of monitoring (Alternate Response).
- `Review Intelligence` must occur **before** `Disseminate Information`, and `Disseminate Information` can only happen once after each review (Alternate Precedence).
- The process **must end** with `Disseminate Information`.

**Data-aware / Multi-perspective constraints:**

- `Gather Intelligence` and `Analyze Data` **must both occur** in every process instance (Co-Existence).

---

**Summary in natural language:**

The model represents an intelligence-driven threat response lifecycle. It begins with intelligence gathering from various sources, which must immediately be followed by data analysis. The process then follows a strict sequence: analysis leads to threat identification, which informs strategy development. Once strategies are developed, countermeasures must be implemented without delay, immediately triggering a monitoring phase. Monitoring is followed (possibly multiple times) by reporting findings. Before information can be officially disseminated, a review step is required. The entire process ends with the dissemination of intelligence products. Throughout the process, rich data attributes are captured — such as source reliability, confidence scores, threat levels, costs, and geographic scope — enabling a multi-perspective analysis of the intelligence operation.
