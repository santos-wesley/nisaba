#MP-Declare Model
## Activities
- **Report Disease Outbreak**: The 'Report Disease Outbreak' activity represents the initial reporting of a disease outbreak, capturing the source of the report and the number of cases identified.
- **Verify Outbreak Report**: The 'Verify Outbreak Report' activity involves verifying the validity of a reported disease outbreak, determining whether the report is confirmed, a false alarm, or inconclusive.
- **Initiate Investigation**: The 'Initiate Investigation' activity represents the formal start of an investigation into a confirmed disease outbreak.
- **Collect Samples**: The 'Collect Samples' activity involves gathering biological or environmental samples for laboratory analysis as part of the disease outbreak investigation.
- **Analyze Samples**: The 'Analyze Samples' activity represents the laboratory analysis of collected samples to identify the disease and its characteristics.
- **Confirm Disease**: The 'Confirm Disease' activity involves officially confirming the type of disease causing the outbreak, including its type and associated mortality rate.
- **Implement Control Measures**: The 'Implement Control Measures' activity represents the deployment of control strategies such as vaccination, quarantine, hygiene campaigns, or social distancing to contain the disease outbreak.
- **Inform Public**: The 'Inform Public' activity involves communicating information about the disease outbreak and control measures to the public, monitoring their response.
- **Monitor Disease Spread**: The 'Monitor Disease Spread' activity represents the ongoing surveillance and tracking of the disease's spread within the population.
- **Declare End of Outbreak**: The 'Declare End of Outbreak' activity involves officially declaring that the disease outbreak has been contained and is no longer a threat.

## Attributes
- **Report Source: Hospital, Clinic, Public, Other**: The attribute 'Report Source: Hospital, Clinic, Public, Other' specifies the possible sources from which a disease outbreak is reported, including hospitals, clinics, the general public, or other sources.
- **Number of Cases: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Number of Cases: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the number of confirmed cases associated with a reported disease outbreak, ranging from 1 to 1000.
- **Disease Type: Influenza, COVID-19, Measles, Cholera**: The attribute 'Disease Type: Influenza, COVID-19, Measles, Cholera' specifies the possible types of diseases that can be confirmed during an outbreak investigation.
- **Mortality Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Mortality Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the mortality rate associated with a confirmed disease, expressed as a floating-point percentage between 0.0 and 100.0.
- **Control Measures: Vaccination, Quarantine, Hygiene Campaign, Social Distancing**: The attribute 'Control Measures: Vaccination, Quarantine, Hygiene Campaign, Social Distancing' specifies the possible control measures that can be implemented to contain a disease outbreak.
- **Days to Control: integer between 1 and 365**: The attribute 'Days to Control: integer between 1 and 365' represents the estimated number of days required to control the disease outbreak, ranging from 1 to 365 days.
- **Investigation Result: Confirmed, False Alarm, Inconclusive**: The attribute 'Investigation Result: Confirmed, False Alarm, Inconclusive' specifies the possible outcomes of verifying an outbreak report, indicating whether the outbreak is confirmed, a false alarm, or inconclusive.
- **Public Response: Cooperative, Anxious, Resistant**: The attribute 'Public Response: Cooperative, Anxious, Resistant' specifies the possible responses from the public when informed about a disease outbreak and its control measures.

## Binds
- **Report Disease Outbreak: Report Source, Number of Cases**: The bind 'Report Disease Outbreak: Report Source, Number of Cases' specifies that the activity 'Report Disease Outbreak' requires the attributes 'Report Source' and 'Number of Cases'.
- **Verify Outbreak Report: Investigation Result**: The bind 'Verify Outbreak Report: Investigation Result' specifies that the activity 'Verify Outbreak Report' requires the attribute 'Investigation Result'.
- **Confirm Disease: Disease Type, Mortality Rate**: The bind 'Confirm Disease: Disease Type, Mortality Rate' specifies that the activity 'Confirm Disease' requires the attributes 'Disease Type' and 'Mortality Rate'.
- **Implement Control Measures: Control Measures, Days to Control**: The bind 'Implement Control Measures: Control Measures, Days to Control' specifies that the activity 'Implement Control Measures' requires the attributes 'Control Measures' and 'Days to Control'.
- **Inform Public: Public Response**: The bind 'Inform Public: Public Response' specifies that the activity 'Inform Public' requires the attribute 'Public Response'.

## Interleaved Description
The MP-Declare process model for the disease outbreak management process is described as follows:

### **Trace-Related Constraints**
#### **Init Constraints**
The process begins with the activity 'Report Disease Outbreak,' which involves the initial reporting of a disease outbreak, capturing the source of the report and the number of cases identified. This activity requires the attributes 'Report Source,' which can be a Hospital, Clinic, Public, or Other source, and 'Number of Cases,' which represents the number of confirmed cases ranging from 1 to 1000.

#### **End Constraints**
The process concludes with the activity 'Declare End of Outbreak,' which involves officially declaring that the disease outbreak has been contained and is no longer a threat. This activity must be the last to occur in the process, ensuring a coherent and definitive closure to the outbreak management lifecycle.

### **Flexible Ordering Constraints**

#### **Precedence Constraints**
The activity 'Analyze Samples' is permitted to occur only if a 'Confirm Disease' activity has occurred before it within the same process instance. This establishes an initial context where the laboratory analysis of collected samples to identify the disease and its characteristics is logically preceded by the official confirmation of the disease type and its associated mortality rate. Multiple 'Confirm Disease' events can occur, and any number of events, including other 'Analyze Samples' activities, can happen between a 'Confirm Disease' and a subsequent 'Analyze Samples,' ensuring that sample analysis is always grounded in a prior disease confirmation.

#### **Response Constraints**
1. If a 'Report Disease Outbreak' activity occurs, then a 'Verify Outbreak Report' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This defines an ordered flow where the initial reporting of the outbreak, capturing the report source and number of cases, is followed by the verification of the outbreak report's validity, determining whether the report is confirmed, a false alarm, or inconclusive. There is no requirement on how soon or what events may occur in between, and a single 'Verify Outbreak Report' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding 'Report Disease Outbreak' events.

2. If a 'Verify Outbreak Report' activity occurs and the activation condition 'A.Investigation Result is Confirmed' is satisfied, then an 'Initiate Investigation' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This ensures that when the verification confirms the outbreak, the formal start of an investigation into the confirmed disease outbreak follows as part of the ordered flow. Multiple 'Verify Outbreak Report' events can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Initiate Investigation' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding confirmed verification events.

3. If a 'Collect Samples' activity occurs, then an 'Analyze Samples' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance, and the 'Analyze Samples' activity must occur within at most 48 hours after the 'Collect Samples' event. This ensures a timely ordered flow where the gathering of biological or environmental samples for laboratory analysis is followed by the actual laboratory analysis to identify the disease and its characteristics, maintaining the process's momentum with a strict time constraint.

4. If an 'Implement Control Measures' activity occurs, then an 'Inform Public' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance. This defines an ordered flow where the deployment of control strategies—such as vaccination, quarantine, hygiene campaigns, or social distancing—is followed by communicating information about the disease outbreak and control measures to the public, while monitoring their response. Multiple 'Implement Control Measures' events can be pending at the same time, and a single 'Inform Public' can fulfill the obligation for all preceding events.

5. If a 'Report Disease Outbreak' activity occurs and the activation condition 'A.Number of Cases > 500' is satisfied, then an 'Implement Control Measures' activity must eventually occur after it at some point in the same process instance, and the 'Implement Control Measures' activity must occur within at most 24 hours after the 'Report Disease Outbreak' event. This ensures that for large-scale outbreaks exceeding 500 cases, control measures are deployed urgently within a strict 24-hour window, establishing a rapid response ordered flow that prioritizes containment in critical scenarios.

#### **Succession Constraints**
1. A 'Confirm Disease' activity must be followed by an 'Implement Control Measures' activity at some point later in the same process instance, and conversely, an 'Implement Control Measures' event can occur only if a 'Confirm Disease' event has occurred before it. This establishes a causal link where the official confirmation of the disease type and its associated mortality rate logically leads to the deployment of control strategies, and control measures cannot be implemented without prior disease confirmation. Multiple 'Confirm Disease' and 'Implement Control Measures' events are allowed, with each confirmation eventually leading to corresponding control measures and each control measure being justified by a preceding confirmation.

2. An 'Inform Public' activity must be followed by a 'Monitor Disease Spread' activity at some point later in the same process instance, and conversely, a 'Monitor Disease Spread' event can occur only if an 'Inform Public' event has occurred before it. This establishes a causal link where communicating information about the disease outbreak and control measures to the public—while monitoring their response, which can be Cooperative, Anxious, or Resistant—logically leads to the ongoing surveillance and tracking of the disease's spread within the population. Multiple 'Inform Public' and 'Monitor Disease Spread' events are allowed, with each public notification eventually leading to disease spread monitoring and each monitoring activity being justified by a preceding public notification.
