## Activities
- **Report Disease Outbreak**: The 'Report Disease Outbreak' activity involves reporting a potential disease outbreak to the relevant authorities.
- **Verify Outbreak Report**: The 'Verify Outbreak Report' activity involves checking the validity and accuracy of the reported disease outbreak.
- **Initiate Investigation**: The 'Initiate Investigation' activity involves starting an official investigation into the reported outbreak.
- **Collect Samples**: The 'Collect Samples' activity involves gathering biological or environmental samples for analysis.
- **Analyze Samples**: The 'Analyze Samples' activity involves testing and examining collected samples in a laboratory setting.
- **Confirm Disease**: The 'Confirm Disease' activity involves officially identifying and confirming the disease responsible for the outbreak.
- **Implement Control Measures**: The 'Implement Control Measures' activity involves putting in place actions to control and contain the disease outbreak.
- **Inform Public**: The 'Inform Public' activity involves communicating relevant information about the outbreak to the general public.
- **Monitor Disease Spread**: The 'Monitor Disease Spread' activity involves tracking and observing the progression and spread of the disease.
- **Declare End of Outbreak**: The 'Declare End of Outbreak' activity involves officially announcing that the outbreak has ended.

## Attributes
- **Report Source: Hospital, Clinic, Public, Other**: The attribute 'Report Source: Hospital, Clinic, Public, Other' specifies the possible sources from which a disease outbreak can be reported.
- **Number of Cases: integer between 1 and 1000**: The attribute 'Number of Cases: integer between 1 and 1000' represents the number of reported cases of the disease, ranging from 1 to 1000.
- **Disease Type: Influenza, COVID-19, Measles, Cholera**: The attribute 'Disease Type: Influenza, COVID-19, Measles, Cholera' specifies the possible types of diseases that can be confirmed during the outbreak.
- **Mortality Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0**: The attribute 'Mortality Rate: float between 0.0 and 100.0' represents the mortality rate percentage of the confirmed disease, ranging from 0.0 to 100.0.
- **Control Measures: Vaccination, Quarantine, Hygiene Campaign, Social Distancing**: The attribute 'Control Measures: Vaccination, Quarantine, Hygiene Campaign, Social Distancing' specifies the possible measures that can be implemented to control the disease outbreak.
- **Days to Control: integer between 1 and 365**: The attribute 'Days to Control: integer between 1 and 365' represents the number of days required to control the outbreak, ranging from 1 to 365.
- **Investigation Result: Confirmed, False Alarm, Inconclusive**: The attribute 'Investigation Result: Confirmed, False Alarm, Inconclusive' specifies the possible outcomes of the outbreak investigation.
- **Public Response: Cooperative, Anxious, Resistant**: The attribute 'Public Response: Cooperative, Anxious, Resistant' specifies the possible reactions from the public when informed about the outbreak.
